# Flight computer requirements (first principles)

What the board must do, derived from the project goals (`../../CLAUDE.md`), the owner's decisions,
the flight envelope of both flights, and the board's own design points. Every design review checks
the board against this file. Verification column: **CAD** = KiCad files and checks, **Calc** =
datasheet calculation, **Bench** = measured on a built board, **Flight**.

## Sources

- **Project goals:**
  - hold roll rate near zero, and fly a commanded roll program (e.g. a 90° turn and hold);
  - flight 1 on an AeroTech K850DM, about Mach 1.45, roll only (owner confirmation; parent CLAUDE.md);
  - flight 2 on an L1000W, about Mach 1.9, possibly adding pitch/yaw;
  - single deploy at apogee: one primary pyro channel, with a fully isolated Featherweight Blue Jay backup.
- **Owner decisions:**
  - one board flies both flights with no respin, so flight 2's worst case applies now;
  - flight condition (Mach, dynamic pressure) is estimated from accelerometer + barometer;
  - roll angle is integrated gyro, relative to the orientation at liftoff;
  - on a flight-computer reset, hang or brownout the tab must return to neutral quickly;
  - roll control is active during the burn as well as the coast (exact start and stop points open);
  - flight 2's pitch/yaw surfaces are undecided, so the board's 4-servo limit becomes their constraint;
  - no in-flight tab-deflection sensing (servos are characterised on the bench, under load);
  - telemetry radio and GPS: R7–R9 carried them on the board (decision 2026-10-15; u-blox MAX-F10S and EBYTE E22-900MM22S, selection in `../../reports/Compact rocket GPS and radio.md`). **R10** (owner decisions O1 / O2, 2026-10-26; CAD candidate, not accepted) removes them and uses an external GPS (HGLRC M100 Mini) on DB125 4-position J410 and an external radio (MicoAir LR900-F) on DB125 4-position J420 (F10). The radio uplink must never arm or fire recovery;
  - **R10 single screw switch (2026-10-28):** one external screw switch on J400 powers and arms the board; J124 and its fused ARM link are removed. The pyro feed remains behind Q120 / Q121. Pad checks take place on the rail; the power switch is turned on last and off first (R10_DECISIONS "Single screw switch for power and arming");
  - Tripoli/NAR safety code.
- **Flight envelope:** the parent project and owner confirmation supersede the old I500T baseline.

  These are project design points and inherited estimates, not flight measurements. No board impact was found
  (Inference): a longer flight still logs about 3 MB of the 16 MB QSPI, and the 10 km barometer requirement has its own
  root. Re-check F5's window, the pad-energy Q_flight and the Temperature row's 525 s against the new envelope.
  K850DM certification lists 2041 g motor mass and 1224 g propellant; the old vehicle
  2.36/2.05 kg mass pair is not applicable. Sources and disposition: M3_R6.md.
  Keep ≥ ±64 g accelerometer capability (U4 supports higher ranges), ±4000 dps gyro,
  a high-rate control window that ends at apogee or when the tab loses authority (≤ 35 s on the derived envelope;
  the earlier ≤ 30 s was a design choice, R23-REQUIREMENTS-02), ≥10 km barometer capability and the 100 g
  retention design load. Final vehicle simulation/weighing must establish that the
  selected sensor ranges, logging duration, servo/harness loads and battery reserve
  cover both flights before launch.

  The earlier M0.9 stuck-tab roll estimate was about 1100 °/s; the earlier uncontrolled
  roll estimate was 1000–1850 °/s. They are historical estimates, not K850DM predictions.
  Scaling the latter linearly to M1.9 approaches ±4000 dps (Inference); verify range
  and control margin against the updated flight model.

## Accepted risks (recorded decisions)

- **R10 single-switch arming (2026-10-28).** No physical pyro-bus break while the board is on. The external screw switch on J400 is the pad arming device; PC1 / ACT_EN_MCU and PE4 separately control the load switch and fire FET, both off through reset / unpowered. USB or a self-powered port can keep logic up with the switch open; connect the e-match only with no back-feed source attached. Firmware never fires before accelerometer + barometer launch detection, with ground sense refusals and in-flight logging retained. Final-copper topology passes the CAD contracts; physical switch access / contact reliability, revised sense thresholds and dummy-load gate waveforms remain unverified.

- **R10 no battery-lead fuse (OQ-6, revised 2026-10-28, `f202ec3`).** A hard short from the pack to U9 / Q120 (the lead, J1, BAT_IN copper, D8, C80 / C96, C88 and C89 / C95) is not current-limited; Q120's drain is on BAT_IN, so the RAW_ACT path bypasses U9 and only R134 (2 Ω) limits it (D-R21-03). The owner accepted this residual risk with strain relief, no chafe points and crimped or soldered leads. U9's downstream limit and R134's shorted-igniter limit do not protect that stretch. The later 2026-10-28 single-switch decision also removes J124 and its ARM-link fuse (F8).

- **No on-board servo-fault containment (R10).** Servo power no longer passes through the board (F4), so a hard short on a servo lead or inside a servo loads the harness and pack, can pull the primary computer down, and is not cleared by a board battery-lead fuse (none is fitted, OQ-6). Recovery then relies on the isolated backup altimeter (R2_VERIFICATION §2). Per-servo inline fast fuses in the harness are the containment. (R7–R9 differed: servo power ran through the board until an external 15 A fuse opened.)
- **The tab's state while the computer reboots depends on the servo** (hold or go limp with no pulses): bench item under F6. A single supply interruption longer than U9's chatter immunity (≥ 10.3 ms worst at 6.0 V, about 8.1 ms at the four-stall 5.71 V, D-R24-19, D-R26-03), or a train of shorter ones that adds up (F6), turns U9 off; the board restarts 30–130 ms after power returns (C87 220 nF, D-R19-10), plus boot.
- **Flight 2's servo harness** runs in an external raceway at the current approximately Mach 1.9 design point (estimate; final vehicle/harness loads unqualified), so a lead short is more likely: per-servo inline fuses are recommended there.
- **A reversed 3S pack** (two faults at once) can ring U9's input to about −19 V, past its −15 V limit.
- **Harness miswiring remains hazardous.** R10 uses unkeyed DB125 screw terminals for every wired connection. J1/J400 and the four four-position ports (J9, J410, J420, J440) share green bodies; J121 remains black. Verify every supply, ground and signal against its individual pin legend and continuity-check the complete harness with all recovery loads disconnected. R7–R9's keyed-GH camera description is historical.
- **Load-switch gate leakage (R10).** A bridge or contamination on the ACT_GATE net can turn Q120/Q121 on with the power switch open, which puts the bus on J121.2. Q124 still blocks firing, so a second fault is needed to fire. Mandatory conformal coat and the "RAW_ACT dead with the switch open" bench step and per-flight J121.2 check (ORDERING §8.3) screen it. Round 18 measured two ACT_GATE via barrels 0.2505 mm from inner-layer copper and a single-via bias path at Q121 (R18-SAF-01, -02; see R10_VALIDATION, Round 18).
- **No hardware revision ID**: firmware cannot tell this board from another revision by itself; the firmware build is bound to the board by hash (AGENTS.md §3).
