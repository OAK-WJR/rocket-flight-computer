# M3-ASSY-R5: two-sided re-layout at 46 × 100 mm

**Status: CAD only.** Nothing here has been built or measured. Requirements are in
[REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md); R5 keeps R4's schematic and BOM ([M3_R4.md](M3_R4.md)) and changes
only the board: parts on both sides, 100 mm instead of 193 mm long.

**How R5 is built:** R5 is generated from the R4 board (git commit fa68b38) by the scripts below; their
docstrings give the reason for every choice. `m3_design/r5_build.sh` restores the R4 board from git, runs them
in order, and runs the board gate, the safety contract and the envelope check. Freerouting is not needed to
rebuild: its session file is kept in git, and so is the finisher's result (`r5_finish.json`, replayed): the
finisher's search is not repeatable (KiCad's DRC reports one and the same open connection now as a pair of items,
now as two pour pieces), so a run that leaves nothing open is recorded and replayed.

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `m3_design/r5_pcb.py` | outline, holes, placement (manual tables for the critical parts, `r5_place.tsv` for the frozen auto-placement), rule areas and zones, then the scripted critical copper (below) |
| `m3_design/r5_maze.py` | the grid router behind every scripted connection: 0.1 mm grid, 0/45/90° segments with a turn penalty, the board's clearances and the pyro spacing rules, checked vias |
| `m3_design/r5_routes.py` | the critical routes (AGENTS.md): USB, HSE, QSPI, SPI1 to the IMU, the input and actuator power paths, the logic and camera supplies with their switching loops, the servo feed, the pyro block's exits to the MCU, U9's control network, decoupling, return vias |
| `m3_design/r5_route.py` | Freerouting v2.4.1 export (DSN: power pours and In2 islands as keep-outs, gated In2 keep-outs under the critical B.Cu traces, the default clearance 0.17 mm; `--seed` shuffles the order, `--warm` a second session from a finished board) and session import for the non-critical nets |
| `m3_design/r5_finish.py` | the connections Freerouting leaves, drawn by the grid router (to any point of the island to reach, crossing the router's copper only where that leaves fewer open), stranded GND pads tied to In1, then forced moves while they gain anything: a GND island's pad tied across movable copper, a cleared box with a via of its own or a track to the nearest grounded copper, one remaining connection at a time; each kept only if fewer connections stay open (section 1, L5) |
| `m3_design/r5_post.py` | track clean-up (no merge across the pyro block's edge; spikes trimmed, acute corners cut), GND stitching, GND vias beside critical layer changes, GND island vias, the USB reference under the D+/D- pair, silkscreen, fab-layer designators, title block |
| `m3_design/r5_autoroute.dsn`, `.ses` | the Freerouting input and session (rebuilds without Java) |
| `m3_design/r5_finish.json` | the finisher's recorded result (the router copper it removed, the copper it added), replayed by `r5_build.sh` |
| `m3_design/check_actuators.py` | the safety contract; check 15b now pins R5's pyro block rectangle |
| `tools/jlc_orient.py`, `tools/export_fab.py` | bottom-side placement rows (section 1, L9) |
| `tools/jlc_cost.py` | the two-sided assembly fees (REQUIREMENTS M3) |
| `tools/dc_solve.py` | DC solve of a net's copper (layers and via barrels) between two pad sets (new) |
| `tools/fit_check.py` | the envelope check, now on the whole board against the coupler bore |

Section 1 is the R5 layout. Section 1b onwards are what the independent review passes found and what changed
because of them.

## 2. Deviations accepted (with reason)

Everything M3_R4 §2 records about the pyro block still holds: the block moved as one rigid group, with its own
copper, so the solder-bridge residuals, the bare gate copper near bus and 3V3 copper (mandatory coating), the
cross-channel R134/R135 spacing, the DB128L tongues and the sense nets' MCU-side defaults are unchanged. The R4
items about the old board (the eight M3 holes and their washers, the R4 zip-tie holes, J2 at y ≥ 176, C22 20 mm
from VDDA, C24 at J2, the R1–R3 router copper) no longer apply. New in R5:

- **The servo chain sits under the pyro block** (R150–R157 on the bottom, 10–25 mm from J122; the clamp D121
  beside C121). R4 placed the series resistors beside J122's signal pins. In R5 that column is where the SPI1 run
  to the bottom-side IMU passes, and every trial with the chain beside J122 or in the band north of it left
  9–18 connections open. Electrically the series resistor protects the MCU pin wherever it sits on the line; the
  cost is longer servo lines (Inference: no effect at 333 Hz PWM).
- **Not all power copper is scripted.** The supply paths, the switching loops, decoupling, the high-current copper,
  the 3V3 feeds to pads outside the In2 3V3 fill and most GND pad vias are scripted (AGENTS.md); the remaining
  branches of 3V3 and 3V3A to pull-ups and small loads, and GND pad ties, are drawn by the autorouter and the
  finisher at 0.2 mm (0.25 mm for 3V3/3V3A by the finisher). They carry milliamps.
- **{N_RET} critical layer changes have no GND via within 1.5 mm** ({RET_LIST}): no legal spot (the USB vias sit
  between J3's pads, the In2 RAW_ACT strip and D4; the IMU-side vias between the IMU's body keep-out, the In2 pyro
  lanes and the servo clamp). At these lengths and edge rates (USB full speed; SPI1 at ≤ 10 MHz) the detour to the
  nearest GND via, 2–4 mm, adds well under 1 nH (Inference). Where a B.Cu segment changes to F.Cu the reference
  also changes from In2 (3V3) to In1 (GND), which a GND via alone would not bridge.
- **{N_HID} designators are hidden** (no spot within reach that is nearer their own part than any other, clear of
  pads and vias, on either side; the fab layers and the assembly drawing carry them): {HID_LIST}.
- **Through-hole pins at full connection** (no thermal relief): {FULL_LIST}. DRC finds their spokes starved (the
  pours reach them from one side only); ORDERING asks JLC to check hole fill.
- **The In2 3V3 fill is split into {N_3V3} pieces** by the autorouter's In2 tracks (each piece has its own 3V3
  vias). No critical B.Cu trace crosses a split (In2 is kept clear under them during routing).
- **Small networks some millimetres from their pins** ({DIST}): U11's soft-start, status pull-up and OVLO divider
  (the bottom round U11 is full: J1's pin tails, the 3V3 buck, J9), U7's DVDT capacitor and ILIM resistor (J121's
  pin tails, the camera buck). All are DC or slow nodes (Inference: no measurable effect on the limits). The IMU's
  VDDIO capacitor C30 sits beside C31 on the IMU's east side, {C30_DIST} from VDDIO through the In2 3V3A plane (west
  of the IMU it took the interrupt line's only way out); both pins are 3V3A.
- **Routing style:** {STYLE}.

## 3. Consequences for firmware (recorded, not written or reviewed here)

- **Board binding:** the R5 copper invalidates the board binding in `m3_firmware/build.py`; a new binding and a
  pin-table review are needed before any build (AGENTS.md §3). The pin assignment itself is R4's (schematic
  unchanged).
- **IMU (U4, LSM6DSV320X) on the bottom side**, KiCad rotation 0. Flipping a part to the bottom turns it 180°
  about the board's long axis, so: +X_imu = −x_board, +Y_imu = −y_board (aft, as R4), +Z_imu out of the bottom
  face. **Roll = −gyro_Y, as in R4**, and thrust still reads −a_Y; the X and Z signs (pitch and yaw) flip
  relative to R4. Confirm every sign on the bench. The IMU is about 13 mm off the roll axis (R4: 15.5 mm):
  about 1.4 g of centripetal acceleration at 1850 °/s.
- Everything else in M3_R4 §3 stands.

## 4. Operating notes (changes from M3_R4 §4)

- **Sled:** the board rests on four standoffs at the corner M3 holes, with the top face on the bore axis
  (REQUIREMENTS P2). Bottom parts hang up to 3.0 mm below the bottom face (L1, the 3V3 buck's inductor), so the
  sled plate sits below that, and it is non-conductive under the board (bare pin tails, test pads, power pours). It leaves a channel
  under the board along the J122 tie's path, from hole (21.5, 50.6) to hole (27.0, 64.6).
- **J122 tie:** fitting the tie over all four seated plugs is an assembly requirement (MARGIN_PRESCRIPTION
  P1-13), not optional.
- **MS5611:** it now faces the sled, on the far side of the board from both LEDs; keep the foam cover.
- Everything else in M3_R4 §4 stands.
