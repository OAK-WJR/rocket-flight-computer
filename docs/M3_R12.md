# M3-ASSY-R12: R11's circuit, pruned and re-sourced, on a one-sided four-layer board, 46 × 90 mm

## Current state

This section is the one place that states where R12 stands. [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md),
[R12_VALIDATION.md](R12_VALIDATION.md) and [R12_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md](R12_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md) summarize it and link
here. When something changes, change it here first.

**Summary.** M3-ASSY-R12 is a CAD candidate on branch `m3-assy-r12` (the AVA re-layout, D-R12-93 … -99, and round 2's fixes,
D-R12-100 … -106, round 3's fixes D-R12-107 … -112, round 4's fixes D-R12-113 … -120, the AVA polish pass D-R12-121 …
-124, round 5's fixes D-R12-125 … -133 and round 6's D-R12-134 … -143). It keeps R11's circuit: the
net names, the R10 safety networks and the TFBGA-100 ball map. Every part no requirement needs is deleted, cheaper
equivalents are re-sourced (D-R12-04 … -19, -49 … -52), and the board is laid out new for JLC **Economic** PCBA. That
means every part on the top face, four layers and 46 × 90 mm (D-R12-99; it was 46 × 100 mm until the re-layout). It is **not accepted and nothing is ordered**. R9 stays
the accepted board and R8 its fallback; R10 and R11 stay unaccepted candidates. No firmware exists, no physical test
has been done. Stage F round 1 found verified real errors; its fix passes are recorded in STATUS.md. The AVA re-layout
(placement, U1 re-pins, zone routing, 90 mm outline) followed. Round 2 found 16 real errors (1 Major / 15 Minor),
all fixed by D-R12-100 … -106 without changing that board. Round 3 found 14 real errors (2 Major / 12 Minor),
all fixed by D-R12-107 … -112; only C25 / C26 changed on the board (18 → 15 pF). Round 4 found 8 real errors (0 Major /
8 Minor), all fixed by D-R12-113 … -120; only R166's footprint description changed on the board. The AVA polish pass
(D-R12-121 … -124) changed signal copper only. Round 5 found 22 real errors (1 Critical / 21 Minor), all fixed by
D-R12-125 … -133: the Critical one was the fire-net copper breaking the carried R10 limits, now fixed in copper and gated
by check_r12 S-10 (default-deny); U6's corner lands, GPS_TX's pad exit and the J121 pours also changed. Round 6 found 17
real errors (1 Critical / 1 Major / 15 Minor), all fixed by D-R12-134 … -143, and an independent verifier of the fix
found V-1 … V-5: the Critical one was an unlimited 3V3 → D120 → Q124 path when R131 is bridged during a commanded fire
(now R136 5.1 kΩ in series and R131 5.1 kΩ); the verifier added the pack-energy nets to S-10 (R132 / R175 / R120 moved)
and U9's FLT now reaches PC13 through R95 with D95 clamping it. Decision IDs D-R12-01 … D-R12-143 are in [`m3_design/r12/design/R12_DECISIONS.md`](../m3_design/r12/design/R12_DECISIONS.md). The plan and reading guide is
[`r12_plan.md`](../m3_design/r12/design/r12_plan.md), and the stage records are `stageB.md`, `stageC.md`, `stageD.md` and
`stageE_relayout.md` (the re-layout) in the same folder. The board hash and routing figures below are round 6's board
`f26dfef1…` (`3da8840`, Measured).

### What R12 changes from R11

- **Assembly and board (D-R12-01, -03, -24).** The six-layer two-sided 46 × 50 mm board becomes a four-layer one-sided
  46 × 100 mm board (46 × 90 mm since D-R12-99) on Economic PCBA. Vias are 0.3 / 0.45 mm. U401 and its two VIPPO vias are deleted.
- **Pruned and re-sourced (D-R12-04 … -19, -49 … -52).**
  - USB limiter and mux → SS34 diode-OR D5 / D6.
  - Port LDO → U400 TPS563208 making PORT_4V8 directly.
  - Camera limiter and UART isolation → U403 TPS563208 with its EN as the camera switch.
  - U9 TPS259470L → A (auto-retry).
  - Pyro FETs → AO3401A ×2 and AO3400A.
  - D120 → BAT54C.
  - Crystal → X322525MOB4SI with C25 / C26 15 pF C0G ±5 % (D-R12-107, formerly 18 pF).
  - 22 µF capacitors consolidated on one 1206 code.
  - Port and camera UART ESD deleted (D402 kept).
  - U4 → LSM6DSVE.
  - New high-g U6 FXLS90322 on SPI1 (chip select HG_CS).
  - U5 → MS5607.
  - Buzzer → QMB-09B-03 THT, JLC THT assembly (D-R12-85).
- **Connectors (D-R12-29 … -31).** R11's GH ports become SH 1.0 mm clones in the MicoAir H743's port orders. J121
  becomes R10's DB125 screw terminal, and J1 becomes 5.0 mm-pitch pads.
- **U1 re-pins (D-R12-61A … -64A).** Barometer, microSD, QSPI_NCS, HG_CS and VBUS_SENSE move to balls facing their pads
  ([Firmware](#firmware)).
- **Stage D (D-R12-78 … -82).**
  - A third damper capacitor, C79.
  - C88 on the U9 feed.
  - Second vias on the fire return and U9 OUT.
  - The post stage and legend table.
  - J122's harness note made optional.
  - Polarity marks on the buzzer's Fab layer.
- **Port-fix pass (D-R12-83 … -85, stageD.md §7).**
  - PORT_4V8 divider R400 47k + R404 5.1k over R401 10k **0.1 % 25 ppm/K** (one new Extended code): every port inside
    4.5–5.0 V at every tolerance and drift corner while U400 VIN ≥ VO / 0.75 (Calculation / Inference: drift and load
    budget; O-R12-16, -26 closed; the input condition since D-R12-131, see "Power").

  - 14 U1 re-pins (D-R12-93): servos on TIM1 / TIM2, the barometer on I2C1, the camera on UART4, PORT_SENSE on PD10,

  - Copper routed again by zone and merged from fragments (D-R12-96); R107 and LED2 rotated (D-R12-97); C24 moved to J2

## Power

Figures are from `m3_design/r12_stageD.py` (`stageD_budget.json`) and `tools/r12_hotplug.py`, re-run on the 90 mm
board `f26dfef1…` ([stageE_relayout.md §4](../m3_design/r12/design/stageE_relayout.md); method in
[stageD_analysis.md](../m3_design/r12/design/stageD_analysis.md)), and from `r12_budget.py` (`r12_windows.json`) for
the regulator windows.

- **Battery entry (D-R12-09).** The chain is J1 → D8 SMAJ12CA → the R166 / C89 / C95 / C79 damper → C96 → C88 → U9
  TPS259470A.
  - U9 auto-retries after 110 ms on a fault (Datasheet).
  - OVLO rises at 9.80–10.71 V, so a 3S pack is cut off and a full 2S pack is not (Calculation).
  - ILIM is 3.92–4.89 A with the 1 % pair (Datasheet at 750 Ω; 1/R scaling and drift Inference).
  - The EN chain R80 / R165 470k, R81 300k, C87 220 nF and D402 is R10's.
  - U9's FLT (U9.4, net U9_FLT, 0.200 mm from U9.5 BAT_IN in the package) reaches PC13 only through R95 10k, with R89
    10k to 3V3 on U9's side and D95 BAT54C clamping PC13 to 3V3 (D-R12-138): a BAT_IN–FLT bridge passes ≤ 0.53 mA
    through each resistor into 3V3 and holds PC13 ≤ 4.32 V (Calculation; V_F from LCSC's C37704 listing).
- **Rails (Calculation / Inference: drift; `r12_windows.json`).**

  | Rail | Initial | Drift | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 3V3 (U2 LMR50410XDBVR, PFM) | 3.254–3.482 V | 3.224–3.516 V | TI SLUSDW3A §7.8 PFM band −1.5 / +2.5 % as an effective V_FB, 1 % divider (D-R12-139; was 3.270–3.431 / 3.240–3.464 V from §7.5's V_FB alone); nominal 3.35 V |
  | PORT_4V8 (U400; R401 0.1 %, D-R12-83) | 4.603–4.938 V | 4.571–4.972 V | inside 4.5–5.0 V while U400 VIN ≥ VO / 0.75; nominal 4.769 V |
  | CAM_5V (U403, EN = PC12) | 5.221–5.691 V | 5.162–5.757 V | U403 VIN ≥ VO / 0.75; not a full-2S-range guarantee (see below) |

  CAM_5V needs U403 VIN ≥ VO / 0.75 (TI SLVSD90B §7.3: maximum recommended duty 75 %), or
  6.96–7.59 V for the initial window (Calculation). At four stalls U403 sees about 5.60 V (Calculation / Inference: pack, load and drop model); frequency foldback
  and minimum off-time limit the output. An optimistic camera-pin estimate is about 4.95 / 5.05 V at the
  maximum / typical off-time, using the 1 A load and R10 harness / transient allowances (Calculation / Inference;
  omitted losses can lower it). This does not establish a camera pass or failure; see stageD_analysis.md §6.

  PORT_4V8 has the same condition (D-R12-131, R12F5-TRI-02): U400 (the same TPS563208) holds the window only while its
  VIN ≥ VO / 0.75, 6.14–6.58 V initial and 6.09–6.63 V drift (Calculation). With copper (stage D, ports at 0.561 A; the
  drop to VIN an upper bound) U400 VIN is 5.552 V at four stalls and 5.486 V on a 6.0 V open pack, where the window needs
  a duty of about 0.90–0.92. At 580 kHz the minimum off-time (310 / 220 ns) caps the duty at 0.820 / 0.872, so the
  highest held output is 4.51 / 4.80 V at four stalls (4.46 / 4.74 V on the 6.0 V pack); below VIN 7 V the frequency
  folds back to about 200 kHz (SLVSD90B §6.4.1), capping it at 0.938 / 0.956, which holds 5.16 / 5.26 V (5.10 /
  5.19 V), above the 4.972 V window top. So the window should hold with foldback (Calculation / Inference: U9 RON, pack
  model, converter allocations, foldback frequency, DCR and hot RDS(on)); it is not guaranteed, and the bench check is
  R12_VALIDATION row 15. No design change.

  VLOGIC is the diode-OR of RAW_PROTECTED (D5) and VBUS (D6).
- **Hot-plug (Simulation / Inference; D-R12-78, -79).** R10's model, corners and limits run on R12's extracted feed
  with C79 modelled.
  - **0 / 2304 required cases fail.** The minimum U9 IN is −14.255 V against −15.0 V, and the steepest fall is
    9.254 V/µs against 10.
  - The C88 topology check passes: C88.1 → U9.5 is 1.3 mm, 1.60 mΩ and 0.42 nH (D-R12-84). The BAT_IN / BAT_DAMP
    copper is frozen through the re-layout; the extracted feed and transient results are unchanged on `66323cf0…`,
    `db1f0999…`, `68e6b650…`, `6e874d4e…` and `f26dfef1…` (only the inputs' board hash changed in round 4 and in the
    polish pass; in round 5 C13's pad positions changed the electrical SHA to `6b54be98…`, and in round 6 U9.4's net
    name to `3a599c52…`, with every row identical).
    C25 / C26 change the electrical inventory / hash (D-R12-107), not those results.
    The tool treats RAW_PROTECTED as one lumped node, so the new RAW trunk does not enter it.
  - The cold-limit and no-clamp checks pass.
  - **R166 and the damper stay (D-R12-09).** R166 shorted fails 1164 cases, and the pre-C79 44 µF fails 8.
  - The below-envelope 0 / 0.01 Ω harness rows fail as on R10, outside F9's ≥ 0.03 Ω domain.
- **EN timing and chatter (Calculation / Inference).** R10's model applies with R12's four-stall BAT_IN of 5.7209 V.
  - A single drop-out trips U9 at 8.13 ms.
  - The J400 bench rule's worst-corner limit is 7.56 ms. The rule itself is total open time under 7 ms in every sliding
    100 ms window, with U9 on throughout.
  - The 7 ms line holds down to 5.647 V.
  - Turn-on is nominally 88 / 56 / 45 ms at 6.0 / 7.4 / 8.4 V; the R12 bound is 27–114 ms (Calculation / Inference).
- **Hold-up (Calculation / Inference).** C84 now holds VLOGIC only, behind D5, and sits beside U2 (4.14 mΩ of copper,
  was 47.8 mΩ). From a battery break it lasts 2.23–6.12 ms unshed with the card writing and 6.68–11.43 ms shed (R11:
  0.46–0.54 ms). From the PA6 trip it lasts 0.47–1.72 ms. The required hold-up time is still an open owner question.
- **Copper and IPC-2152 (Calculation on CAD widths; the rise is an upper bound, an Inference).**
  - **U9 IN (D-R12-84):** 0.8 mm from J1 to C88.1 (24.9 °C at R10's 2.9 A design current); only U9.5's 1.3 mm, 0.4 mm
    pin entry is over 30 °C (55.2 °C), and U9.5's own bar is 0.3 mm wide. Series copper 7.55 mΩ. O-R12-25 is closed.
  - **U9 OUT:** U9.6's 0.3 mm bar track (1.6 mm, 76.8 °C, pin-entry class), then 0.8 mm into C82.1; the two via stubs are
    0.45 mm (1.0 mm in all, each carrying about half). Two vias.
