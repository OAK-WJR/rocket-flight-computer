# M3-ASSY-R12: R11's circuit, pruned and re-sourced, on a one-sided four-layer board, 46 × 90 mm

## Current state

This section is the one place that states where R12 stands. [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md),
[R12_VALIDATION.md](R12_VALIDATION.md) and [R12_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md](R12_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md) summarize it and link
here. When something changes, change it here first.

**Summary.** M3-ASSY-R12 is a CAD candidate on branch `m3-assy-r12` (the AVA re-layout, D-R12-93 … -99, and round 2's fixes,
D-R12-100 … -106, and round 3's fixes D-R12-107 … -112). It keeps R11's circuit: the
net names, the R10 safety networks and the TFBGA-100 ball map. Every part no requirement needs is deleted, cheaper
equivalents are re-sourced (D-R12-04 … -19, -49 … -52), and the board is laid out new for JLC **Economic** PCBA. That
means every part on the top face, four layers and 46 × 90 mm (D-R12-99; it was 46 × 100 mm until the re-layout). It is **not accepted and nothing is ordered**. R9 stays
the accepted board and R8 its fallback; R10 and R11 stay unaccepted candidates. No firmware exists, no physical test
has been done. Stage F round 1 found verified real errors; its fix passes are recorded in STATUS.md. The AVA re-layout
(placement, U1 re-pins, zone routing, 90 mm outline) followed. Round 2 found 16 real errors (1 Major / 15 Minor),
all fixed by D-R12-100 … -106 without changing that board. Round 3 found 14 real errors (2 Major / 12 Minor),
all fixed by D-R12-107 … -112; only C25 / C26 changed on the board (18 → 15 pF). Decision IDs D-R12-01 … D-R12-112 are in
[`m3_design/r12/design/R12_DECISIONS.md`](../m3_design/r12/design/R12_DECISIONS.md). The plan and reading guide is
[`r12_plan.md`](../m3_design/r12/design/r12_plan.md), and the stage records are `stageB.md`, `stageC.md`, `stageD.md` and
`stageE_relayout.md` (the re-layout) in the same folder. The board hash and routing metrics below are the final
round-3 board's (`5b1f006`); its geometry is unchanged from the signal-only AVA cleanup (`871f77a`).

### What R12 changes from R11

- **Assembly and board (D-R12-01, -03, -24).** The six-layer two-sided 46 × 50 mm board becomes a four-layer one-sided
  46 × 100 mm board (46 × 90 mm since D-R12-99) on Economic PCBA. Vias are 0.3 / 0.45 mm. U401 and its two VIPPO vias are deleted.
- **Pruned and re-sourced (D-R12-04 … -19, -49 … -52).**
  - USB limiter and mux → SS34 diode-OR D5 / D6.
  - Port LDO → U400 TPS563208 making PORT_4V8 directly.
  - Camera limiter and UART isolation → U403 TPS563208 with its EN as the camera switch.
  - U9 TPS259470L → A (auto-retry).
  - Pyro FETs → AO3401A ×2 and AO3400A.
  - D120 → BAT54C.
  - Crystal → X322525MOB4SI with C25 / C26 15 pF C0G ±5 % (D-R12-107, formerly 18 pF).
  - 22 µF capacitors consolidated on one 1206 code.
  - Port and camera UART ESD deleted (D402 kept).
  - U4 → LSM6DSVE.
  - New high-g U6 FXLS90322 on SPI1 (chip select HG_CS).
  - U5 → MS5607.
  - Buzzer → QMB-09B-03 THT, JLC THT assembly (D-R12-85).
- **Connectors (D-R12-29 … -31).** R11's GH ports become SH 1.0 mm clones in the MicoAir H743's port orders. J121
  becomes R10's DB125 screw terminal, and J1 becomes 5.0 mm-pitch pads.
- **U1 re-pins (D-R12-61A … -64A).** Barometer, microSD, QSPI_NCS, HG_CS and VBUS_SENSE move to balls facing their pads
  ([Firmware](#firmware)).
- **Stage D (D-R12-78 … -82).**
  - A third damper capacitor, C79.
  - C88 on the U9 feed.
  - Second vias on the fire return and U9 OUT.
  - The post stage and legend table.
  - J122's harness note made optional.
  - Polarity marks on the buzzer's Fab layer.
- **Port-fix pass (D-R12-83 … -85, stageD.md §7).**
  - PORT_4V8 divider R400 47k + R404 5.1k over R401 10k **0.1 % 25 ppm/K** (one new Extended code): every port inside
    4.5–5.0 V at every tolerance and drift corner (Calculation / Inference: drift and load budget; O-R12-16, -26 closed).
  - U9 0.3 mm east and C88 on the bar line: the U9 feed is 0.8 mm except its pin entry (O-R12-25 closed).
  - Every part JLC-assembled (owner): C84 → reflow-rated Honor HV1E477M0810PZ, LS120 JLC THT, no owner BOM.
- **AVA re-layout (D-R12-93 … -99, stageE_relayout.md).**
  - 79 parts re-placed from the plan's floorplan (D-R12-94): the sensor stack U5 / U6 / U4 on the roll axis, C84 beside
    U2, the port buck at the forward end of the RAW trunk, J2 / J3 mid-east.
  - 14 U1 re-pins (D-R12-93): servos on TIM1 / TIM2, the barometer on I2C1, the camera on UART4, PORT_SENSE on PD10,
    BAT_FAULT_N on PC13 ([Firmware](#firmware)).
  - In2: the 3V3A island dropped, the RAW_PROTECTED island reshaped (D-R12-95).
  - Copper routed again by zone and merged from fragments (D-R12-96); R107 and LED2 rotated (D-R12-97); C24 moved to J2
    (D-R12-98).
  - Outline 46 × 90 mm (D-R12-99, owner).
- **Owner acceptance.** The changes to REQUIREMENTS text are A-1 … A-10 in [R12_VALIDATION.md](R12_VALIDATION.md).

  TPS259470A.

  - OVLO rises at 9.80–10.71 V, so a 3S pack is cut off and a full 2S pack is not (Calculation).
  - ILIM is 3.92–4.89 A with the 1 % pair (Datasheet at 750 Ω; 1/R scaling and drift Inference).
  - The EN chain R80 / R165 470k, R81 300k, C87 220 nF and D402 is R10's.
- **Rails (Calculation / Inference: drift; `r12_windows.json`).**

  | Rail | Initial | Drift | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|

  VLOGIC is the diode-OR of RAW_PROTECTED (D5) and VBUS (D6).

## CH1 pyro

The circuit is unchanged from R10 / R11 in topology and gate values (AGENTS §2.2, §2.4). Q120 / Q121 (AO3401A) switch
BAT_IN to RAW_ACT; R134 2 Ω feeds J121.2 (IGN); J121.1 (OUT) goes to Q124 (AO3400A) and then GND. PC1 enables the bus
and PE4 fires (active high). Every gate keeps its series resistor, pull-down and gate–source capacitor (S-3 OK).

- **Layout.**
  - All five fire paths are F.Cu with no series via.
  - Q124's source reaches GND through two tracks and two vias, (8.2, 42.6) and (9.2, 42.6).
  - J121 is the DB125-2 at (7.4, 37.0) with R10's land. S-8 keep-outs on In1 / In2 surround both barrels (D-R12-68A,
    -73A).
- **Fire budget (Calculation / Inference: FET hot factors, pack, harness, match and converter load).**

  | Item | Value |
  |---|---|
  | Loop | 4.726699 Ω (R11 4.620493 Ω) |
  | Copper at 20 / 70 °C | 23.61 / 28.25 mΩ |
  | Fire current, idle … four stalls | 1.505934 … **1.250000 A** at BAT_IN 5.7209 V |
  | MJG J-Tek floor | 1.125 A: **+11.1 %** |
  | GND return Q124 → J1.2 (DC solve) | 2.585 mΩ, within the 5 mΩ allowance |

- **Shorted match (Calculation / Inference).**
  - Fire current 2.164 A at four stalls and 2.608 A idle; with the leads also shorted, 2.430 / 2.929 A.
  - Dissipation for at most 36 ms: Q120 / Q121 up to 0.631 W each, Q124 up to 0.492 W.
  - At four stalls, Q120 / Q121 gate-drive magnitude is only 4.40 V (4.35 V with leads also shorted), below
    the −4.5 V RDS(on) row. These fault-current and dissipation figures therefore assume an RDS(on) lower
    than the real value (Inference); they are not qualified fault bounds (D-R12-103).
  - Watchdog guard 34.853 ms against 36 ms (Calculation / Inference: R10's timing allocations and gate-discharge model; same inputs).
  - Adiabatic rise at 4.2 A: 0.5 mm 5.7 / 10.3 K at 20 / 36 ms; 0.8 mm 2.2 / 4.0 K.
- **Gate drive (Calculation / Inference).**
  - During a fire, Q120 / Q121 VGS is −4.576 V (about −4.58 V) at four stalls: Q120 drops 91.9 mV,
    so ACT_FET_S is 5.629 V. The margin beyond the −4.5 V RDS(on) row is 0.076 V (Calculation / Inference:
    Q120 hot factor and the 0.1 V Q122 drop). At BAT_IN 6.0 / 7.4 / 8.4 V during a fire, VGS is
    −4.80 / −5.94 / −6.76 V; bus on before fire gives −4.88 / −6.04 / −6.87 V under the same inferred model.
    It exceeds ±12 V only with the pack above about 14.4 V
    (maximum-ratio corner 14.41 V, assuming a 0.1 V Q122 drop; O-R12-10).
  - Q124 VGS is at least 2.778 V: (3.2401 − 0.4) × 9.9k / 10.1222k, above the 2.5 V RDS(on) row
    (Calculation / Inference: DS12110 Rev 8 Table 158 VOH allowance applied at 0.32 mA; drift; D-R12-108).
    Nominal gate τ is 48.4 µs (Calculation on R126 / C126).
- **PA6 during a fire (Calculation / Inference; O-R12-07, D-R12-108).** Each RAW branch uses the smaller of its own
  resistance from U9 and D5's: camera 10.81 mΩ, ports and D5 + R92 23.53 mΩ. These give lower bounds on the margin
  to the trip top (nominal ×11 / 1 % divider): healthy **+0.174 / +0.083 V**, match shorted **+0.025 / −0.066 V**,
  match and leads shorted **−0.019 / −0.110 V**. Healthy four-stall VLOGIC is 5.124–5.324 V; the trip window is
  4.62–4.95 V nominal, 4.537–5.041 V with 1 % resistors (PA6 0.42–0.45 V). A PA6 trip during the pulse must be
  logged, not treated as a battery loss.

## Firmware

No R12 firmware exists. R10's rules ([M3_R10.md, "Firmware"](M3_R10.md#firmware)) and R11's pin deltas
([M3_R11.md, "Firmware"](M3_R11.md#firmware)) carry over, except as below.

**R10 rule 4 override (D-R12-109).** For the first-article dummy-fire check, reject BAT_IN − RAW_ACT **above
0.4 V**, replacing R10's 0.2 V line. Use a 2 Ω dummy with 0.3 Ω total leads and the budget's 7.4 V / 0.15 Ω
source, no servo stalls, and the budget's converter loads. At the budget's 70 °C FET / copper corner, healthy
current is 1.505934 A: the two 73.5 mΩ AO3401A FETs drop 0.221 V; conservatively assigning all 28.25 mΩ of fire
copper to BAT_IN → RAW_ACT gives a healthy upper bound of 0.264 V (Calculation / Inference: pack, load,
hot factors and copper-temperature model). The 0.4 V rejection line lies above that bound and below the
0.7–1.0 V body-diode case (Inference; R10 rule 4). Qualify this line on the first article under these conditions;
it is not a measured limit for arbitrary sources or loads. All other cold-screen / powered-BUS checks stay.

**Peripheral setup (D-R12-93).** TIM1 is an advanced-control timer: its outputs stay off until BDTR.MOE is set. PA15 is
JTDI with its internal pull-up at reset. That pull-up, and PA10's USART1_RX pull-up in the loader, reach at most
0.864 V initial / 0.873 V drift against the 10 kΩ 1 % servo pull-down (Calculation / Inference: drift).
Release JTAG and keep SWD (PA13 / PA14) before configuring PA15 as TIM2_CH1. Keep MCO1 off on PA8. In the ROM loader,
PA9 (USART1_TX) may hold SERVO4 high as a steady level and PC11 (SPI3_MISO) may drive the unpowered camera's TX output
at J9.5 through R100 1 kΩ 1 % (resistor-only bound ≤ 3.47 mA initial, 3.50 mA at the drift maximum;
Calculation / Inference: drift); PC12 (SPI3_MOSI) is an input there, so R431 keeps the camera off
(AN2606 roles an Inference, O-R12-23).

**R12 firmware rules (O-R12-11, D-R12-05, -08, -09, -21).**

1. **PD10 (PORT_SENSE) is high when PORT_4V8 is up.** It reads 3.100–3.392 V initial, 3.064–3.430 V drift with PD10's
   ±350 nA leakage, and at most 0.036 V with PORT_4V8 off (Calculation / Inference: drift, D-R12-83 / -86 / -93). Enable
   the port UARTs only while PD10 reads **high**; this reverses R10's "while PA4 reads low". With PD10 low (USB-only)
   keep the port USARTs off and their pins analog. **R10 rule 5 is overridden:** PC1 may go high only while PD10 reads **high**
   (PORT_4V8 up, so U9 and J400 are on) and VBUS_SENSE does not contradict it. In R10 rule 7, replace the
   U11 reverse-blocking note (U11 is deleted): deglitch PORT_SENSE before re-enabling the port UARTs; its
   transient behaviour is unqualified (Inference).
2. **Keep PC10 / PC11 analog while U403 is off** (CAM_PWR_EN PC12 low). Never drive an unpowered camera. CAM_5V is
   PC2_C × 11.2.
3. **U9 auto-retries** after 110 ms on a fault (TPS259470A). A hard fault retries; firmware logs the BAT_FAULT_N (PC13)
   pattern and treats repeated retries as a fault, not a brown-out (Inference on the thermal duty).
4. **PA6 trip threshold 0.42–0.45 V** (VLOGIC 4.62–4.95 V; D-R12-21, O-R12-17). A PA6 trip during a 20 ms fire pulse
   can come from a shorted match. Do not treat it as a battery loss; log it (O-R12-07).
5. **New drivers.** The MS5607 (D-R12-52), the LSM6DSVE (U4; LSM6DSV-family registers, D-R12-50), and the FXLS90322 on
   HG_CS (32-bit SPI with CRC, D-R12-51).
6. **USB-only detection** from VBUS_SENSE (PC9) together with PD10.
7. **The R11 binding note does not apply.** No R12 binding note or executable binding exists. A PCB change invalidates
   the board hash, as AGENTS §3 requires.
