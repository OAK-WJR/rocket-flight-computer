# M3-ASSY-R12: R11's circuit, pruned and re-sourced, on a one-sided four-layer board, 46 × 86.85 mm

## Current state

This section is the one place that states where R12 stands. [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md),
[R12_VALIDATION.md](R12_VALIDATION.md) and [R12_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md](R12_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md) summarize it and link
here. When something changes, change it here first.

**Summary.** M3-ASSY-R12 is a CAD candidate on branch `m3-assy-r12` (the AVA re-layout, D-R12-93 … -99, and round 2's fixes,
D-R12-100 … -106, round 3's fixes D-R12-107 … -112, round 4's fixes D-R12-113 … -120, the AVA polish pass D-R12-121 …
-124, round 5's fixes D-R12-125 … -133, round 6's D-R12-134 … -143, round 7's D-R12-144 … -151 round 8's D-R12-152 … -160 and round 9's D-R12-161 … -169 and round 10's D-R12-170 … -173). It keeps R11's circuit: the
net names, the R10 safety networks and the TFBGA-100 ball map. Every part no requirement needs is deleted, cheaper
equivalents are re-sourced (D-R12-04 … -19, -49 … -52), and the board is laid out new for JLC **Economic** PCBA. That
means every part on the top face, four layers and 46 × 86.85 mm (D-R12-174; 46 × 83 mm from D-R12-171 to D-R12-174; 46 × 85 mm from D-R12-162 to round 10; 46 × 88 mm from D-R12-144 to round 9; 46 × 100 mm until the re-layout, 46 × 90 mm from D-R12-99 to round 7). ### What R12 changes from R11

- **Assembly and board (D-R12-01, -03, -24).** The six-layer two-sided 46 × 50 mm board becomes a four-layer one-sided
  46 × 100 mm board (46 × 90 mm since D-R12-99, 46 × 88 mm since D-R12-144, 46 × 85 mm since D-R12-162, 46 × 83 mm since D-R12-171) on Economic PCBA. Vias are 0.3 / 0.45 mm. U401 and its two VIPPO vias are deleted.
- **Pruned and re-sourced (D-R12-04 … -19, -49 … -52).**
  - USB limiter and mux → SS34 diode-OR D5 / D6.
  - Port LDO → U400 TPS563208 making PORT_4V8 directly.
  - Camera limiter and UART isolation → U403 TPS563208 with its EN as the camera switch.
  - U9 TPS259470L → A (auto-retry).
  - Pyro FETs → AO3401A ×2 and AO3400A.
  - D120 → BAT54C.
  - Crystal → X322525MOB4SI with C25 / C26 15 pF C0G ±5 % (D-R12-107, formerly 18 pF).
  - 22 µF capacitors consolidated on one 1206 code.
  - Port and camera UART ESD deleted (D402 kept).
  - U4 → LSM6DSVE.
  - New high-g U6 FXLS90322 on SPI1 (chip select HG_CS).
  - U5 → MS5607.
  - Buzzer → QMB-09B-03 THT, JLC THT assembly (D-R12-85).
- **Connectors (D-R12-29 … -31).** R11's GH ports become SH 1.0 mm clones in the MicoAir H743's port orders. J121
  becomes R10's DB125 screw terminal, and J1 becomes 5.0 mm-pitch pads.
- **U1 re-pins (D-R12-61A … -64A).** Barometer, microSD, QSPI_NCS, HG_CS and VBUS_SENSE move to balls facing their pads
  ([Firmware](#firmware)).
- **Stage D (D-R12-78 … -82).**
  - A third damper capacitor, C79.
  - C88 on the U9 feed.
  - Second vias on the fire return and U9 OUT.
  - The post stage and legend table.
  - J122's harness note made optional.
  - Polarity marks on the buzzer's Fab layer.

## Firmware

No R12 firmware exists. R10's rules ([M3_R10.md, "Firmware"](M3_R10.md#firmware)) and R11's pin deltas
([M3_R11.md, "Firmware"](M3_R11.md#firmware)) carry over, except as below.

**R10 rule 9 override (D-R12-157; R12F8-TRI-11).** PA6 reads VLOGIC behind D5, not the pack. At the pad load,
use a corrected pack estimate `Vpack_est = VPA6 / kPA6 + ΔVfeed_pad + VF_D5_pad`, where `kPA6` is the measured
R60 / R61 ratio and `ΔVfeed_pad` includes U9 (45 mΩ maximum RON) and the battery/RAW copper to D5.
The corresponding READY threshold is **`VPA6 / kPA6 ≥ 7.4 V − ΔVfeed_pad − VF_D5_pad`**, with measured ADC/divider
error reserved on the refusing side. D5 VF **0.25–0.45 V is an Inference**, so this is a Calculation / Inference
until the pad-load drops are measured; without `ΔVfeed_pad` it is 6.95–7.15 V on VLOGIC, not a fixed 7.4 V.
For conservative refusal before calibration, use the minimum supported drop correction (0.25 V for D5 within
that inferred range), and do not treat it as a qualified READY threshold. Firmware/first-article qualification
must establish the correction and error bound; telemetry labels the corrected estimate as pack voltage and
raw PA6 as VLOGIC. All other rule 9 checks carry. The falling PA6 trip is separately amended by D-R12-164.

**R10 rule 4 override (D-R12-109).** For the first-article dummy-fire check, reject BAT_IN − RAW_ACT **above
0.4 V**, replacing R10's 0.2 V line. Use a 2 Ω dummy with 0.3 Ω total leads and the budget's 7.4 V / 0.15 Ω
source, no servo stalls, and the budget's converter loads. At the budget's 70 °C FET / copper corner, healthy
current is bounded at R134's drift low end 1.9666 Ω: **1.52468 A**. Conservatively assigning all
29.975 mΩ of the 70 °C loop copper plus two 73.5 mΩ AO3401A FETs and the converter's coupled BAT_IN drop
gives **0.27318 V** (Calculation / Inference: pack, loads, hot factors, copper model and drift, D-R12-183).
The 0.4 V rejection line is above that bound and below the
0.7–1.0 V body-diode case (Inference; R10 rule 4). Qualify this line on the first article under these conditions;
it is not a measured limit for arbitrary sources or loads. All other cold-screen / powered-BUS checks stay.

**Rule 4 extension (D-R12-164, amended by -172; PA6, VLOGIC sense).** The PA6 trip threshold is
0.425 V falling / 0.455 V band top (R60 / R61 0.1 % 25 ppm/K; window 4.6274–5.0546 V VLOGIC at the drift
corner, Calculation / Inference). A PA6 trip that begins during a commanded fire pulse (from PE4 high
to the end of the ≤ 20 ms pulse plus its tail) is logged with its time and the PA6 reading. It is not
treated as battery loss: no battery-loss shutdown, shed or state change follows from it, and the fire
pulse is not cut short by it. A shorted match (or match and leads) still trips PA6 at the drift corner
(−0.1258 / −0.1705 V below the trip top; Calculation / Inference), so this rule is required. A healthy
four-stall fire is +0.0270 V clear of the trip top at drift (Calculation / Inference). A PA6 trip outside
a fire pulse is battery loss as before (hold-up 0.471–1.897 ms unshed at drift, Calculation / Inference).
Firmware and bench qualification remain open.

V_F 0–0.40 V, the match 1–2 Ω and its leads 0–0.3 Ω are Inference; ratiometric to VDDA):

**R10 rule 3 restated for R12 (D-R12-127; QSPI_IO2 / HSE_OUT / PE4 neighbours, R23-SAFETY-08 (1) carried; the S-10
exceptions QSPI_IO2 and HSE_OUT depend on it, R12_VALIDATION row 30).** Physics as R10: C126 220 nF, R126 220 Ω,
R127 1 kΩ, R128 10 kΩ. A steady IO2-against-PE4 fight puts 1.19–1.98 V on Q124's gate and fires the match after about
0.55 ms (Inference). AO3400A V_GS(th) min is 0.65 V (Datasheet).
- **Ground refusal:** before PC1 goes high, run a QSPI quad read-verify of a known pattern and refuse on any mismatch.
- **QSPI use while PC1 is high:** PE2 (ball A3) stays in its QSPI alternate function, never a GPIO driven high. No
  memory-mapped or parked quad reads, no long all-ones writes at a low clock, and the flash output drive is not at 100 %.
- **PE4:** write ODR low and select output mode before HSE is enabled, and keep PE4 driven low except during a permitted
  fire. An HSE start failure (HSERDY timeout) or a CSS event on the ground refuses.
- **Other neighbours:** never make PC13 (ball A2, BAT_FAULT_N) an output; keep it an input with no pull (it reads U9's
  FLT through R95 with D95 clamping it, D-R12-138). Keep U1's NC balls A1, B1, B3, B4, C4, D3 and
  D4 (PC14, PC15, PE3, PB8, PE1, PE5, PE0) in analog mode.
- **Cold screen:** during part of the passive cold screen, run sustained QSPI traffic and refuse if CONT or BUS moves out
  of band.
- **First article, before first power and coating:** X-ray U1 for bridges round ball C3. This replaces R10's "inspect
  U1 pins 1–5", which does not apply to a BGA. Then, with the board unpowered, meter U3.3 (QSPI_IO2) to R127.1 both
  ways. An intact board reads above 0.9 kΩ or open; a beep or under 100 Ω is a bridge: reject. 0.91 kΩ is R127 ∥
  (R126 + R128) (Calculation); the reading through the clamps is an Inference.

**R12 firmware rules (O-R12-11, D-R12-05, -08, -09, -21).**

1. **PD10 (PORT_SENSE) is high when PORT_4V8 is up.** It reads 3.103–3.388 V initial, 3.068–3.426 V drift with PD10's
   ±250 nA FT_xx leakage (DS12110 Rev 8 Table 60), and at most 0.025 V with PORT_4V8 off, against VIH 2.438 / 2.461 V
   (0.7 × the 3V3 maximum, initial / drift; ceiling VDD + 0.3 at the 3V3 minimum 3.554 / 3.524 V) (Calculation /
   Inference: the PFM band and drift, D-R12-83 / -86 / -93 / -131 / -139). Enable
   the port UARTs only while PD10 reads **high**; this reverses R10's "while PA4 reads low". With PD10 low (USB-only)
   keep the port USARTs off and their pins analog. **R10 rule 5 is overridden:** PC1 may go high only while PD10 reads **high**
   (PORT_4V8 up, so U9 and J400 are on) and VBUS_SENSE does not contradict it. In R10 rule 7, replace the
   U11 reverse-blocking note (U11 is deleted): deglitch PORT_SENSE before re-enabling the port UARTs; its
   transient behaviour is unqualified (Inference).
2. **Keep PC10 / PC11 analog while U403 is off** (CAM_PWR_EN PC12 low). Never drive an unpowered camera. CAM_5V is
   PC2_C × 11.2.
3. **U9 auto-retries** after 110 ms on a fault (TPS259470A). A hard fault retries; firmware logs the BAT_FAULT_N (PC13)
   pattern and treats repeated retries as a fault, not a brown-out (Inference on the thermal duty).
4. **PA6.** R60 / R61 0.1 %; threshold 0.425 V falling / 0.455 V band top (D-R12-164). Follow the rule 4
   extension above: a trip beginning during a commanded fire is logged with time/reading, without battery-loss
   shutdown, shed, state change or cutting the pulse short. Outside a fire it is battery loss as before.
5. **New drivers.** The MS5607 (D-R12-52), the LSM6DSVE (U4; LSM6DSV-family registers, D-R12-50), and the FXLS90322 on
   HG_CS (32-bit SPI with CRC, D-R12-51).
6. **USB-only detection** from VBUS_SENSE (PC9) together with PD10. R9 1 kΩ / R110 1.5 kΩ give
   0.6 nominal ratio. At drift, 4.45–5.5 V VBUS gives 2.634–3.344 V, high from 4.158 V VBUS;
   VIH is 2.461 V at the 3V3 maximum. PC9 FT maximum is 4.0 V even with VDD at zero;
   the drift maximum has 0.656 V margin (Calculation / Inference; D-R12-180). No internal pull.
   On battery-only power PC9 sees D6's reverse leakage across R110 (1.5 kΩ, 1.5 × the level the 1 kΩ gave): not
   computed, D6's hot leakage is unqualified (Inference); first article: PC9 reads low on battery alone, hot. After
   LIFTOFF a VBUS_SENSE reading never lowers PC1 or vetoes a fire (AGENTS §2.3; round 12 verification review).
7. **The R11 binding note does not apply.** No R12 binding note or executable binding exists. A PCB change invalidates
   the board hash, as AGENTS §3 requires.

**Additional R12 firmware rules (D-R12-186; R12F12-TRI-13).**

(a) Drive BUZZ_DRV (PE8) only as a software-toggled approximately 2.7 kHz square wave at ≤ 50 % duty.
Its timer function TIM1_CH1N is SERVO3's complement and must not drive the buzzer. Force PE8 low on every
exit and keep IWDG running while it sounds. A steady high draws about 0.32 A in a ≤ 50 mA coil
(Calculation / Inference: coil resistance and 3V3 window).

(b) Run SPI1 in mode 0 for U4 and U6. Write U4 IF_CFG.I2C_I3C_disable as the first access, before any U6 traffic.

(c) Keep USB VBUS sensing disabled: PA9 is SERVO4 on R12.

(d) Set PUPDR = 00 on PC9, PC10, PC11 and PD10. This overrides the carried R11 RX pull-up rule.

(e) Keep the system clock ≤ 200 MHz (U2 / L1 hardware budget, D-R12-184).

(f) The flight estimator and fire rule must work with U6 absent. The LIFTOFF AND barometric-gain rule stays.

(g) Do not start a microSD write burst while the buzzer is sounding. Schedule writes in beep gaps.
This is the hot stacked-corner margin rule in D-R12-184.
