# M3-ASSY-R12: R11's circuit, pruned and re-sourced, on a one-sided four-layer board, 46 × 100 mm

## Current state

This section is the one place that states where R12 stands. [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md),
[R12_VALIDATION.md](R12_VALIDATION.md) and [R12_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md](R12_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md) summarize it and link
here. When something changes, change it here first.

**Summary.** M3-ASSY-R12 is a CAD candidate on branch `r12-stage-d` (from `m3-assy-r12`). It keeps R11's circuit: the
net names, the R10 safety networks and the TFBGA-100 ball map. Every part no requirement needs is deleted, cheaper
equivalents are re-sourced (D-R12-04 … -19, -49 … -52), and the board is laid out new for JLC **Economic** PCBA. That
means every part on the top face, four layers and 46 × 100 mm. It is **not accepted and nothing is ordered**. R9 stays
the accepted board and R8 its fallback; R10 and R11 stay unaccepted candidates. No firmware exists, no physical test
has been done, and no review round (r12_plan stage F) has run. Decision IDs D-R12-01 … D-R12-82 are in
[`m3_design/r12/design/R12_DECISIONS.md`](../m3_design/r12/design/R12_DECISIONS.md). The plan and reading guide is
[`r12_plan.md`](../m3_design/r12/design/r12_plan.md), and the stage records are `stageB.md`, `stageC.md` and `stageD.md`
in the same folder.

### What R12 changes from R11

- **Assembly and board (D-R12-01, -03, -24).** The six-layer two-sided 46 × 50 mm board becomes a four-layer one-sided
  46 × 100 mm board on Economic PCBA. Vias are 0.3 / 0.45 mm. U401 and its two VIPPO vias are deleted.
- **Pruned and re-sourced (D-R12-04 … -19, -49 … -52).**
  - USB limiter and mux → SS34 diode-OR D5 / D6.
  - Port LDO → U400 TPS563208 making PORT_4V8 directly.
  - Camera limiter and UART isolation → U403 TPS563208 with its EN as the camera switch.
  - U9 TPS259470L → A (auto-retry).
  - Pyro FETs → AO3401A ×2 and AO3400A.
  - D120 → BAT54C.
  - Crystal → X322525MOB4SI with 18 pF.
  - 22 µF capacitors consolidated on one 1206 code.
  - Port and camera UART ESD deleted (D402 kept).
  - U4 → LSM6DSVE.
  - New high-g U6 FXLS90322 on SPI1 (chip select HG_CS).
  - U5 → MS5607.
  - Buzzer → QMB-09B-03 THT, owner-fitted.
- **Connectors (D-R12-29 … -31).** R11's GH ports become SH 1.0 mm clones in the MicoAir H743's port orders. J121
  becomes R10's DB125 screw terminal, and J1 becomes 5.0 mm-pitch pads.
- **U1 re-pins (D-R12-61A … -64A).** Barometer, microSD, QSPI_NCS, HG_CS and VBUS_SENSE move to balls facing their pads
  ([Firmware](#firmware)).
- **Stage D (D-R12-78 … -82).**
  - A third damper capacitor, C79.
  - C88 on the U9 feed.
  - Second vias on the fire return and U9 OUT.
  - The post stage and legend table.
  - J122's harness note made optional.
  - Polarity marks on the buzzer's Fab layer.
- **Owner acceptance.** The changes to REQUIREMENTS text are A-1 … A-10 in [R12_VALIDATION.md](R12_VALIDATION.md).

## Power

Figures are from `m3_design/r12_stageD.py` (`stageD_budget.json`) and `tools/r12_hotplug.py`, re-run on `d776c5d7…`
([stageD_analysis.md](../m3_design/r12/design/stageD_analysis.md)), and from `r12_budget.py` (`r12_windows.json`) for
the regulator windows.

- **Battery entry (D-R12-09).** The chain is J1 → D8 SMAJ12CA → the R166 / C89 / C95 / C79 damper → C96 → C88 → U9
  TPS259470A.
  - U9 auto-retries after 110 ms on a fault (Datasheet).
  - OVLO rises at 9.80–10.71 V, so a 3S pack is cut off and a full 2S pack is not (Calculation).
  - ILIM is 3.96–4.84 A (Datasheet / Calculation).
  - The EN chain R80 / R165 470k, R81 300k, C87 220 nF and D402 is R10's.
- **Rails (Calculation, `r12_windows.json`; the drift case is an Inference).**

  | Rail | Initial | Drift | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 3V3 (U2 LMR50410) | 3.270–3.431 V | 3.240–3.464 V | |
  | PORT_4V8 (U400) | 4.539–4.943 V | 4.489–4.999 V | O-R12-16: 11 mV under the LR900's 4.5 V at the drift corner |
  | CAM_5V (U403, EN = PC14) | 5.221–5.691 V | 5.162–5.757 V | |

  VLOGIC is the diode-OR of RAW_PROTECTED (D5) and VBUS (D6).
- **Hot-plug (Simulation / Inference; D-R12-78, -79).** R10's model, corners and limits run on R12's extracted feed
  with C79 modelled.
  - **0 / 2304 required cases fail.** The minimum U9 IN is −14.255 V against −15.0 V, and the steepest fall is
    9.251 V/µs against 10.
  - The C88 topology check passes: C88.1 → U9.5 is 3.03 mm, 3.72 mΩ and 0.99 nH.
  - The cold-limit and no-clamp checks pass.
  - **R166 and the damper stay (D-R12-09).** R166 shorted fails 1164 cases, and the pre-C79 44 µF fails 8.
  - The below-envelope 0 / 0.01 Ω harness rows fail as on R10, outside F9's ≥ 0.03 Ω domain.
- **EN timing and chatter (Calculation / Inference).** R10's model applies with R12's four-stall BAT_IN of 5.7208 V.
  - A single drop-out trips U9 at 8.13 ms.
  - The J400 bench rule's worst-corner limit is 7.56 ms. The rule itself is total open time under 7 ms in every sliding
    100 ms window, with U9 on throughout.
  - The 7 ms line holds down to 5.647 V.
  - Turn-on is nominally 88 / 56 / 45 ms at 6.0 / 7.4 / 8.4 V; R10's 30–130 ms window is kept.
- **Hold-up (Calculation / Inference).** C84 now holds VLOGIC only, behind D5. From a battery break it lasts 2.23–6.12 ms
  unshed with the card writing and 6.68–11.43 ms shed (R11: 0.46–0.54 ms). From the PA6 trip it lasts 0.47–1.72 ms. The
  required hold-up time is still an open owner question.
- **Copper and IPC-2152 (Calculation on CAD widths; the rise is an upper bound, an Inference).**
  - U9.6's 0.3 mm pin entry rises 76.8 °C at R10's 2.9 A design current. It is pin-entry class and has two vias now.
  - **The 0.4 mm C96.1 → C88.1 → U9.5 run (4.13 mm) rises 55.2 °C at 2.9 A**, about 36 °C at the 2.34 A fire-time
    current, which is over the 30 °C basis (O-R12-25).
  - The PORT_4V8 trunk (0.5 mm, 75 mm along the east edge) is 70–75 mΩ to the ports.
- **Port voltage under load (Calculation / Inference; O-R12-16, -26).** At the 0.561 A always-on budget the port pins
  read at least 4.497–4.500 V at the initial tolerance and 4.447–4.450 V at the drift corner (J420 lowest). The LR900
  needs 4.5 V.
- **U6 supply (O-R12-22, Calculation).** VCC is at least 3.258 V initial and 3.227 V drift at U2's 1 A rating. That is
  92 mV over U6's 3.135 V minimum: closed as a calculation.
- **USB-only power (D-R12-04).** VLOGIC is 4.30–5.00 V from a USB 2.0 host port (Inference on the diode drop). It needs
  at least 4.45 V at J3 (O-R12-03). The ports are off on USB-only power.

## CH1 pyro

The circuit is unchanged from R10 / R11 in topology and gate values (AGENTS §2.2, §2.4). Q120 / Q121 (AO3401A) switch
BAT_IN to RAW_ACT; R134 2 Ω feeds J121.2 (IGN); J121.1 (OUT) goes to Q124 (AO3400A) and then GND. PC1 enables the bus
and PE4 fires (active high). Every gate keeps its series resistor, pull-down and gate–source capacitor (S-3 OK).

- **Layout.**
  - All five fire paths are F.Cu with no series via.
  - Q124's source reaches GND through two tracks and two vias, (8.2, 42.6) and (9.2, 42.6).
  - J121 is the DB125-2 at (7.4, 37.0) with R10's land. S-8 keep-outs on In1 / In2 surround both barrels (D-R12-68A,
    -73A).
- **Fire budget (Calculation / Inference: FET hot factors, pack, harness, match and converter load).**

  | Item | Value |
  |---|---|
  | Loop | 4.726699 Ω (R11 4.620493 Ω) |
  | Copper at 20 / 70 °C | 23.61 / 28.25 mΩ |
  | Fire current, idle … four stalls | 1.505915 … **1.249978 A** at BAT_IN 5.7208 V |
  | MJG J-Tek floor | 1.125 A: **+11.1 %** |
  | GND return Q124 → J1.2 (DC solve) | 2.575 mΩ, within the 5 mΩ allowance |

- **Shorted match (Calculation / Inference).**
  - Fire current 2.164 A at four stalls and 2.608 A idle; with the leads also shorted, 2.430 / 2.929 A.
  - Dissipation for at most 36 ms: Q120 / Q121 up to 0.631 W each, Q124 up to 0.492 W.
  - Watchdog guard 34.853 ms against 36 ms (carried from R10: same inputs).
  - Adiabatic rise at 4.2 A: 0.5 mm 5.7 / 10.3 K at 20 / 36 ms; 0.8 mm 2.2 / 4.0 K.
- **Gate drive (Calculation / Inference).**
  - Q120 / Q121 VGS is −4.65 V at the four-stall point. It passes ±12 V only with the pack above 14.60 V (O-R12-10).
  - Q124 VGS is at least 3.17 V, with τ 48.4 µs.
- **PA6 during a fire (Calculation / Inference; O-R12-07).** A four-stall fire into a shorted match, with the leads
  also shorted, can bring VLOGIC 0.005 V (nominal ratio) to 0.096 V (1 % divider) into the PA6 trip band. Firmware must
  not read that as a battery loss.

|---|---|---|

**R12 firmware rules (O-R12-11, D-R12-05, -08, -09, -21).**

1. **PA4 (PORT_SENSE) is high when PORT_4V8 is up.** It reads 3.08–3.40 V (Calculation). Enable the port UARTs only
   while PA4 reads **high**; this reverses R10's "while PA4 reads low". With PA4 low (USB-only) keep the port USARTs
   off and their pins analog.
2. **Keep PD5 / PD6 analog while U403 is off** (CAM_PWR_EN PC14 low). Never drive an unpowered camera. CAM_5V is
   PC2_C × 11.2.
3. **U9 auto-retries** after 110 ms on a fault (TPS259470A). A hard fault retries; firmware logs the BAT_FAULT_N (PA5)
   pattern and treats repeated retries as a fault, not a brown-out (Inference on the thermal duty).
4. **PA6 trip threshold 0.42–0.45 V** (VLOGIC 4.62–4.95 V; D-R12-21, O-R12-17). A PA6 trip during a 20 ms fire pulse
   can come from a shorted match. Do not treat it as a battery loss; log it (O-R12-07).
5. **New drivers.** The MS5607 (D-R12-52), the LSM6DSVE (U4; LSM6DSV-family registers, D-R12-50), and the FXLS90322 on
   HG_CS (32-bit SPI with CRC, D-R12-51).
6. **USB-only detection** from VBUS_SENSE (PA10) together with PA4.
7. **The R11 binding note does not apply.** No R12 binding note or executable binding exists. A PCB change invalidates
   the board hash, as AGENTS §3 requires.
