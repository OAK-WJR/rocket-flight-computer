# M3-ASSY-R3: GNSS removed, 46 × 193 mm, cost-down

Status: **design and CAD only.** Nothing in this document has been measured on a real
board. Values come from the KiCad files, a named datasheet or JLC/LCSC page, or are
marked **INFERENCE**. Design spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-m3-assy-r3-design.md`.

## 1. Why

- The GNSS receiver is dropped from the vehicle.
- The board has to fit the main-section electronics bay. Measured from the booster/main
  joint in the vehicle CAD, the bay floor (bulkplate top) is at joint − 44.6 mm and the
  nose-shoulder base is at joint + 550 mm, giving 595 mm of free length. The stack from the
  floor up:

  | Item | Length |
  |---|---|
  | 2S battery bay | ~80 mm |
  | Battery leads | ~15 mm |
  | This board | 193 mm |
  | Eyebolt nut and charge wiring | ~25 mm |
  | Recovery (estimate) | ~260 mm |
  | **Slack** | **~22 mm** |

- The battery is no longer under the board. That frees the back of the sled and relaxes the
  height budget of `GEOMETRY_REV_G.md`. That model assumed a 54.66 mm bore with a 17 mm LiPo
  under the board. The board's first ~70 mm now sits inside the coupler bore (Ø50.8 mm). The
  servo header (courtyard 72.9 mm from the aft edge) lands in the full Ø54.6 mm bore, where
  plugged servo housings have room (INFERENCE from the chord model).

## 2. What changed

Board change by `m3_design/r3_cut.py` (re-runnable, idempotent):

| Item | Change |
|---|---|
| U6 SAM-M10Q-00B, C33 100 nF, C34 22 µF | Removed (schematic and PCB). These were the only GNSS parts |
| GPS_TX / GPS_RX | Nets deleted (109 segments, 4 vias). U1 pins 63 (PC6) and 64 (PC7) now carry no-connect flags |
| 3V3A branch to U6 | 15 F.Cu segments north of via (36.5, 172.3) deleted; all 14 remaining 3V3A pads stay connected |
| GND stitching forward of y = 186.2 | 99 vias deleted. Kept: the 12 strip vias clear of the new washers |
| In2 GND plane under the GNSS | Deleted; the full-board GND zones are trimmed to y = 192.5 and refilled |
| Outline | 46.0 × 193.0 mm (was 238.0) |
| Forward M3 pair | (4, 234) / (42, 234) → (4, 189) / (42, 189). The other six M3 holes and both zip-tie holes are unchanged |
| Silkscreen / title blocks | `M3-ASSY-R3`; sheet 04 renamed "Voltage diagnosis and indicators" |

**Verified copper unchanged.** Against R2, R3 has 2,418 of R2's 2,645 tracks and vias. The 227
deleted items are all listed above. **0 tracks or vias are new or changed, and no footprint
moved.** This was checked by UUID and geometry with pcbnew.

Board: 205 footprint positions, 180 fitted parts, 25 test pads. PCB SHA-256
`516f6a49…63d43f5` (before the field-only BOM edit of §4; see git for the final hash).

## 3. Side effects

- **3V3A (VREF+) filter.** C33/C34 were also on 3V3A. What remains is C10/C11 (2 × 22 µF),
  C22 (1 µF) and four 100 nF caps behind L2 (4.7 µH). By my estimate (**INFERENCE**, not
  simulated) the LC corner moves from about 8.9 kHz (67.5 µF) to about 10.9 kHz (45.4 µF),
  with slightly higher Q. The GNSS load (tens of mA) also goes away. Check ADC noise on
  3V3A at bring-up with the v0.9 bench image.
- **MCU pins.** PC6/PC7 are unused and left in their reset analog mode.

## 4. BOM cost-down

| Refs | From | To | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R74 R77 R100 R101 R103 | C22548 Yageo RC0603FR-071KL, 1 k 1 % 100 ppm 0.1 W 75 V 0603 (Extended) | **C21190 UNI-ROYAL 0603WAF1001T5E**, 1 k 1 % ±100 ppm 100 mW 75 V 0603 (**Basic**) | JLC API on 2026-10-05; LCSC datasheet `lcsc_datasheet_2206010130_UNI-ROYAL-Uniroyal-Elec-0603WAF1001T5E_C21190.pdf` |

**Considered and not made:**
- **100 nF C1591 → C14663.** C128/C140 are on PYRO_OUT in the simulated actuator band, and no
  DC-bias curve exists for the Yageo replacement. The line stays Extended either way, so the
  swap would save nothing.
- **All other Extended lines.** Each is protected or has no same-footprint, equal-or-better
  Basic or Promotional part. The full analysis is in the research record below.

**Result at 5 boards (live JLC prices, 2026-10-05):**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Parts, at placed quantity | **$54.94 per board** (`tools/jlc_cost.py`) |
| JLC order minimums and attrition (**INFERENCE**) | about +$4 per board |
| Unique Extended LCSC codes | **48** (R2: 50) |

**Fees** (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price, updated 2026-09-09):

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Economic PCBA feeder | $3.07 per Extended code, so 48 × $3.07 = $147 per order |
| Setup | $8.18 |
| Stencil | $1.53 |
| Through-hole | $0.0164 per joint + $3.58 labour |
| Bare PCB, live signed-out quote (46 × 193, 4 layers, 1.6 mm, ENIG, 1 oz in/out, 5 pcs) | $65.01 |

**All-in estimate: about $100–110 per board** before shipping, tax and U4 Global Sourcing fees.
R2 was about $130. The spec's $75 target cannot be reached without giving up robustness. These
options need an owner decision:
- Hand-solder the 5 THT connector codes (−5 codes). `DESIGN_ASSURANCE.md` bought JLC THT
  specifically to avoid cold joints.
- A Basic 12 pF crystal with new load caps (needs an AN2867 gain-margin check).
- Drop ENIG (−$2.48 per board) or 1 oz inner copper (−$3.38 per board). Both are part of the
  frozen stackup and finish rationale.

## 6. Pin-mux audit (pre-fab), DS12110 Rev 11 / RM0433 Rev 9 / AN2606 Rev 70

**No wrong pin assignment.** All 100 U1 pins agree between schematic and PCB. Every alternate
function and ADC channel used exists on its pin. PA5 = TIM8_CH1N (AF3) is now verified (it was
an inference in `M3_ACTUATOR_MERGE.md` §2.4).

**Open owner decisions, not changed in R3:**

| # | Item | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | PE4/PE5 (pyro gates) are also TRACED1/TRACED2. Enabling synchronous trace would drive the gates | Accepted: moving them needs re-routing. Mitigation: trace off, SWD only; LCKR lock; PE3/PE6 sentinels |
| P2 | PB0 (RAW_ACT_SENSE, 10 k / 4.7 k) is an FT_a pin. With the ADC on, its limit is 4.0 V. `sim_r2` shows 4.92 V at the 15.4 V TVS-clamp corner (transient, < 0.2 mA) | Open. A same-footprint fix exists: R125 → 3.3 k (C25890, Basic). It changes the RAW_ACT scaling and that branch's simulation |
| P4 | `PYRO*_GATE_N` are active-high despite the `_N` | Open: rename before the firmware binding is frozen, or document |
| P5 | IMU_INT1 (PE10) has no pull on the net | Firmware: internal pull-down |

**Documentation corrections found** (not yet applied to the frozen documents):
- The FT pin limit is 6.0 V, not 7.3 V, and 4.0 V for FT_a pins used by the ADC.
- PC2SO/PC3SO reset to 0 (switch closed) on LQFP100.
- The trace rule must be "off, SWD only": async SWO uses PB3, which is SPI1_SCK.
