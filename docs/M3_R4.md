# M3-ASSY-R4: R3 validation fixes, IMU change, first-principles requirements

**Status: CAD only.** Nothing here has been built or measured. Requirements are in
[REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md); the R3 findings this revision answers are in
[R3_VALIDATION.md](R3_VALIDATION.md).

**How R4 is built:** R4 is generated from the R3 files (git commit 533af8e) by the scripts below;
their docstrings give the reason for every edit. `m3_design/r4_build.sh` restores the R3 files from
git, runs them in order, and runs the board gate and the safety contract.

| Script | What it edits |
|---|---|
| `m3_design/r4_lib.py` | footprints (run on the R3 library) |
| `m3_design/r4_sch.py` | schematic (and the M3:R footprint filter in `kilib/m3.kicad_sym`) |
| `m3_design/r4_pcb.py` | board |
| `m3_design/check_actuators.py` | safety-contract rules |
| `m3_design/assembly_review/m3.kicad_dru` | custom design rules, including the pyro spacing rules (pass 2) |

Section 1 is the first R4 cut. Sections 1b–1n are what the evaluation passes found (twelve independent reviews; pass 11 closed a design gap pass 10 had kept, section 1m answers the review of that fix, and section 1n records the last review, which changed no copper)
against REQUIREMENTS.md, and what changed because of them. Later passes correct earlier numbers in
place; the tables say where.

**Doc-only findings (pass 2)**, now in REQUIREMENTS.md, ORDERING.md or the sections below:
- Ride-through numbers corrected (A1); F6 now states them at the 7.4 V launch floor, and what happens after a brownout longer than the ride-through (restart up to about 1.1 s, servo powered without pulses, then unpowered) is a bench item and an accepted risk.

- The sled is non-conductive over the whole bottom face and the rails.

No free spot exists within 6 mm. TP84's and R89's designators moved to the bottom side when the input damper took their spots, and C89's, C95's, R166's and R167's sit there too (pass 4, Y2), as do C25's, Y1's and C14's (pass 9: R170 and R171 took their spots), C13's (pass 10, AE5), and C120's, R124's, R125's, R143's, R168's, R173's, R174's and R181's (passes 11–12, clear of vias and nearer their own parts).
- **J122's "-" column, its channel numbers 1–4 and J123 have no top label** (no room: the series resistors sit 0.9 mm from the header on one side, parts and their designators on the other). The full legend is on the bottom side.
- **The DB128L terminals' 0.6 mm dovetail tongues sit outside their courtyards**: abutting pairs interlock by design. The only part at a free end is R182, 0.27 mm from black J120's; DORABO's drawing appears to put the groove, not the tongue, on that end (Inference: check at first assembly).
- **R72 (C861313) has no EasyEDA footprint**; its placement correction comes from the same R0603 footprint used by other parts (non-polar).
- **The ADC sense filters stay at the actuator-band end.** These are slow monitoring channels, logged only in flight. V3V3_SENSE (5 kΩ, no capacitor) needs a long ADC sampling time.
- **USB D+ meets D4 on a 2 mm stub, and U9 OUT reaches its first capacitor (C82) about 9.5 mm away.** Both are low-impact; the dense area makes moving them riskier than leaving them.
- **PB0 (RAW_ACT_SENSE) sees up to 0.75 V while VDDA = 0**: after switch-off C121 holds RAW_ACT for seconds, and at power-up RAW_ACT rises before VDDA. That exceeds the VDDA + 0.3 V analog-mode figure (AN4899, as quoted) but stays inside DS12110's unpowered FT-pin limit; since pass 10 the source is 5.6 kΩ (R172), so any injection is ≲ 0.14 mA (Inference).
- **D8's feed is a 0.7 mm F.Cu trace**, which would open before the external 15 A fuse if D8 failed short (Inference): it acts as a fusible link and leaves the board without its TVS. Since pass 5 nothing else hangs behind it: the OVLO divider (R82), the bleed (R167) and TP80 each have their own via into the In2 BAT_IN plane, so an opened link cannot silently take OVLO with it (Z3).
- **The middle four M3 holes need nylon washers**: signal tracks run 0.63–1.71 mm from the hole edge, inside the 3.5 mm washer radius.
- **No on-board servo-fault containment** (REQUIREMENTS, accepted risks). A hard short on a servo lead blows the external 15 A fuse and takes the primary computer down; recovery then relies on the isolated backup altimeter.

## 3. Consequences for firmware (recorded, not written or reviewed here)

- **IMU (LSM6DSV320X):**
  - WHO_AM_I is 0x73; set I2C_I3C_disable; SPI ≤ 10 MHz.
  - Keep OIS_PU_DIS = 0: pins 10/11 are open and rely on their internal pull-ups (DS14623 table 25).
  - High-g channel at ±64 g or ±128 g; integrate it above about 15 g.
  - Calibrate the high-g offset (±1.5 g typical) against the low-g channel on the pad, and re-zero the gyro there (±1 dps, 0.006 dps/°C).
  - Axes with U4 at 0° on the top side: +X_imu = +x_board, +Y_imu = −y_board (aft), +Z_imu out of the top face. So **roll = −gyro_Y**, the same mapping as the ICM-45686, and thrust reads −a_Y. Confirm the sign on the bench.
  - The IMU is about 15.5 mm off the roll axis: 1.7 g of centripetal acceleration at 1850 °/s.
- **Servo failsafe (F6):**
  - TIM5 keeps sending the last pulse through a CPU hang, until the watchdog resets the MCU. Make neutral (1520 µs) the first output after reset.
  - How the HV6120 behaves with no pulses, and unpowered under hinge load, is a bench measurement.
  - REQUIREMENTS F6 states the outcomes (pass 5 correction; pass 3 had prescribed a watchdog fed only by the roll loop, which contradicted FMEA §2 and V-1): neutral first after any reset; a stalled roll loop caught and the tab commanded neutral within about 50 ms without a reset; no single sensor or loop failure may cause repeated resets or block deployment; the watchdog serviced in every phase. FMEA F-1's 100 Hz safety task feeding a ~400 ms watchdog meets the reset outcomes but not the 50 ms target for a hang inside the roll interrupt or a CPU lockup, where TIM5 keeps the last pulse until the watchdog restarts the board (pass 6, AA4). F6 now allocates the fix: the same structure with the IWDG period ≤ 32 ms, the roll interrupt's run time bounded by transfer timeouts, up to about 6 ms for NRST to release (C24), and neutral within about 10 ms of release: about 48 ms in all. BOR level: FMEA F-3. Control window: burn and coast (REQUIREMENTS Sources), ending with every servo at 1520 µs ≥ 200 ms before any fire (F7, FMEA F-5's rule); F-5's "ends at burnout" is superseded by the project decision.
  - With the MCU in reset the servo lines are 11 kΩ to GND; a servo whose input pull-up is below about 60 kΩ to 5 V would read high (bench item).
- **Pyro:**
  - PE4/PE5 drive the gates, active high. They are TIM15_CH1N and TIM15_CH1, the complementary outputs of one timer channel: with both enabled, PE4 is the inverse of PE5 and channel 1 fires as soon as the timer's outputs are enabled (pass 5 correction; this line used to ask for TIM15 pulse timing). Follow FMEA F-16: TIM1/TIM15 never clocked, PE3–PE6 as GPIO locked with LCKR, the ≤ 20 ms pulse ended by a top-priority timer interrupt writing BSRR, the watchdog as backstop.
  - Into a dead short R134/R135 see about 34 W (4.1 A, below the 5 A link fuse, so nothing clears it). Bourns' single-pulse chart is crossed at about 59 ms; how the part then fails (open, or overheating) is not documented (Inference; bench item). Never repeat a pulse without a cool-down: at most 3 pulses per channel, ≥ 1 s apart, and no retry loop (Inference: 20 ms pulses 120 ms apart reach the chart limit at about the fifth).
  - Before the arming step, an arm-sense reading above about 0.2 V is a hard fault: a miswire, a stray strand, or both halves of a bus-side top shorted (pass 10, AE3; it was 0.3 V).
  - Cold continuity (links out, MCU on): arm only with CONT and arm sense both in the healthy band (set from the bench; about 0.09–0.11 V by calculation). Cold signatures per channel, CONT / arm sense (calculation since pass 11: 2 × 5.1 k over 2 × 2 k in parallel, 3.3 V, BAT54A about 0.25 V): healthy 0.098 / 0.098 V; open igniter 0.144 / 0 V; shorted FET 0 / 0 V; one drain-side half shorted (R173, R129, R174 or R141) 0.142 / 0.077 V; one bus-side half shorted (R132, R175, R144 or R176) 0.077 / 0.142 V; one drain-side half open 0 / 0.144 V; one bus-side half open 0.144 / 0 V, the same as an open igniter; one bottom half open (drain side) 0.184 / 0.100 V, (bus side) 0.100 / 0.184 V; one bottom half shorted 0 V on that reading; both halves of a top shorted (two faults) 0.26 / 0.023 V or 0.023 / 0.26 V. Anything outside the healthy band blocks arming; above about 0.2 V or below about 0.05 V on either reading is a hard fault.
  - The series resistors R168–R172 carry no DC current, so these readings are unchanged; sample PC0/PC1/PC4/PC5/PB0 with a long sampling time: the source is 5.6 kΩ nominal (5.67 kΩ with 1 % parts, against Table 184's 5.65 kΩ 10-bit limit), and about 2 µs is extrapolated from Table 185, which stops at 3.3 kΩ for 10 bits (Inference). Prove each input is connected: an open series resistor or joint leaves the pin floating. Read each input twice in a row, right after converting a channel near 0 V and right after VREFINT; a connected input reads the same both times, a floating one follows the previous channel (more than about 20 mV apart is a sense fault, never "healthy"; Inference, confirm on the bench by lifting one resistor on a spare board).
  - Drive the PE3/PE6 sentinels low. Never enable trace on PE2–PE6 or SWO (PB3 is SPI1_SCK).
  - After a reset in flight, re-arm only under FMEA §7.4′.4.
- **ADC and inputs:**
  - RAW_ACT_SENSE (PB0, through R172 since pass 10) is RAW_ACT / 11.2 since pass 11 (R177 + R124 over R125 ∥ R182). Refuse to arm when it reads outside the pack range.
  - PC3 (V3V3_SENSE) needs a long sampling time; never set PC2SO/PC3SO.
  - PC14 (pull-pin) reads low when the pin is in. It is an advisory pad-arming witness only (FMEA F-7, REQUIREMENTS F9): detect launch with the accelerometer, and never start the roll reference, the control window or flight logging on the pin.
  - Keep LED1 and LED2 off while the barometer is in use.
- **USB:**
  - PD15 is valid with the D+ pull-up on or off (the 1 k/1 k divider keeps a back-fed VBUS below VIL).
  - Soft-disconnect when PD15 reads low, debounced 10–20 ms.
- **Clocks and drive:** medium GPIO slew on lines that cross the In2 splits; QSPI ≤ 80 MHz. At 480 MHz (VOS0, 105 °C junction limit) a typical MCU dissipates 0.4–1.1 W and a worst-case one 1.3–1.8 W against 45 °C/W (DS12110 Tables 130, 228): a 70 °C bay allows about 0.78 W. Run at 200 MHz (300 MHz at most) from power-on to landing, or at 400 MHz only with junction-temperature monitoring that throttles (pass 5 correction, Z11; pass 6, AA5: in flight too). Enable the HSE clock security system. Timestamp IMU samples with an MCU timer (the IMU's output rate is ±1 %). On LQFP100 the USB supply is VDD: use the matching USB supply configuration, and keep the D+ pull-up off while PD15 reads low.
- **Logging and flash:**
  - Log at 500 Hz to apogee + 30 s and ≤ 50 Hz on the parachute, with a RAM ring buffer before launch (REQUIREMENTS F5).
  - Pre-erase the flash before flight; a chip erase can take 200 s.
  - Page programming can take 3 ms per 256 B, so keep records ≤ 128 B at 500 Hz (25 % throughput margin).
- **Power:** on USB power alone the actuators and both pyro buses are dead. U9's fault output (BAT_FAULT_N) also goes low on every reverse-current-blocking event, such as a servo-stall dip (SLVSFC9C Table 7-3): filter it before acting on it.
- **Firmware binding:** the R4 copper invalidates the board binding in `m3_firmware/build.py`; a new binding and pin-table review are needed before any build (AGENTS.md §3).

## 4. Operating notes

- **Power switch timing:**
  - The board comes on 0.4–1.1 s after the switch closes.
  - Switch bounce shorter than 0.19/0.33/0.41 s at 6.0/7.4/8.4 V does not drop it (worst case 0.11/0.21/0.27 s; at the 7.4 V launch floor at least 0.21 s).
  - A brownout longer than that restarts the board in up to about 1.1 s; meanwhile the servo is powered without pulses, then unpowered (bench item, REQUIREMENTS F6).
  - U9 is the latch-off variant: after a fault trip, hold the switch open at least 1.5 s.
- **Pack:**
  - 2S LiPo of at least 1.0 Ah for flight 1 and 1.5 Ah for flight 2 (camera and four servos), at least 7.4 V resting at launch after the pad wait; no LiHV packs.
  - At most 0.15 Ω of source resistance at the coldest pack temperature, including leads, connector and fuse.
  - With these, four stalled servos keep BAT_IN above U9's worst-case turn-off.
- **Camera:** keep it off until arming; it dominates the pad-time budget (REQUIREMENTS operating assumptions). It is fed from RAW_PROTECTED through U7; C84's hold-up serves the logic only while the camera is off (with it on, C84 also feeds the camera and holds for about 1–3 ms, Inference). A camera that powers its own 5 V input (internal battery) can back-feed the board with the switch open: bench-check the chosen camera. A 1 A camera on a 6.0 V pack draws about 1.02 A at U7, close to its 1.09 A minimum limit, and U7 latches off on thermal shutdown (cycle CAM_PWR_EN to recover).
- **J2 (SWD):** fly it bare. J2.6 is unconnected since pass 9 (AD2): BOOT0 stays on R5 at the MCU, and DFU goes through SWD or a firmware jump to the system bootloader.
- **A wrong 3S pack** keeps the board off (OVLO 9.63–9.96 V), and the input TVS does not conduct; only R167 and U9's OVLO-state draw, about 1.5 mA, flow. A deeply discharged 3S pack (≤ about 3.4 V per cell) can pass OVLO and put about 10 V on the servos. Avoid both.
- **Pyro block:** coat it and J2 locally before flight (mandatory, ORDERING §5), and never probe R134/R135 while armed.
- **MS5611:** keep LED1 and LED2 dark from arming to landing and cover U5 with opaque black open-cell foam. LED2 is 1.68 mm and LED1 2.3 mm from its open top.
- **Assembly:** Standard PCBA (240 ± 5 °C reflow; AA1). Fly only after U5 has been compared with a reference barometer at least 7 days after reflow (TE's recovery time), and only after the dummy-load pyro gates of ORDERING §5 have passed.
- **First power-up:** USB first, then a current-limited bench supply, never a battery first.
- **Battery harness:** crimped or soldered leads and a locking fuse holder. A break in the battery lead itself is ridden through for only about 5–15 ms (C84; about 1–3 ms with the camera on), against 0.21 s for the power switch.
- **After landing:** switch off promptly. U9 turns off only at 4.45–4.61 V (about 2.2 V per cell), so a long wait over-discharges the pack.
- **Pack changes:** ARM links out first. After any unplug, wait at least 2 s and check TP80 below 1 V before plugging a pack in (with the switch closed the actuator FETs can stay on for about 1.3 s; AD3).
- **Storage:** disconnect the pack. R167 (the input bleed, Y1) draws 0.84 mA with the switch open, so a 1 Ah pack is flat in about 7 weeks.
- **Pyro block on the bench:** never probe R134/R135 while armed (TP124, the GND pad between the igniter straps, was removed in pass 5; use TP88 or J2 pin 1 for GND).
- **Bench checks before flight:** reverse-polarity hot-plug with the flight harness and a warm pack (scope on U9 IN). Before it, measure R166 four-wire across its pads (0.32–0.34 Ω) and the BAT_IN–GND capacitance at 1 kHz with no pack (about 60 µF; about 40 µF means a damper capacitor is missing, about 20 µF that R166 is open), and after any unplug wait 2 s (TP80 below 1 V). Hot check: at 70 °C or more with the switch open, RAW_ACT stays below 0.1 V (Q122 leakage, pass 9). Plug a warm pack in 20 times and re-measure R166 (pulse check; more than 1 % change is a finding); camera back-feed with the switch open; servo behaviour with power but no pulses, unpowered under hinge load, and with its input against 11 kΩ to GND; one ARM link at a time with both arm senses read; USB hot-plug from an always-on legacy source (scope on VBUS); the plug-out cold test repeated with the camera on and the buzzer sounding (Z2); J3 leg solder fillets; and, destructively on one spare board, a channel held on into a dead short from an 8.4 V source able to give 5 A, recording whether R134 opens or overheats (section 3; sim_r2/FINDINGS). The cold-test readings in the healthy band also prove R168–R172 and their joints (section 3's floating-pin check).
