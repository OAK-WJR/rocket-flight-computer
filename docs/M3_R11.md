# M3-ASSY-R11: R10's circuit on a compact six-layer board, 46 × 50 mm

## Current state

This section is the one place that states where R11 stands. [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md),
[R11_VALIDATION.md](R11_VALIDATION.md), [R11_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md](R11_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md) and
[REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md) summarize it and link here. When something changes, change it here first.

**Summary.** M3-ASSY-R11 is a CAD candidate on branch `m3-assy-r11`: R10's circuit (netlist, part values and every
AGENTS §2 rule, D-R11-01) on a new 46 × 50 mm six-layer board with an STM32H743VIH6 TFBGA-100, 0402 passives where the
rating allows, latching JST-GH ports and plated wire pads for the battery and the pyro channel. It is **not accepted
and nothing is ordered**. R9 stays the accepted board and R8 its fallback; R10 stays an unaccepted candidate on
`m3-assy-r10`. No firmware exists, no physical test has been done, and no review round (r11_plan stage F) has run on
this board yet. Decision IDs D-R11-01 … D-R11-84 are in
[`m3_design/r11/design/R11_DECISIONS.md`](../m3_design/r11/design/R11_DECISIONS.md); the plan and reading guide is
[`r11_plan.md`](../m3_design/r11/design/r11_plan.md).

Evidence labels (AGENTS §2.5), as in the stage records: **Measured** = read from the CAD or a tool run on it;
**Calculation** = arithmetic on CAD copper and datasheet values; **Simulation** = the numerical hot-plug model;
**Inference** = anything resting on an unmeasured or estimated input (a figure with an inferred input is an Inference).
**Datasheet** = read from the maker's document. No figure here is a physical measurement.

### What R11 changes from R10, and what it closes

- **Package and size (D-R11-02 … -06).** LQFP-100 → TFBGA-100 (C964146); 38 resistors and 13 capacitors to 0402;
  eight DB125 terminals (945 mm² of courtyard on R10) → six GH ports and two wire-pad pairs; 46 × 90 mm, four layers →
  46 × 50 mm, six layers.
- **Closed R10 items.** U9's EN chain now has **no via** (R10 kept one, owner row 29; D-R11-25). VCAP1, U4.6 / U4.7's GND
  and Q120 / Q121's gate drive have second barrels (D-R11-29, -34, -57; R10 kept VCAP1 and U4.6 / U4.7 single), while
  VCAP2, BOOT0's and PA4's dog-bones are listed single-via exceptions (D-R11-45); the fire path is F.Cu only with no series via, and PYRO1_GATE has no via (stageD_analysis.md §2).
- **Netlist change (D-R11-86).** R11's only change to R10's netlist: the CAM_5V sense divider is R104 + R107 (5.1k +
  5.1k, net CAM_5V_DIV) over R105 ∥ R106 (2k ∥ 2k), so no single resistor fault takes PC2_C past its 4.0 V absolute
  maximum (218 parts).
- **New deviations.** The owner-acceptance list is [R11_VALIDATION.md](R11_VALIDATION.md); the main ones are PE4's 3V3
  ball neighbours at the TFBGA (D-R11-23; its driven neighbour B3 / IMU_CS is NC since D-R11-85), PC1's four inert
  neighbours (D-R11-85), nine single-via exceptions (D-R11-45, -48, -58), three route_check allowlist entries
  (D-R11-61, -85), the R11 legend set (D-R11-83), 0.5 oz inner copper and the JLC DFM queries.

## Power

R10's power circuit, values and tolerances are unchanged (D-R11-01); only the copper and placement are new. Figures are
from `m3_design/r11_stageD.py` (`stageD_budget.json`) re-run on `3bb83272…` (stageD3.md §3: every figure equal to
`5f1a7e1d…`'s, stageD2.md §4) unless labelled.

- **U9 front end (D-R11-25).** U9 (TPS25947, B.Cu, rotation 180) has EN (pin 1) facing J400 / R165. EN runs on one
  B.Cu run to R165.2 with R81 and C87 tapped 1.3 and 2.5 mm from U9.1 (Measured on the table): **no via in the EN
  chain**. IN runs J1.1 → C88.1 → U9.5 on B.Cu with no via (check_r11 U9 IN). J400.2 reaches R165.1 / D402.1 through one
  BAT_SW_RETURN barrel with a 1.3 mm S-8 keep-out; an open barrel leaves EN low through R81, so the board stays off
  (fail-safe, Inference).
- **EN timing and chatter (Calculation / Inference: C87's 0.72 X7R allowance; stageD_analysis.md §3).** R10's model
  re-implemented reproduces every R10 figure: a single drop-out that trips U9 is 10.35 / 20.11 / 26.02 ms at
  6.0 / 7.4 / 8.4 V, 8.07 ms at the four-stall 5.7133 V and 6.30 ms at 5.5 V; the one-open-block-per-100 ms limit is
  7.50 ms at 5.71 V. R10's bench rule (total open time under 7 ms in every sliding 100 ms window, U9 on throughout at
  5.71 V) stands. The 30–130 ms turn-on was not re-derived; its inputs are identical (Inference by identity).
- **Hot-plug (Simulation / Inference; D-R11-74, -79; stageD2.md §3; re-run on `3bb83272…` with identical figures, stageD3.md §3).** R10's model, corners and limits on R11's
  extracted feed, including the In2 BAT_IN pour (Kron-reduced; plane-pair inductance an Inference). Stage D failed 576 of
  2304 required cases on the 10 V/µs falling slew; with C80 moved onto the B.Cu trunk beside C88 (D-R11-79) **all 2304
  pass**: steepest fall 8.663 V/µs ('near'), minimum U9 IN −14.630 V (≥ −15.0), steepest rise 4.899 V/µs, R166 peak
  547.4 W / 2.925 mJ. The below-envelope 0 / 0.01 Ω harness rows fail as on R10 (outside F9's ≥ 0.03 Ω domain).
- **Rails (Calculation on CAD widths; IPC rise an upper bound, Inference; stageD_analysis.md §5, stageD2.md §4).** Every
  K-power path is on 35 µm outer copper and within the 30 °C basis except three recorded narrow pin entries
  (`power_narrow`: U9.5 0.3 mm for 2.0 mm, U9.6 0.3 mm for 1.5 mm, U401.9 0.25 mm for 0.5 mm). DC solves through the In2
  regions: BAT_IN J1 → U9.5 11.771 mΩ; RAW_PROTECTED U9 → U11 14.470, U9 → U400 22.733 mΩ; PORT_4V8 U401 → J410 / J420 /
  J440 17.742 / 19.951 / 15.398 mΩ (worst port drop 37.9 mV at 1.9 A, 11.2 mV at the 0.561 A budget). The narrowest
  current-carrying In2 neck is 1.4 mm against a 0.88 mm inner floor for 1.9 A (D-R11-27; Calculation, Inference inputs).
- **Hold-up (Calculation / Inference; stageD_analysis.md §4).** C84 and the allocations are R10's, so every figure is
  R10's: 0.464–0.539 ms all ports on, 1.304–1.412 ms camera off / shed; from the PA6 trip 0.103–0.164 ms and
  0.291–0.430 ms. The VLOGIC copper U11 → U2 is 20.456 mΩ (R10's trunk 7.0 mΩ), about 26 mV at 1.294 A, not in the
  model (its effect on hold-up not computed). The required hold-up remains an open owner question (R19-REQ-07).
- **USB-only (Calculation; stageD_analysis.md §5).** 0.4582 A at 5.00 V (R10 0.4596), still above U10's 0.453 A limit:
  R10's bench rule (full-speed or SD-logging tests on pack power, or capped clock and SD rate on USB) applies.
- **U401 thermal (Inference; D-R11-75, -80).** U401.11 carries R10's two GAP-3 VIPPO vias (0.5 / 0.3 mm, resin-filled,
  copper-capped, at (41.2, 22.4) / (41.8, 22.4)). R10's inferred 80–110 °C/W and 109.2–123.9 °C junction figure carry
  over only as an Inference: R11's inner planes are 15.2 µm and U401 sits over J430. The 100 mA AUX allowance rests on
  that Inference until a first-article thermal test; R10's 1.1 °C margin is not claimed.
- **Port endpoints (Calculation; stageD_analysis.md §5).** R10's endpoint minima (camera 5.031682 V, radio 4.601504 V,
  GPS 4.631504 V, AUX 4.642 V) take only harness, switch and transient allowances; R11's board copper adds at most
  0.6 mV at the radio's 0.30 A and 25 mV at the camera's 1 A, not in those minima.

|---|---|
| +X_imu | −x_board (west) |
| +Y_imu | −y_board (aft) |

Roll about +y_board reads **−gyro_Y**; forward thrust reads **−a_Y**. Lever arm to the roll axis (x = 23 on the top
face, R10's sled datum, Inference for R11's sled): **x = 0.0 mm** (R10 −6.0 mm), z = −2.06 mm (U4 3D-model box centre

on a_Z (Inference), so R10's "a_X reads negative during roll" sign check does not apply; a bench sign check of
−gyro_Y and −a_Y precedes flight. check_r11 M-7 holds the lever at 0.0 ± 0.05 mm.

## Firmware

No R11 firmware exists. R10 firmware cannot run on R11: an R11 binding changes the TIM, UART, ADC and EXTI assignments
together (binding note `r11_firmware_rules`). `m3_design/r11/design/r11_binding_note.json` (generated by
`m3_design/r11_binding_note.py`) is a **record only**, not an executable binding; `m3_firmware/assembly_binding.py`
(the R1 chain) and `tools/check_r11.py` do not check an R11 binding (D-R11-76).

**R10's rules carry over.** The 13 round 23 rules and the arming, timing, port-map, shedding, pins-and-debug, sensor
and fire-allocation rules of [M3_R10.md, "Firmware"](M3_R10.md#firmware) apply to R11, with R10's 49 obligations
carried verbatim in the binding note and overridden by its 19 R11 deltas. Where an R10 rule names a pin, use R11's:
