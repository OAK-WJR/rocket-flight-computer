## R6 implementation and validation update — 2026-10-13

This section records the current native CAD and supersedes earlier R6 implementation claims. Validation is still in progress; the fabrication package is not yet released. Existing Markdown was not used as design context.

Pyro channel 2 is removed. J9 is a JST GH 1.25 mm four-pin connector, J130 a JST SH seven-pin radio port, J131 a JST SH four-pin GPS port, and J2 a TC2030 contact footprint. LS120 is the JLC-stocked MLT-5020, C94598, with a conservative 5.3 × 5.3 × 2.5 mm body envelope.

The selected modules are EBYTE E22-900T22D and HGLRC M100 Mini. The dedicated AP63301 rail is nominally 4.8 V to stay below the GPS maker's 5 V maximum: calculated resistor/reference extremes are 4.674–4.930 V before ripple and transients. TPS2553 limits the shared port output to 351–449 mA; upstream TPS259570 limits input to 458–523 mA. A 10 kΩ enable pulldown disables the buck and upstream switch during MCU reset. Firmware must keep port GPIO high impedance until power is established and before disabling it.

Pin numbering follows the native connector pads:

| Connector | Pin mapping |
|---|---|
| J2 TC2030 | 1=3V3 reference, 2=SWDIO, 3=NRST, 4=SWCLK, 5=GND, 6=NC |
| J9 camera | 1=CAM_5V, 2=GND, 3=CAM_RX_WIRE, 4=CAM_TX_WIRE; original net mapping retained |
| J130 radio | 1=M0/PC8, 2=M1/PC9, 3=radio RXD from PC6 USART6_TX, 4=radio TXD to PC7 USART6_RX, 5=AUX/PA8, 6=port supply, 7=GND |
| J131 GPS | 1=port supply, 2=GND, 3=GPS TXD to PA10 USART1_RX, 4=GPS RXD from PA9 USART1_TX |

Radio and GPS signal lines have individual 1 kΩ series resistors and SRV05 protection matching the existing ports. M0/M1 have default pulldowns. The GPS manufacturer's stock pigtail lacks an unambiguous keyed pin-number drawing; use a verified custom harness matching J131, not an assumed stock cable pinout.

The current board is 46 × 100 mm, within the 110 mm maximum but above the preferred 80–90 mm target. It retains five mounting holes including the mid-span support and preserves channel-1 terminal geometry. The three peripheral connectors form one edge block. J1 is grouped beside channel 1. C29 now directly bypasses the flash VCC pin.

Independent layout review found and corrected a narrow actuator power path and a buck feedback trace beneath the switching region. Via banks and a 3V3 reroute restore the wide RAW_ACT front pour; C174 and feedback copper are now on the quiet side of U14. Preliminary 0.1 mm copper-grid calculations at 7.7 A: RAW_ACT 3.95 mΩ / 30.4 mV / 0.234 W; BAT_IN 4.13 mΩ / 31.8 mV / 0.245 W; ACT_FET_S 1.04 mΩ / 8.0 mV / 0.062 W. These are modeled copper values at room temperature, excluding component/contact resistance. Final board-bound results must follow routing.

The current five-board Standard assembly estimate is $84.14 per board with 48 extended codes. The retained U4 IMU supplier code is out of stock; the buzzer is stocked. Final BOM/CPL, cost evidence, CAD checks and fabrication package must be regenerated from the final board. Bench qualification remains necessary for supply startup/transients, module current, harness polarity, thermal behavior and flight tracking performance.
