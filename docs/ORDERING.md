Do not centre perforations on or inside Edge.Cuts. Verify the actual drill diameter, offsets and rail support in Confirm Production file.

In R9 C303 sits at the bias-tap node, east of the line (R9_DECISIONS D-S4-02); its role below is unchanged. **What each land may take when fitted by hand on the bench** (never as a JLC line: each new code would be Extended, past REQUIREMENTS M3's 52-code limit):
  - **C303 (GNSS_RF_ANT): a capacitor only.** It sits on the 3.2 V antenna-bias node (L30's antenna end), so an inductor there shorts the bias: the limiter holds the current, but the antenna goes dark. Use a capacitor rated ≥ 6.3 V.
  - **C305 (GNSS_RF_IN): a capacitor only while R304 is 0 Ω**, for the same reason (R304 joins it to the bias node). It may take an inductor only once R304 is replaced by a series capacitor (U30's RF_IN is DC-blocked inside the module, u-blox DS note 18).
  - **C313 and C314 (radio): a capacitor or an inductor.** The radio line carries no DC (D31 is a bidirectional TVS); an 0402 inductor at C313 can serve as a DC bleed to GND for a DC-floating antenna (EBYTE manual §5.1 shows 330 nH at ANT; `pass2_electrical.md` §5–6).
  - **C304 (GNSS_ANT_GATE): a capacitor only, 1.0–2.2 nF**, 0402, X7R or C0G, ≥ 10 V (the node never exceeds VCC_RF, about 3.2 V), for example Samsung CL05B102KB5NNNC (1.0 nF, 50 V X7R, LCSC C14442) or CL05B222KB5NNNC (2.2 nF, LCSC C84703), the same series as C302; start with 1.0 nF, the value pass 2's loop model used (62° worst phase margin, `pass2_electrical.md` ELECTRICAL-01). Fit it only if the short test oscillates (section 5). Never an inductor or a short there: it holds Q30's gate at GND, so Q30 stays fully on, Q32 cannot lift the gate (no current limit) and LNA_EN cannot switch the antenna off; a resistor to GND weakens both in the same way.

The 123.9 °C figure counts only U401's own heating: U400 and L400 (8.7–13.3 mm away) lose 1.2–2.2 W and logic and SD writes add 1.4–2.2 W in the same case, on a board with roughly 10–15 °C/W to the bay air (Inference), and U400 has no junction estimate (about 0.46 W, roughly 41 °C rise at 89 °C/W, [Diodes AP63201 DS41326](https://www.diodes.com/assets/Datasheets/AP63200-AP63201-AP63203-AP63205.pdf) p4 θJA on a single-layer board). The worst outcome is losing the GPS, radio and AUX together (thermal shutdown); there is no pyro hazard. The calculated 123.9 °C against TJ ≤ 125 °C rests on the inferred 110 °C/W (JEDEC RθJA 62.5 °C/W, [TI SBVS282A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a91.pdf) pp. 4–5; the board's real figure is unmeasured), a 1.1 °C margin. If the measured figure leaves no margin, lower the AUX allowance.

- Keep PC1/ACT_EN_MCU and PE4 low for the **passive cold screen before enabling the bus**. After any prior enable,
  wait ≥ 1 s and verify discharge/ACT_GATE off. Qualify healthy CONT/BUS ≈ 99.369/99.338 mV and open ≈ 146.23/0 mV
  with actual leakage/ADC/temperature error; ground faults refuse enable. Only then enable PC1, keep PE4 low and
  wait ≥ 100 ms before powered checks. In flight these readings are logged/annunciated and never veto a fire.
- **Load-switch gate drive (D-R18-01, D-R18-02, D-R19-04).** With PC1 high, measure the Q122 gate (2.19 V nominal; 1.92 V at the VOH floor; C123 10 nF now sets a gate time constant of about 7 µs, Calculation) and ACT_GATE settling at the lowest loaded battery voltage (calculated worst-case settled |VGS| 4.74 V, 31.9 ms to −4.5 V; the ≥ 100 ms wait above stays). Q122 saturating at that gate is Inference, because AO3400A RDS(on) is specified only at 2.5 V.
- Replace ARM-link transitions with 20 screw-switch on/off cycles, verifying RAW_ACT<1 V and 3V3<0.3 V between cycles
  with all back-feed sources disconnected. Retain reset, camera-switching, servo/buzzer and source-transition disturbance tests.
  Firmware may fire only after accelerometer+barometer launch detection; commanded dummy-load firing exercises that policy.
  With C87 at 220 nF (D-R19-10) the calculated turn-on delay is 30–129 ms and a J400 drop-out shorter than 10.6 / 20.5 / 26.4 ms (worst case at 6.0 / 7.4 / 8.4 V) cannot trip U9 (Calculation). Measure the chosen switch's contact chatter at flight vibration: pass if the longest drop-out is < 10 ms (REQUIREMENTS F6).
- Static/leakage gates use the flight firmware, dummy load and isolated instruments. After coating and before each flight,
  powered NRST at C24.1 must be **≥0.90×3V3** measured at C15.1 with a 10 MΩ DMM, corresponding to the retained
  ≥ 250 kΩ leakage requirement. Do not substitute an unpowered resistance reading.
- Test PC1/PE4 low-output modes and their GPIO locks/readback in reset/loader/debug transitions, with synchronous trace
  disabled; qualify the documented debugger-override experiment with a dummy load. No trace/debugger test with an e-match.
  Normal physical current ends ≤ 20 ms including tail; intact-hardware watchdog-fault guard ≤ 36 ms. Qualify IWDG timing,
  physical tail, hot dummy-load pulses, source/harness resistance and selected MJG J-Tek lot/charge 20 ms firing.
- **Future programming/boot acceptance (D-R17-02):** with e-match disconnected,
  record and read back the intended user-flash BOOT_ADD0 and relevant boot settings,
  then verify reset reaches the bound application. BOOT0's 10k pull-down selects
  BOOT_ADD0; it does not constrain that option byte. [ST AN2606 Rev 70 Table 2
  Pattern 10 p35 and Table 135 p321](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/an2606-stm32-microcontroller-system-memory-boot-mode-stmicroelectronics.pdf)
  permits BOOT0=0/BOOT_ADD0=0x1FF0 ROM entry; PA2 can drive USART2_TX onto servo 3
  despite R152/R156. No option bytes were programmed/read here and no R10 firmware
  exists. Deliberate ROM service uses dummy loads/no e-match, only before IWDG
  starts, with PC1/PE4 locked low, camera EN/OE low and SD_CS high. PC1/PE4 are not
  loader pins. The current primary text was checked; current PDF raster inspection
  is not claimed.
- **Port faults/back-feed:** GPS/radio/AUX are powered whenever the battery path is on (they are off on USB-only power) and have no EN/OE/FAULT or individual power-cycle facility.
  Their digital UARTs require powered-board FT setup and internal RX pulls always off; **a device on J410, J420 or J440 must drive its TX high at no more than 3.6 V** (no 5 V-logic devices), because PORT_4V8 rises about 44 ms or more before 3V3 and an FT pin allows only 4.0 V while VDD = 0 (DS12110 Rev 8 Table 119 p212; R20-MCU-02); measure the PORT_4V8-to-3V3 lag on first articles; the operating pull-off threshold is relevant I/O supply+0.3 V, separate from the 4 V absolute-stress note; no self-powered module
  with the board off. Short each port with worst camera/SD load and observe logic/flight-state survival.
  Camera alone retains U402/U12/U13: test OE-before-EN disable and all allowed source transitions.
  With no e-match, characterize separately powered-port reverse current and rail decay; back-feed may hide J400 opening.
  Do not promise detection/shutdown of an unobservable opening. Connect the e-match only without USB or any self-powered module on any port (GPS, radio, AUX or camera).
- **Power/thermal:** measure port endpoints at 6.0 V loaded battery, maximum GPS/radio/camera/card loads and the 100 mA AUX allowance (U401 junction calculated at 109.2–123.9 °C at a 70 °C bay with the inferred 80–110 °C/W, against 125 °C), U400/U401 and
  U9 neck temperatures, independent Q124 cutoff and high-side turn-on at 70 °C. U401's VIPPO and calculated thermal
  estimate require first-article temperature verification. Qualify USB-only attach/startup against the actual current-limited host.
- **USB-only operation (R18-PWR-02, R18-MCU-02).** With the pack disconnected, U10 (R85 66.5 kΩ; limit 0.3477–0.4530 A) is the only limit between VBUS and the logic. The upper allocation of 1.4 W logic
  + 0.825 W continuous SD writes would draw 0.4596 A at VBUS 5.00 V and 0.4856 A at 4.75 V, above the 0.453 A maximum; at the minimum limit the guaranteed input power is about 1.61 W (at most 0.785 W of logic with
  continuous SD writes); the lower allocation (0.6 W + 0.776 W) draws 0.281–0.296 A and fits (Calculation; details in M3_R10, "Power"). Run full-speed and SD-logging tests on pack power (switch on, e-match disconnected, dummy loads),
  or cap the clock and SD write rate in firmware while PA4 (LOGIC_SOURCE_ST) reports the USB input, and measure the USB-only draw on first articles. The GPS and radio ports are off on USB-only power: disconnect
  the J410, J420 and J440 module leads for USB-only sessions, or verify that J410.3, J420.3 and J440.3 stay near 0 V, and characterise module behaviour. Firmware enables each port TX (PD8, PB13, PC6) only while PA4 reads low (U11 on IN2, the battery) and parks it analog, no pull, when PA4 rises or PA6 trips (D-R19-13).
- **Per-flight check before the e-match is inserted (R18-SAF-06, R19-SAF-02).** With the pack connected and J400 open:
  (1) confirm the board is **off**: no telemetry and no buzzer, or, with access, RAW_PROTECTED at TP81 and 3V3 at C15.1 both below 0.3 V. A board that is on with J400 open means a leak or bridge at the EN network, a chafed J400.2 lead (R19-SAF-02), or an open via in the EN chain to R81 (R20-SAF-01: EN would float 0.2 mm from BAT_IN; Inference): do not insert the e-match;
  (2) measure J121.2 (IGN) to GND with a floating meter (never an earthed instrument ground on an igniter terminal): it must read below 0.1 V. A higher reading means Q120/Q121 or their gate bias has failed on, so the bus is live with the switch off; do not insert the e-match. TP120 (RAW_ACT) faces the sled and is not a substitute for this reading;
  (3) after any check with J400 closed, open J400 and confirm again that the board turns off before anyone works at the rocket.
- **PORT_4V8 level, first article and pre-flight (R20-PWR-02).** On pack power, with the e-match disconnected and before any module is connected, measure J410.1, J420.1 and J440.1 to GND: each must read 4.71–4.87 V. This is an ordinary level check of U401 and its feed. Since D-R20-02 the feedback tap R402.1 joins the output after both OUT vias (new via (30.575,11.6)), so a single open OUT via no longer leaves FB at GND; any fault that did would raise PORT_4V8 to about 5.2–5.55 V on all three ports (Calculation), above the LR900's 4.5–5 V and the M100 Mini's 3.3–5 V. Do not connect a module if the reading is out of range.
- **Hot-plug:** use the final unfused harness, measured R ≥ 0.03 Ω and L 0.1–2 µH, switch open and e-match/USB/self-powered
  sources disconnected. Wait ≥ 2 s after unplugging and confirm TP80<1 V before reconnection. R166 is 0.36 Ω/1%;
  measure resistance before/after repeated hot/cold insertions. Scope U9 IN and OUT together; below −10 °C,
  IN≥max(−15 V, OUT−21 V), falling slew ≤ 10 V/µs, rising ≤ 100 V/µs. The current board's extracted-feed model
  passes 2304/2304 cases (Simulation/Inference); this does not qualify the actual harness or measured hot-plug waveform.
- **Battery-break/SD:** no hardware port-buck cutoff or GPS/radio shedding. Qualify PA6 watchdog threshold, latency,
  stopping SD/QSPI writes/UARTs without flush, camera OE/EN and actual load decay with state/sensors/safety timing retained.
  Current allocation (with the 100 mA AUX allowance) gives 0.464–0.539 ms all-ports/SD-write and 1.304–1.412 ms camera-off/logic-shed/card-idle at 300.8 µF
  to 3.8 V (Calc/Inference), not measured hold-up. Select a card ≤ 200 mA write/≤30 mA idle and qualify busy latency,
  buffering, power-loss/reset recovery and full-flight storage alongside primary QSPI.
- **Sensors/installed assembly:** retain the measured under-body IMU_CS 0.7596+0.0918 mm,
  SPI1_MISO 0.3257 mm, IMU_INT1 0.3250 mm and 3V3A 0.3257 mm stress-guidance departure
  (R16-MEC-01/D-R16-02). The rejected prototype has 0.106949 mm supply clearance; the next combined
  recipe creates an acute 45° CS junction at B(14.2,44.7). Neither is a
  production fix ([receipt](../m3_design/assembly_review/sources/r10_review/round16_minor_prototypes.json)); no complete TN1383 compliance is claimed. Qualify first-article
  bias/temperature/vibration. Verify IMU roll=−gyro_Y, thrust=−a_Y and negative a_X during roll with the retained −6 mm
  lever arm; check HSE under buzzer/QSPI activity, including the OSC_IN amplitude (the drive level could reach about 242 µW at worst-case ESR and swing: Inference, R18 MCU lens), and U4/U5 temperature under MCU load. U5 needs its recovery interval,
  venting and opaque-cover light/LED-toggle test; keep LEDs dark in flight until that test passes.
  With dummy load, installed LR900-F at full power from its flight position must cause no fire trigger and no invalid
  continuity/bus readings. Complete harness/tool/card/sled/bore/shroud and shock/vibration/retention checks.
