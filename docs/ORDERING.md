# Ordering M3-ASSY-R7 prototypes (JLCPCB)

**This is a bench-prototype order, not a flight release.** The first boards are for power-up, dummy-load fire tests, servo tests and the RF bench tests in section 5. No firmware exists for R7 yet (it needs a new board binding, AGENTS.md §3), and v0.8.1 must not run on R2–R7. What R7 changed and what is still open: `docs/M3_R7.md` and `docs/R7_VALIDATION.md` (R7 is R6's circuit plus the on-board GNSS and radio: `docs/M3_R6.md`). Requirements: `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`. Parts plus feeder fees are about $106 per board at 5 boards (2026-10-17 and 2026-10-18 runs; prices change daily); the all-in price (PCB, assembly fixed fees, the consigned IMU, shipping) is well above that.

Coordinates below are KiCad millimetres: x = 0 is the left long edge, **y = 0 is the aft edge, where the two SMA jacks are**, and y = 118 is the forward edge, where J1's wire entry faces. Gerber Y = −KiCad y.

Filled copper starts 0.50 mm inside the board edges (the SMA pads on the aft edge excepted), so a 0.60 mm hole needs its centre at least 0.30 mm outside the finished board, on the sacrificial tab side. ## 4. Assembly (PCBA)

- **Assembly side: both sides.** Top: 96 parts — 89 reflowed SMD parts, the two SMA jacks J30/J31 (fitted in the through-hole step) and five through-hole parts (J1, J121, J122, J123, J124) — plus 2 test pads (TP30, TP84); bottom: 123 SMD parts and 22 test pads (bare copper; TP89 moved to the bottom in review pass 2). 219 placed parts in all. Heavy and tall parts (C84, C121, the three screw terminals, J3, J9, J122, J123, LS120, U1, both RF modules and both jacks) are on the top; the bottom carries only SMD parts and test pads. J2 is a TC2030 pad pattern with no part.
- **Service: Standard PCBA including through-hole assembly**, so JLC also fits the terminals, headers and the SMA jacks (the CPL contains every placed part, SMD and THT). JLC's capabilities page (https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities, read 2026-10-09) gives the reflow as 240 ± 5 °C for Standard and 255 ± 5 °C, not adjustable, for Economic. U5 (TE MS5611: 250 °C, 40 s max; TE's AN808 forbids hand soldering it), C84 (Panasonic OS-CON SVPK, lead-free condition C: 250 °C) and LS120 (Huaneng MLT-5020, C94598: 250 °C peak, ≤ 10 s above 245 °C, preheat 170–190 °C for 90 ± 30 s, ramps ≤ 4 °C/s; datasheet §3 item 9, https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/23f5c6599ed9cb614c21ac31ad2b1106.pdf?productCode=C94598, closing `pass1_requirements.md` R7P1-13) allow only a 250 °C peak; u-blox gives the MAX-F10S a 245 °C peak with 40–60 s above the 217 °C liquidus (IM §5.2: exceeding it "may permanently damage the module"), exactly Standard's upper bound, so the order remarks ask for that limit on the final reflow; EBYTE gives the E22-900MM22S a 230–250 °C peak with 30–90 s above liquidus (manual §7.1). **Economic is not acceptable for this BOM**, and it assembles one side only. C121's maker publishes no datasheet.
- **Quantity:** JLC assembles at least 2 boards; stock was checked for 5.
- **Edge rails and fiducials: added by JLCPCB** (Standard requires them); the rails carry JLC's own tooling holes. TH1 (44.8, 27.2), TH2 (1.4, 82.6) and TH3 (1.4, 112.0), 1.152 mm NPTH with 0.148 mm mask expansion per JLC's tooling-hole article (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-add-tooling-holes-for-pcb-assembly-order), stay on the board; Standard does not need them.
- **DNP placeholders:** C303, C305 (GNSS) and C313, C314 (radio), top side, are 0402 pi-network shunt lands for later VNA tuning (each joins its RF line through a 0.94 mm trace to the series pad; include those traces in the tuning model, `pass3_rf.md` P3-RF-03); C304 (bottom, beside R302 at (7.74, 16.90)) is the limiter's loop-compensation land, GNSS_ANT_GATE to GND (section 5). They are in no BOM and no CPL, carry no paste, and JLC fits nothing there. R304 and R316 (0 Ω series links) are fitted. TP30 (SAFEBOOT) is a bare test pad. **What each land may take when fitted by hand on the bench** (never as a JLC line: each new code would be Extended, past REQUIREMENTS M3's 52-code limit):
  - **C303 (GNSS_RF_ANT): a capacitor only.** It sits on the 3.2 V antenna-bias node (L30's antenna end), so an inductor there shorts the bias: the limiter holds the current, but the antenna goes dark. Use a capacitor rated ≥ 6.3 V.
  - **C305 (GNSS_RF_IN): a capacitor only while R304 is 0 Ω**, for the same reason (R304 joins it to the bias node). It may take an inductor only once R304 is replaced by a series capacitor (U30's RF_IN is DC-blocked inside the module, u-blox DS note 18).
  - **C313 and C314 (radio): a capacitor or an inductor.** The radio line carries no DC (D31 is a bidirectional TVS); an 0402 inductor at C313 can serve as a DC bleed to GND for a DC-floating antenna (EBYTE manual §5.1 shows 330 nH at ANT; `pass2_electrical.md` §5–6).
  - **C304 (GNSS_ANT_GATE): a capacitor only, 1.0–2.2 nF**, 0402, X7R or C0G, ≥ 10 V (the node never exceeds VCC_RF, about 3.2 V), for example Samsung CL05B102KB5NNNC (1.0 nF, 50 V X7R, LCSC C14442) or CL05B222KB5NNNC (2.2 nF, LCSC C84703), the same series as C302; start with 1.0 nF, the value pass 2's loop model used (62° worst phase margin, `pass2_electrical.md` ELECTRICAL-01). Fit it only if the short test oscillates (section 5). Never an inductor or a short there: it holds Q30's gate at GND, so Q30 stays fully on, Q32 cannot lift the gate (no current limit) and LNA_EN cannot switch the antenna off; a resistor to GND weakens both in the same way.

## 5. After delivery (JLC does none of this)

- **Cover U5's two pressure ports** with a small dot of polyimide tape before any of the steps below (TE asks for the opening to be protected from particles during assembly), and remove it only before the 7-day barometer comparison.
- **Inspect J3's four shell legs** from both sides; reflow by hand with extra solder wherever a slot is not filled. **Inspect J30/J31:** all four leg fillets, top and bottom, visually or by X-ray (the leg pads are solid in the GND pours with stitching vias 0.2 mm away, so cold joints are a realistic first-article defect; `pass2_dfm.md` DFM-06); flange flush to the edge; check the jack fit in the 1.7 mm slot (JLC's board is 1.6 mm ± 10 %). Inspect D30/D31 beside the centre pads for bridges or shift (DFM-05), and **D30, D31 and C302 seated flat (no tombstone or skew)**: D30.2 and D31.2 connect solid to the GND pour (no thermal relief, kept for the clamp's low-inductance return), which heats their two terminals unequally in reflow, and C302 sits beside them (P3-RF-02, P3-DFM-05, P4-RF-03; tombstoning risk is a general-practice inference with no maker figure). **Before any power-up, ohmmeter each jack's centre pin to its shell:** J31 reads open (D31 is a TVS); J30 reads like a diode into the unpowered bias path (L30, then Q30's body diode), not a short (Inference). A short on either means a bridge at D30/D31 or the centre pad: rework before power.
- **Remove the edge rails** by nibbling the tabs with a cutter; never snap them (R166 sits 0.75 mm and TP84 0.7 mm from the left edge; J9's retention tabs 0.5 mm, TP76 0.7 mm and C74 0.73 mm from the right edge). File the tab stubs flush with the board edge.
- **Order of the remaining steps:** bench acceptance tests, the RF tests and the pyro gates (below; armed steps with dummy loads) → the final SWD session (flight firmware image, hash recorded) → local clean → conformal coating of the pyro block, and of J2 over polyimide tape → cure → staking (J30/J31 included) → the static and leakage pyro gates, the NRST gate and the plug-out cold test again (cleaning and coating change the leakage they look for) → coat TP122 → the static gate once more → tap/modal check on the sled → U5 check against a reference barometer at least 7 days after reflow (TE's recovery time). If the flight firmware is not final when the pyro block is coated, cover and coat J2 after the last SWD session instead, then repeat the NRST gate and the static gate.
- **Conformal coating of the pyro block and J2 is mandatory** (REQUIREMENTS P3, MARGIN_PRESCRIPTION §4.13): roughly 13–22 kΩ of leakage from 8.4 V to a gate turns its FET on, and bare gate copper sits 0.79–1.74 mm from bare bus or 3V3 copper; J2's bare NRST pad sits 0.48 mm from its GND pad with only the MCU's 30–50 kΩ internal pull-up holding it, so a leak there holds the board in reset (no primary deployment, no servo pulses). Coat locally:
  - the pyro block on the top side: Q124, R126–R134, R173, R175, R178, R180, C126–C129, D120 and the copper between them;
  - TP122 on the bottom side, last: after the post-coating leakage reading, which probes it (it is the gate test pad); then repeat the static gate;
  - **J2, after the final SWD session before flight.** J2 is a bare TC2030 pad pattern (since R6; pads 1 3V3, 2 SWDIO, 3 NRST, 4 SWCLK, 5 GND) so that the probe can mate: until the last SWD session keep coating off its six pads and three locating holes, and clean it with the pyro block. Then clean it locally with isopropyl alcohol, cover all six pads and the three locating holes with polyimide tape, and brush the pyro-block acrylic over the tape and its edges. To re-flash later, use USB DFU (the firmware's jump to the STM32 system bootloader; BOOT0 is not on J2), or strip J2 locally (cut round the tape, peel it, swab the pads with isopropyl alcohol), then re-cover, re-coat and repeat the NRST gate and the pyro gates (they are repeated after any firmware change anyway).
  - **NRST gate** (after J2 is coated, and before each flight): NRST-to-GND leakage ≥ 1 MΩ. Read it powered (USB or the bench supply; MCU running or blank, not held in reset) with a 10 MΩ DMM: NRST at C24 pad 1 (bottom side) must read ≥ 0.97 × the 3V3 rail measured at the same time at C15 pad 1 beside it (≥ 3.2 V on a 3.3 V rail). With the 30–50 kΩ pull-up and the meter's own 10 MΩ, that ratio means at least about 1 MΩ to GND; a healthy board reads about 0.995 (Inference, typical STM32 NRST characteristics). Do not use an unpowered ohmmeter reading: it runs through the pull-up, the MCU's protection diodes and the 3V3 rail's loads. A low reading: strip J2, clean, re-coat and read again. The reading also covers NRST's other bare copper (C24, U1 pin 14), which is not coated.
  - Keep coating off the terminal entries and screws, the mating surfaces of J3, J9, J122 and J123, J2 until its last SWD session, U5's pressure port (the MS5611 top holes), LS120, every other test pad, **both RF modules (U30, U31; u-blox: coating voids the warranty) and the SMA jacks' threads and mating faces**, and U4 (the IMU) with about 3 mm around its outline on both faces: resin or epoxy near a MEMS sensor shifts its offsets (ST TN1383 §4).
  - Clean only locally before coating (a swab with isopropyl alcohol on the pyro block), never the whole board: U5's open port must not see solvent or an ultrasonic bath.
  - Use an acrylic conformal coating (for example MG Chemicals 419D or HumiSeal 1B31), brushed on.
- **Staking** (REQUIREMENTS P3, MARGIN_PRESCRIPTION R14), after the coating has cured: a fillet of flexible epoxy at the base of C84 (OS-CON) and C121 (on C121's west and north sides only: U4 and U5 lie under its body on the bottom), the three screw terminals, J122 and J123, never on or near U4. **J30/J31, after the RF bench tests:** a structural-epoxy fillet from the flange back face to the board edge and along the four GND legs on both faces, kept off the centre pad, the RF line and D30/D31, the threads and the mating face. U30 and U31 get no staking and no adhesive (0.5 g each; u-blox IM §5.2). Silicone, if used at all, only away from coated areas: it makes acrylic coating crater and delaminate, even where it never touched (DESIGN_ASSURANCE §6.3). Mated servo plugs get zip ties; the pull-pin plug (J123, no tie point nearby) gets a dab of epoxy or RTV.
- **Never probe R134 while the board is armed** (GND test points: TP88, TP74; J2 pad 5 until J2 is coated).
- **First power-up:** only after the J30/J31 centre-to-shell check above. USB first, then a current-limited bench supply, never a battery first (DESIGN_ASSURANCE). **GNSS antenna ESD rule:** connect or disconnect the GNSS antenna (or any lead on J30) only with the board unpowered, on an ESD-safe bench, and never hot-plug it in the field: D30 (ESD103-B1-02EL, shared with the radio port to save an extended code) clamps at 20–48 V at 1–16 A TLP, and u-blox gives U30's RF_IN only ±5.5 V DC and no ESD figure (`pass2_electrical.md` ELECTRICAL-04). **Whenever an SMA plug nut is turned** (VNA, 50 Ω load, antenna), counter-hold the jack's 6.5 mm square flange with thin flat-jaw pliers (jaw no thicker than the 1.5 mm flange, kept off the thread) and hold the plug body, so the board never reacts the torque; tighten to the makers' recommended mating torque: 0.8–1.13 N·m for the jack (Kinghelm KH-SMA-KE8-G datasheet p. 1, https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/35ad22a9bea57e53d493009a20c931ef.pdf?productCode=C530661), or the plug's (or VNA cable's, or load's) maker's recommended value if that is lower, for example 0.45 N·m (0.60 N·m maximum) for Amphenol's brass SMA series (Amphenol RF SMA cut sheet, "Brass SMA Specifications", https://www.tti.com/content/dam/ttiinc/manufacturers/amphenol/RF/Products/pdf/SMA-Cutsheet.pdf); use a calibrated SMA torque wrench (REQUIREMENTS P3). Never key the radio without an antenna or a 50 Ω load on J31 (the SX1262's 10:1 VSWR limit), and on USB power alone keep transmit power at ≤ +14 dBm (U10's current limit; `pass1_electrical.md` E9).
- **Pyro gates before any real igniter** (REQUIREMENTS F8), run with the flight firmware image (record its hash) and repeated after any firmware change: on a blank MCU the pins stay in their reset state, so the gates would prove only the pull-downs. For every armed step: a dummy load on the IGNITER 1 terminal J121; USB and the SWD probe unplugged (USB keeps the MCU powered, and either can tie the board to mains earth); the bench supply current-limited to 1 A or less (a 2S pack only for the commanded fire). **Never clip a scope or meter ground to an igniter terminal:** both ends of the load sit near RAW_ACT when armed, so an earthed ground clip shorts the channel through R134 (up to 4.1 A and 34 W, below the 5 A link fuse) while the scope reads about 0 V. Measure load current with a clamp-on current probe or a floating battery DMM, or load voltage with a differential probe rated for at least 10 V common mode.
  - Static (MARGIN_PRESCRIPTION P1-14): armed, a 10 Ω, ≥ 5 W dummy load and a floating DMM ammeter in series on the channel. The only current is the continuity current through the sense divider (2 × 5.1 kΩ over 2 × 2 kΩ in parallel: RAW_ACT / 11.2 kΩ, 0.54–0.75 mA): pass at RAW_ACT / 11.2 kΩ + 0.1 mA or less, with the MCU idle, held in reset (J2 pad 3 NRST to pad 5 GND; once J2 is coated, a short across C24's two pads, NRST to GND, on the bottom side) and, after switch-off, while RAW_ACT decays.
  - Leakage: armed, MCU held in reset, TP122 (bottom side) to GND with a 10 MΩ DMM reads about 0 mV; 5 mV or more means about 1.8 MΩ or less to an 8.4 V net: clean again. The static gate alone only sees leakage near the 13–22 kΩ that turns a FET on.
  - Dynamic: a clamp-on current probe (or a differential probe across the load) on a 1 Ω, ≥ 10 W dummy load, with a pulse-width trigger at 0.2 A for 10 µs or longer, which ignores the C128 charge spike at link insertion (about 0.3 µs): no trigger during 20 ARM-link insertions and removals with the MCU idle (DESIGN_ASSURANCE §6.3, M3_ACTUATOR_MERGE §4), 20 power-switch on/off cycles with the link in (RAW_ACT below 1 V between cycles), 20 reset hold/release cycles, and, armed with the MCU running, 20 camera on/off cycles, the buzzer sounding, a sweep of all four servo outputs and a held servo stall (what the board does on the pad after arming).
  - **RF immunity (REQUIREMENTS F10):** armed, the same dummy load and trigger, with the radio transmitting continuously at +22 dBm into its flight antenna and then into a 50 Ω load on J31: no trigger, and the continuity and ARM sense readings stay in range (FMEA I-11/V-19).
  - One commanded fire into a 1–2 Ω, ≥ 10 W dummy load from a 2S pack, measured the same way, to confirm the fire current (F7). USB and SWD are unplugged by then, so the fire needs a trigger without a host connection (for example a timed fire set up before unplugging).
  - If the power switch is the pad arming device: RAW_ACT dead with the switch open (FMEA H-6(b), T-1).
- **RF bench tests before any flight** (`docs/M3_R7.md`, `pass1_electrical.md` §6, `pass1_layout.md` L15):
  - With the radio held in reset by hardware: PD9 high-impedance (blank or reset MCU), RADIO_NRST reads below 0.66 V (R311 against the SX1262's internal pull-up and whatever the module adds).

  - VNA S21 between J30 and J31 at 915, 1176 and 1575 MHz, target ≤ −50 dB, before the keyed-transmitter test.
  - Coexistence: radio keyed continuously at full power into its antenna while GNSS C/N0 is logged; at least 39 dB isolation from the radio port to the GPS input (F10). Repeat with the buzzer sounding.
  - Supply: scope 3V3 and 3V3A at transmit key-up and during GNSS start-up (up to 100 mA inrush); the supply ramp must stay within the MAX-F10S's 25 µs/V–35 ms/V.
- **Tap or modal check on the sled** (REQUIREMENTS P2): the board on its flight sled with both coax leads fitted and the plugs carried by the sled (P3); excite it with a light tap and read the first mode with an external accelerometer (the on-board IMU's output rate and anti-alias filter limit what it can see). Record f1 against the M3_R7 estimate; a much lower value, or rocking driven by the plugs, goes back to the owner (a sixth support was declined on the strength of the estimate).
- **Bench checks before any flight** (`docs/M3_R4.md` §4): reverse-polarity hot-plug with the flight harness and a warm pack (scope U9 IN and OUT simultaneously: IN ≥ −15 V, OUT − IN ≤ 21 V at the −20 °C operating corner, falling input slew ≤ 10 V/µs; [TPS25947 Rev C §6.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf)), after measuring R166 four-wire (0.32–0.34 Ω) and the BAT_IN–GND capacitance at 1 kHz (about 60 µF), waiting 2 s after any unplug, and followed by 20 plug-ins and a second R166 reading; camera back-feed, servo behaviour without pulses and with its input against 11 kΩ to GND, the ARM link with the arm sense read, USB hot-plug from an always-on legacy source, and the plug-out cold test (both readings of the channel in the healthy band, which also proves the sense series resistors R168, R170 and R172 and their joints) repeated with the camera switched on, the buzzer sounding and the radio transmitting (a gate bridged to those nets turns its FET on only once they are on; with the link out the test sees it safely); C121 inspected and its capacitance and ESR measured (its maker publishes no datasheet, so its reflow rating is unknown); the crystal's drive level checked against NDK's 200 µW maximum for the NX3225GA (10 µW typical, ESR ≤ 50 Ω): OSC_IN at most about 2.7 Vpp with a ≤ 1 pF probe at worst-case ESR (an AN2867-style estimate, about 26 µW × Vpp², Inference), or measured with a current probe; a hot check that RAW_ACT stays below 0.1 V with the switch open at 70 °C or more (Q122 leakage against R120, M3_R4 §1j); and, destructively on one spare board, the channel held on into a dead short from an 8.4 V source able to give 5 A, recording whether R134 opens or overheats (M3_R4 §3).
