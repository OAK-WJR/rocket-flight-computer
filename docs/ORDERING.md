# Ordering M3-ASSY-R5 prototypes (JLCPCB)

**This is a bench-prototype order, not a flight release.** The first boards are for power-up, dummy-load fire tests and servo tests. No firmware exists for R5 yet (it needs a new board binding), and v0.8.1 must not run on R2–R5. What R5 changed and what is still open: `docs/M3_R5.md` (the circuit is R4's: `docs/M3_R4.md`). Requirements: `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`. Parts cost is about $55 per board at 5 boards; the all-in price (PCB, assembly, extended-part fees, shipping) is roughly twice that.

## 1. Regenerate and check

```sh
bash tools/check.sh                         # DRC (incl. the pyro spacing rules) / unconnected / parity / ERC = 0, audit OK
python3 m3_design/check_actuators.py        # safety contract
python3 tools/export_fab.py                 # Gerber zip, BOM, CPL (orientation-corrected)
python3 tools/jlc_stock.py --boards 5       # JLC stock right now, minimum purchases included
python3 tools/jlc_cost.py --boards 5        # parts + Standard PCBA feeder fees per board (REQUIREMENTS M3: <= $84.41; also <= 48 extended codes, R3's count)
```

`export_fab.py` must report both of these:
- `parts without LCSC code: none`
- `CPL rows without a verified JLC orientation correction: none`

`jlc_stock.py` must report `short: none`; a line listed as thin has less than twice the order in stock, so order promptly or reserve it in **Parts Manager**. Stock changes daily. On 2026-10-12 nothing was short; U4 was thin (5 in stock for 5 boards): $55.14 parts per board, 81 unique codes (47 Extended), $79.93 per board with Standard PCBA's $1.53 feeder fee per code; Standard's fixed fees for two-sided assembly are $83.96 per order (setup, stencils, fixtures; one side would be $50.19), and JLC may add a $14.93 handling fee. Economic would be $84.19 per board plus $9.71 per order, but cannot be used (section 4).

## 4. Assembly (PCBA)

- **Assembly side: both sides.** Top: 89 parts (80 SMD, 9 through-hole) and 2 test pads; bottom: 116 SMD parts and 22 test pads. Heavy and tall parts (C84, C121, the six terminals, J2, J3, J122, J123, LS120, the MCU) are on the top; the bottom carries only SMD parts and test pads (bare copper).
- **Service: Standard PCBA including through-hole assembly**, so JLC also fits the terminals and headers (the CPL contains every part, SMD and THT). JLC's capabilities page (https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities, read 2026-10-09) gives the reflow as 240 ± 5 °C for Standard and 255 ± 5 °C, not adjustable, for Economic. Three parts allow only a 250 °C peak: U5 (TE MS5611: 250 °C, 40 s max; TE's AN808 forbids hand soldering it, so it cannot be fitted later by hand), LS120 (Huaneng MLT-8530: 250 °C, ≤ 10 s above 245 °C) and C84 (Panasonic OS-CON SVPK, lead-free condition C: 250 °C). C121's maker publishes no datasheet. **Economic is not acceptable for this BOM** unless JLC confirms in writing that its parts peak at ≤ 250 °C with ≤ 10 s above 245 °C.
- **Quantity:** JLC assembles at least 2 boards; stock was checked for 5.
- **Edge rails and fiducials: added by JLCPCB** (Standard requires them); the rails carry JLC's own tooling holes. TH1 (44.8, 9.2), TH2 (1.4, 64.6) and TH3 (1.4, 90.0) mm, 1.152 mm NPTH with 0.148 mm mask expansion per JLC's tooling-hole article (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-add-tooling-holes-for-pcb-assembly-order), stay on the board for an Economic order; Standard does not need them.
- **Order remarks** (the notes field on the order):
  - Hand-finish the four J3 (USB-C) shell legs so solder fills the slots, and check hole fill on the full-connection through-hole pins: J122.2/3/5/6/8/9/11/12, J123.2, J2.1, J3 legs 1 and 4, J9.1/2, J1.1, J120.1 and J124.2. Solder the through-hole parts by hand or selective soldering, not wave: bottom-side parts sit 1.4–2 mm from several pins. Pin-in-paste alone fills an estimated 12–15 % of J3's slots.
  - Reflow the bottom side first and the top side second: C84 and LS120 (top) then see one reflow, and U5 (MS5611, bottom) two. All three are limited to a 250 °C peak; confirm with TE before ordering that the MS5611 takes two reflows (its datasheet and AN808 give a profile, not a count; parts qualified to J-STD-020 take three).
  - A 0.10 mm stencil is preferred: U11's 0.2 × 0.6 mm apertures release marginally at 0.12 mm (area ratio 0.63), and TI's U9/U11 stencil examples assume 0.10 mm.
  - No PCBA cleaning or washing at any stage, including after through-hole soldering (please confirm in writing): U5 (MS5611) has an open pressure port, and TE asks for no-clean paste without cleaning ("Cleaning might damage the sensor!").
  - Edge rails: tab-routed (mouse bites), no V-cut; tabs only at Gerber Y −69.6…−85 on the X = 0 edge (the edge with J3, J120 and J124; 5 mm clear of tooling holes TH2 and TH3) and Y −35…−46 on the X = 46 edge (J9, J121/J125 and J2); Y = 0 is the battery-terminal end with J1, and KiCad's y is −Y. That keeps every tab at least 5 mm from the edge MLCCs (C83, C91–C93, C72–C77), R166 (a 2512 resistor 0.47 mm from the left edge), C84, LED2 and the tooling holes, and clear of J3 and J2.
  - Use the supplied paste layer exactly; no aperture optimisation (JLC re-cuts apertures by default unless told: its stencil opening standard, updated 2025-04-24). The apertures follow the makers' stencil guidance (U4, U7, U9, U11, Q120/Q121, J3 pin-in-paste).
  - Vias that cannot be ink-plugged (within 0.35 mm of a pad): tent them; never open the mask over a via.
  - All six screw terminals have their wire entries facing the nearest board edge (arrows on the silkscreen under each body). EasyEDA's outline for the orange C430603 is drawn mirrored against its own 3D model; follow the model and our arrows.
- **Choose "confirm parts placement"**, and check JLC's preview before paying.
- **Orientation:** the CPL is corrected per LCSC code. `tools/jlc_orient.py` matches every footprint pad by pad against the LCSC/EasyEDA footprint (`tools/jlc_easyeda/pads.json` → `tools/jlc_corrections.json`).
  - **Screw-terminal body correction.** A symmetric pin row cannot show a 180° error, so the green DB128V terminals (J1, J9, C2915641) carry an explicit +180° correction (CPL 180° and 270°). EasyEDA's model, like the DORABO drawing, has the wire entry 5.20 mm from the pin row on its +y side. JLC's pin 1 then lands on our pin 4; a screw terminal does not care. The DB128L 2-position terminals (black C430601, orange C430603) need no correction: EasyEDA's models have the entry on the same side as ours.
  - Still confirm in JLC's **3D placement preview**:
    - **Every terminal's wire entry faces the nearest board edge:** J1 toward the aft edge; J120 and J124 toward the left edge; J9, J121 and J125 toward the right edge. Arrows under each body on the silkscreen show the entry side.
    - **Terminal colours:** J120 and J124 (ARM) black; J121 and J125 (igniters) orange; J1 and J9 green.
    - Polarity: D9, D120, D122, D123, LED1, LED2, C84, C121.
    - Pin 1: U1–U5, U7–U13 (there is no U6), Q120–Q125, D4, D121 (bottom side), J123 (the PH latch faces inboard).
    - J3: the USB-C receptacle overhangs the board edge by about 0.45–0.5 mm, as HRO's layout intends.
- **Expected DFM queries** (confirm them as deliberate):
  - Vias inside test pads: TP122 and TP123. They are test pads with no paste and no part.
  - J3's locating holes are 0.20 mm from the nearest copper, JLC's NPTH minimum, per HRO's recommended layout.
  - U11's eight 0.2 × 0.6 mm lands are below JLC's listed 0.25 mm minimum pad. They are TI's RUX0012A land, on ENIG.
  - The five M3 holes and the two tie holes are circles on the board outline, so JLC mills them (±0.2 mm) instead of drilling them; the tooling holes are in the NPTH drill file.

## 5. After delivery (JLC does none of this)

- **Cover U5's two pressure ports** with a small dot of polyimide tape before any of the steps below (TE asks for the opening to be protected from particles during assembly), and remove it only before the 7-day barometer comparison.
- **Inspect J3's four shell legs** from both sides; reflow by hand with extra solder wherever a slot is not filled.
- **Remove the edge rails** by nibbling the tabs with a cutter; never snap them (R166 sits 0.47 mm and C83 0.8 mm from the left edge, C74 and TP76 about 0.6 mm from the right edge). File the tab stubs flush with the board edge.
- **Order of the remaining steps:** bench acceptance tests and the pyro gates (below; armed steps with dummy loads) → local clean → conformal coating → cure → staking → the static and leakage pyro gates and the plug-out cold test again (cleaning and coating change the leakage they look for) → coat TP122/TP123 → the static gate once more → U5 check against a reference barometer at least 7 days after reflow (TE's recovery time).
- **Conformal coating of the pyro block and J2 is mandatory** (REQUIREMENTS P3, MARGIN_PRESCRIPTION §4.13): roughly 13–22 kΩ of leakage from 8.4 V to a gate turns its FET on, and bare gate copper sits 0.79–1.74 mm from bare bus or 3V3 copper. Coat locally:
  - the pyro block on the top side: Q124, Q125, R126–R145, R173–R176, R178, R180, R181, C126–C141, D120 and the copper between them;
  - TP122 and TP123 on the bottom side, last: after the post-coating leakage reading, which probes them (they are gate test pads); then repeat the static gate;
  - J2's solder joints and the board around them, on both faces: J2.4 carries NRST, whose 30–50 kΩ internal pull-up a leak to the GND pour would overcome and hold the board in reset. (Since pass 9 J2.6 is unconnected: BOOT0 no longer reaches J2.)
  - Keep coating off the terminal entries and screws, the mating surfaces of J2, J3, J122 and J123, U5's pressure port (the MS5611 top holes), LS120, every other test pad, and U4 (the IMU) with about 3 mm around its outline on both faces: resin or epoxy near a MEMS sensor shifts its offsets (ST TN1383 §4). On J2, coat only the pin joints and the board round them, not the pins above the header's body (the SWD probe mates there).
  - Clean only locally before coating (a swab with isopropyl alcohol on the pyro block and J2), never the whole board: U5's open port must not see solvent or an ultrasonic bath.
  - Use an acrylic conformal coating (for example MG Chemicals 419D or HumiSeal 1B31), brushed on.
- **Staking** (REQUIREMENTS P3, MARGIN_PRESCRIPTION R14), after the coating has cured: a fillet of flexible epoxy at the base of C84 (OS-CON) and C121 (on C121's west and north sides only: U4 and U5 lie under its body on the bottom), the six screw terminals, and J2, J122 and J123, never on or near U4. Silicone, if used at all, only away from coated areas: it makes acrylic coating crater and delaminate, even where it never touched (DESIGN_ASSURANCE §6.3). Mated servo plugs get zip ties; the pull-pin plug (J123, no tie point nearby) gets a dab of epoxy or RTV.
- **Never probe R134 or R135 while the board is armed** (GND test points: TP88, J2 pin 1).
- **First power-up:** USB first, then a current-limited bench supply, never a battery first (DESIGN_ASSURANCE).
- **Pyro gates before any real igniter** (REQUIREMENTS F8), run with the flight firmware image (record its hash) and repeated after any firmware change: on a blank MCU the pins stay in their reset state, so the gates would prove only the pull-downs. For every armed step: dummy loads on both igniter terminals; USB and the SWD probe unplugged (USB keeps the MCU powered, and either can tie the board to mains earth); the bench supply current-limited to 1 A or less (a 2S pack only for the commanded fire). **Never clip a scope or meter ground to an igniter terminal:** both ends of each load sit near RAW_ACT when armed, so an earthed ground clip shorts the channel through R134 (up to 4.1 A and 34 W, below the 5 A link fuse) while the scope reads about 0 V. Measure load current with a clamp-on current probe or a floating battery DMM, or load voltage with a differential probe rated for at least 10 V common mode.
  - Static (MARGIN_PRESCRIPTION P1-14): armed, a 10 Ω, ≥ 5 W dummy load and a floating DMM ammeter in series on each channel. The only current is the continuity current through the sense divider (2 × 5.1 kΩ over 2 × 2 kΩ in parallel: RAW_ACT / 11.2 kΩ, 0.54–0.75 mA): pass at RAW_ACT / 11.2 kΩ + 0.1 mA or less, with the MCU idle, held in reset (J2 pin 4 to pin 1) and, after switch-off, while RAW_ACT decays.
  - Leakage: armed, MCU held in reset, TP122 and TP123 (bottom side) to GND with a 10 MΩ DMM read about 0 mV; 5 mV or more means about 1.8 MΩ or less to an 8.4 V net: clean again. The static gate alone only sees leakage near the 13–22 kΩ that turns a FET on.
  - Dynamic: a clamp-on current probe (or a differential probe across the load) on each 1 Ω, ≥ 10 W dummy load, with a pulse-width trigger at 0.2 A for 10 µs or longer, which ignores the C128/C140 charge spike at link insertion (about 0.3 µs): no trigger during 20 ARM-link insertions and removals per channel with the MCU idle (DESIGN_ASSURANCE §6.3, M3_ACTUATOR_MERGE §4), 20 power-switch on/off cycles with the links in (RAW_ACT below 1 V between cycles), 20 reset hold/release cycles, and, armed with the MCU running, 20 camera on/off cycles, the buzzer sounding, and a sweep of all four servo outputs and a held servo stall (what the board does on the pad after arming).
  - One commanded fire per channel into a 1–2 Ω, ≥ 10 W dummy load from a 2S pack, measured the same way, to confirm the fire current (F7). USB and SWD are unplugged by then, so the fire needs a trigger without a host connection (for example a timed fire set up before unplugging).
  - If the power switch is the pad arming device: RAW_ACT dead with the switch open (FMEA H-6(b), T-1).
- **Bench checks before any flight** (`docs/M3_R4.md` §4): reverse-polarity hot-plug with the flight harness and a warm pack (scope on U9 IN), after measuring R166 four-wire (0.32–0.34 Ω) and the BAT_IN–GND capacitance at 1 kHz (about 60 µF), waiting 2 s after any unplug, and followed by 20 plug-ins and a second R166 reading; camera back-feed, servo behaviour without pulses and with its input against 11 kΩ to GND, one ARM link at a time with both arm senses read, USB hot-plug from an always-on legacy source, and the plug-out cold test (both readings of each channel in the healthy band, which also proves the sense series resistors R168–R172 and their joints) repeated with the camera switched on and the buzzer sounding (a gate bridged to those nets turns its FET on only once they are on; with the links out the test sees it safely); C121 inspected and its capacitance and ESR measured (its maker publishes no datasheet, so its reflow rating is unknown); the crystal's drive level checked against NDK's 200 µW maximum for the NX3225GA (10 µW typical, ESR ≤ 50 Ω): OSC_IN at most about 2.7 Vpp with a ≤ 1 pF probe at worst-case ESR (an AN2867-style estimate, about 26 µW × Vpp², Inference), or measured with a current probe; a hot check that RAW_ACT stays below 0.1 V with the switch open at 70 °C or more (Q122 leakage against R120, M3_R4 §1j); and, destructively on one spare board, a channel held on into a dead short from an 8.4 V source able to give 5 A, recording whether R134 opens or overheats (M3_R4 §3). For every pack change: ARM links out first, and after any unplug wait 2 s and see TP80 below 1 V before plugging a pack in.
