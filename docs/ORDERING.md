TP30 (SAFEBOOT) is a bare test pad. **What each land may take when fitted by hand on the bench** (never as a JLC line: each new code would be Extended, past REQUIREMENTS M3's 52-code limit):
  - **C303 (GNSS_RF_ANT): a capacitor only.** It sits on the 3.2 V antenna-bias node (L30's antenna end), so an inductor there shorts the bias: the limiter holds the current, but the antenna goes dark. Use a capacitor rated ≥ 6.3 V.
  - **C305 (GNSS_RF_IN): a capacitor only while R304 is 0 Ω**, for the same reason (R304 joins it to the bias node). It may take an inductor only once R304 is replaced by a series capacitor (U30's RF_IN is DC-blocked inside the module, u-blox DS note 18).
  - **C313 and C314 (radio): a capacitor or an inductor.** The radio line carries no DC (D31 is a bidirectional TVS); an 0402 inductor at C313 can serve as a DC bleed to GND for a DC-floating antenna (EBYTE manual §5.1 shows 330 nH at ANT; `pass2_electrical.md` §5–6).
  - **C304 (GNSS_ANT_GATE): a capacitor only, 1.0–2.2 nF**, 0402, X7R or C0G, ≥ 10 V (the node never exceeds VCC_RF, about 3.2 V), for example Samsung CL05B102KB5NNNC (1.0 nF, 50 V X7R, LCSC C14442) or CL05B222KB5NNNC (2.2 nF, LCSC C84703), the same series as C302; start with 1.0 nF, the value pass 2's loop model used (62° worst phase margin, `pass2_electrical.md` ELECTRICAL-01). Fit it only if the short test oscillates (section 5). Never an inductor or a short there: it holds Q30's gate at GND, so Q30 stays fully on, Q32 cannot lift the gate (no current limit) and LNA_EN cannot switch the antenna off; a resistor to GND weakens both in the same way.

- **Bench checks before any flight** (`docs/M3_R4.md` §4): reverse-polarity hot-plug with the flight harness and a warm pack (scope U9 IN and OUT simultaneously: IN ≥ −15 V, OUT − IN ≤ 21 V at the −20 °C operating corner, falling input slew ≤ 10 V/µs; [TPS25947 Rev C §6.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf); in R8 U9 is on the bottom at (41.5, 85.5), 27 mm from the input node at J1, so probe U9's IN pin (C88), not the input capacitors), after measuring R166 four-wire (0.32–0.34 Ω) and the BAT_IN–GND capacitance at 1 kHz (about 60 µF), waiting 2 s after any unplug, and followed by 20 plug-ins and a second R166 reading; camera back-feed, servo behaviour without pulses and with its input against 11 kΩ to GND, the ARM link with the arm sense read, USB hot-plug from an always-on legacy source, and the plug-out cold test (both readings of the channel in the healthy band, which also proves the sense series resistors R168, R170 and R172 and their joints) repeated with the camera switched on, the buzzer sounding and the radio transmitting (a gate bridged to those nets turns its FET on only once they are on; with the link out the test sees it safely); C121 inspected and its capacitance and ESR measured (its maker publishes no datasheet, so its reflow rating is unknown); the crystal's drive level checked against NDK's 200 µW maximum for the NX3225GA (10 µW typical, ESR ≤ 50 Ω): OSC_IN at most about 2.7 Vpp with a ≤ 1 pF probe at worst-case ESR (an AN2867-style estimate, about 26 µW × Vpp², Inference), or measured with a current probe; a hot check that RAW_ACT stays below 0.1 V with the switch open at 70 °C or more (Q122 leakage against R120, M3_R4 §1j); and, destructively on one spare board, the channel held on into a dead short from an 8.4 V source able to give 5 A, recording whether R134 opens or overheats (M3_R4 §3). For every pack change: ARM link out first, and after any unplug wait 2 s and see TP80 below 1 V before plugging a pack in.
