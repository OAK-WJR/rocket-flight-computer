## Single screw switch for power and arming (owner decision, 2026-10-28)

One external screw switch on J400 (U9 EN) replaces the separate ARM plug. J124, its 5 A fused link and its ARM / fuse legends are removed. The former PYRO1_BUS joins canonical RAW_ACT on board copper behind Q120 / Q121, never directly to the battery or RAW_PROTECTED. R134 2 Ω, Q124's gate network, the cold pull-up and bus / continuity sense remain.

PC1 (U1.16), formerly NC, drives ACT_EN_MCU through R122 1 kΩ to Q122, with R123 100 kΩ pull-down; there is no automatic RAW_PROTECTED divider enable. After safe boot, firmware keeps PC1 and PE4 low for the qualified passive cold-fault screen before deliberately enabling Q120 / Q121 for powered on-rail bus checks; PE4 separately drives Q124. Both controls are defined off through reset / unpowered. Firmware never fires before launch detected by accelerometer + barometer; on the ground bus / continuity readings are hard refusals and in flight they are logged / annunciated without vetoing recovery.

Switch on only on the pad / rail, on last and off first. The pyro channel is live whenever the board is on; **no physical break while powered** is owner-accepted. GPS / telemetry / IMU pad checks are on-rail. USB or a self-powered camera / GPS / radio can keep logic up with the switch open: connect the e-match only with no back-feed source attached. Bench work uses USB with the pack disconnected or the e-match disconnected; output tests use dummy loads and no e-match. No prelaunch firing self-test with a connected e-match.

The revised topology and required-fitted contracts pass the CAD gate. Revised sense thresholds, physical reset behavior and switch / harness qualification remain bench obligations. AUD-4 removes the advisory pull-pin J123; PC13 is NC / analog and PC14 retains the buzzer. Earlier named-board measurements below are historical until re-qualified.
