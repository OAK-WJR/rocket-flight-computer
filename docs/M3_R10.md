## Placement (`m3_design/r10_place.tsv`, D-R2-01, -03, -05, -07, -09, -12)

AVA's arrangement: U1 in the middle (23, 43) with the IMU U4 and barometer U5 beside it on the bottom; the screw
terminals J121 (IGN), J124 (ARM) and J1 (BAT) in a column on the west edge with wires entering from −x; the three GH
ports J410 (GPS), J420 (RADIO) and J9 (CAM) in a column on the east edge, each with its translator, switch and supply
parts in a cell beside it; J122 at the forward end; the 5.3 V buck and the pull-pin / buzzer in the aft band; the U9
eFuse cell, C84 and the input protection in the forward-east area; USB-C J3 and Tag-Connect J2 on the west edge. The MCU
core block (QSPI flash, crystal, IMU, barometer, decoupling) sits at R9 + (5, −1.5) so R9's hand-routed core tables
apply translated (one exception: C32, D-R2-12). Every row cites its decision; `ptool` and DRC report 0 placement
violations. Length is set by the placement, not chosen (owner priority: correctness, then clean routing).

## CH1 pyro (`m3_design/r10_route_pyro.py`, D-R2-02, D-R2-11)

Fire loop on F.Cu at 1.0 mm: J124.1 (BUS1) → R134 (2 Ω shorted-igniter limit) → J121.2 (IGN); J121.1 (OUT) → Q124's
drain; Q124's source bar with two GND vias. The pulse is at most 4.2 A for 20 ms (8.4 V / 2 Ω; firmware must end every
pulse by 20 ms). Continuity and ARM sense dividers on the bottom (2 × 5.1 kΩ in series over 2 × 2 kΩ in parallel, as R9)
with the 4.7 kΩ series resistors R168 / R170 / R172 at U1 (P-8). Gate network: R126 series from U1.3 (PE4), C126 and
R128 to the source; TP122 (the gate's bare test pad) on the bottom with one via and a GND partner 1.49 mm away. The load
switch Q120 / Q121 (bottom, sources facing) feeds RAW_ACT, which reaches only J124.2, TP120 and the RAW_ACT sense, so the
ARM plug is the only path to the bus (S-1, measured by copper connectivity, not net names). J124's two pads are ≥ 1.5 mm
apart on every layer (S-5). There is no pyro-specific spacing rule: the accepted single-fault loss is on the deviation
list.

## Power (`m3_design/r10_route_power.py`, D-R2-03, -05, -06, -11, -12)

- **Battery:** J1.1 → BAT_IN on F.Cu (1.0 mm) past D8 to the input-protection column C95 / C89 / C96 / C80 (x 29) and on
  B.Cu (1.0 mm) from Q120's drain row along y 86.6 to U9's IN bar (one 0.25 mm neck between its corner pads); C88 at
  U9.5, R167 bleed and R80 / R165 / J400 for the external switch (≈ 7 µA contact current, 470 kΩ each side). The damper
  R166 is turned north-south (one 45° BAT_DAMP run from C95.2).
- **Logic:** U9 (TPS25947 eFuse) → RAW_PROTECTED → U11 (logic / USB mux) → VLOGIC (C84 hold-up) → U2 3V3 buck (L1). The
  VLOGIC divider R60 / R61 / C60 feeds VLOGIC_SENSE on PA6 (ADC12_INP3) for F6's analog-watchdog load shedding.
- **Ports:** RAW_PROTECTED → U400 (AP63201, L400 6.8 µH, feedback R400 / R404 / R401) → PORT_5V3 → U402 → CAM_5V (J9.1);
  PORT_5V3 → U401 (TPS7A9101, R402 / R403, C408 NR/SS, R405 820 Ω bleed) → PORT_4V8 → U410 → GPS_4V8 (J410.1) and U420 →
  RADIO_4V8 (J420.1). Each switch has a 100 kΩ EN pull-down, an ILIM resistor (camera 22.6 kΩ, radio 66.5 kΩ, GPS
  100 kΩ) and a FAULT pull-up to 3V3. Set points and tolerance budgets: `parts_qualification.md` (calculations, not
  measurements).
- **In2 regions** (one 4-region split, D-R2-05 / D-R2-06): RAW_PROTECTED wraps the board (forward area, east strip
  x > 40, the aft band to the buck input); PORT_5V3 a column x 35.2–39.7 (y 7.3–42.5) plus the buck-output strip;
  PORT_4V8 x 28.3–32.4 (y 9.1–29); 3V3 the rest, with a forward finger for the 3V3 buck output. In1 is solid GND; F.Cu and
  B.Cu carry GND pours. Every rail pad reaches its plane by its own via (the frozen fan-out table
  `m3_design/r10_fanout.tsv`, with recorded hand edits) or a hand join; vias keep pads only on the layers they connect
  (D-R2-10). RADIO_4V8 crosses its cell on In2 north of the RX wire (one L of two segments).
- **Not done:** no DC solve or IPC-2152 estimate was run for R10's copper (R10 carries no 7.7 A path; the largest
  board currents are the 4.2 A fire pulse and the ≈ 1 A buck input). This is an open item, not a pass.

## Ports and signals (`m3_design/r10_route_signals.py`, `r10_route_usb.py`, D-R2-06 … D-R2-09)

Every signal is hand-drawn (owner decision D-R2-08); no autorouter output remains. The port UARTs leave U1 on the side of
their cells: GPS USART6 (PC6 TX, PC7 RX, AF7), radio UART5 (PB13 TX, PB12 RX, AF14; neither in AN2606's loader set),
camera UART7 (PE8 / PE7, as R8). The servo PWM lines SERVO1-4 (PA0–PA3, U1.22–25) run as one F.Cu bundle south to the
bottom servo cell (D121 clamp, R154–R157 pull-downs, R150–R153 1 kΩ straight into J122's signal row). USB and SWD are
R9's tables translated (`r10_route_usb.py`).

## Mechanical (fit_check / plate model, profile r10)

Supports (4, 4), (42, 4), (4, 44.3), (3.7, 86), (42, 86): bottom parts ≥ 3.5 mm (nearest C88, 3.84 mm), top hardware
≥ 3.8 mm (nearest J400, 3.86 mm). Bore Ø 50.8 mm fit: the GH ports mated have 0.03 mm margin (J410 / J420 / J9), J122
mated 0.50 mm; CAD envelopes only (the plugs' and wires' real shapes are not modelled). Plate model (masses.json:
conservative mass allowances, an **Inference**): first mode 276.4 Hz pinned (≥ 209 Hz). J122's tie channel clears every
bottom part by 2 mm (M-3). Wire room from the terminals' wire face to the bore wall 4.95 mm (R8 / R9 6.25 mm): owner
question OQ-1.

## Firmware

No R10 firmware exists; v0.8.1 must not run on R2–R10. The binding note
`m3_design/assembly_review/sources/r10_design/r10_binding_note.json` (regenerated by the build) records the board SHA-256
`883d2db5…`, the netlist, pin-table and circuit hashes, U1's 100-pin map checked against the board, U4's position and
rotation and the firmware obligations (port start / shutdown order, F6 shedding on the PA6 analog watchdog, PE4 low
before any reset or loader entry, fire pulses ≤ 20 ms, LR900 channels inside 902–928 MHz, no uplink path to arming). A
new binding must be made through `m3_firmware/assembly_binding.py` (AGENTS §3), never by editing a hash.
