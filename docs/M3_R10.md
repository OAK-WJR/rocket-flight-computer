# M3-ASSY-R10: the leaner board (AVA-informed), 46 × 90 mm

## Current state

This section is the one place that states where R10 stands. [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md),
[ORDERING.md section 8](ORDERING.md#8-m3-assy-r10-branch-m3-assy-r10-cad-candidate-not-accepted),
[R10_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md](R10_VEHICLE_HANDOFF.md) and [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md) summarize it in a few lines and
link here. When something changes, change it here first.

**Summary.** M3-ASSY-R10 is a CAD candidate on branch `m3-assy-r10`: a 46 × 90 mm board with one external screw
switch, eight DB125 screw terminals, an external GPS and radio, a spare AUX port, a hinged microSD socket and no
servo-power copper. It is not accepted and nothing is ordered. R9 (owner-accepted 2026-10-25) stays the accepted board
and R8 its fallback. No firmware exists, and no physical test has been done on a built R10 board. **The PCB changed in
the round 26 fix pass.** Rounds 22 and 23 (ten lenses each) were the closing pair on `5516eeec…`: 0 Critical / 0 Major.
The round 24 board pass (D-R24-01 … D-R24-17) changed it to `1407e850…`. Round 24's ten lenses found 0 Critical /
0 Major on that board and 24 Minor findings, which the triage reduced to 17 real errors (R24-TRI-01 … -17), 11
observations and none not reproducible. The round 24 fix pass (D-R24-18 … D-R24-23) then capped one BAT_FAULT_N
chamfer, making the PCB `92c3ad59…`, and fixed the generated text and checks. Round 25's ten lenses found 0 Critical /

## Single screw switch for power and arming (owner decision, 2026-10-28)

One external screw switch on J400 (U9 EN) replaces the separate ARM plug. J124, its 5 A fused link and its ARM / fuse legends are removed. The former PYRO1_BUS joins canonical RAW_ACT on board copper behind Q120 / Q121, never directly to the battery or RAW_PROTECTED. R134 2 Ω, Q124's gate network, the cold pull-up and bus / continuity sense remain.

PC1 (U1.16), formerly NC, drives ACT_EN_MCU through R122 1 kΩ to Q122, with an R123 2 kΩ pull-down (100 kΩ before the round 18 fix pass) and C123 10 nF from gate to source (round 19 fix pass, D-R19-04); there is no automatic RAW_PROTECTED divider enable. After safe boot, firmware keeps PC1 and PE4 low for the qualified passive cold-fault screen before deliberately enabling Q120 / Q121 for powered on-rail bus checks; PE4 separately drives Q124. Both controls are defined off through reset / unpowered. With PC1 high the Q122 gate is 2.19 V (1.92 V at the VOH floor); that Q122 saturates there, with at most 0.143 mA of drain load, is Inference, because AO3400A RDS(on) is specified only at 2.5 V. Firmware never fires before launch detected by accelerometer + barometer; on the ground bus / continuity readings are hard refusals and in flight they are logged / annunciated without vetoing recovery.

Switch on only on the pad / rail, on last and off first. The pyro channel is live whenever the board is on; **no physical break while powered** is owner-accepted. GPS / telemetry / IMU pad checks are on-rail. USB or a self-powered camera / GPS / radio / AUX module can keep logic up with the switch open: connect the e-match only with no back-feed source attached. The GPS, radio and AUX ports are fed from the battery path, so they are off on USB-only power; a module that has its own USB or battery supply is a back-feed source. Bench work uses USB with the pack disconnected or the e-match disconnected; output tests use dummy loads and no e-match. No prelaunch firing self-test with a connected e-match.

The revised topology and required-fitted contracts pass the CAD gate. Revised sense thresholds, physical reset behavior and switch / harness qualification remain bench obligations. ## Ports and signals (`m3_design/r10_route_signals.py`, `r10_route_usb.py`, D-R2-06 … D-R2-09)

Every signal is drawn by explicit generator polylines. Three identical port UARTs drive direct 3.3 V module UARTs
through the retained 1 kΩ resistors and ground-only ESD: J410 (GPS by default) on USART3 PD8 TX / PD9 RX, J420 (radio
by default) on UART5 PB13 TX / PB12 RX, and the spare AUX port J440 on USART6 PC6 TX / PC7 RX (D-R19-14, owner-approved
2026-10-30). J410, J420 and J440 are electrically identical, so the owner may wire the GPS or the radio to any of them;
firmware keeps a port map (default GPS J410, radio J420, AUX spare) and the silkscreen words show that default. The
camera port J9 is not interchangeable with them: it carries the 5.456 V camera supply, the reversed TX / RX pin order
and the isolated UART7. The camera keeps UART7, U12 / U13 isolation and U402 power control. On USB-only power the three
ports are unpowered, so PD8, PB13 and PC6 must not drive their TX lines then (see Firmware, "Ports and shedding"). Pin
conventions are board-relative: J9 pin 3 RXI / pin 4 TXO; J410 / J420 / J440 pin 3 TXO / pin 4 RXI. On those three,
board TXO (pin 3) is driven by the board: wire it to the module's RX, and wire the module's TX to board RXI (pin 4). J9
is the reverse: pin 3 is where the board receives (the camera's TX goes there) and pin 4 is where the board transmits.
All four terminals use supply pin 1 and GND pin 2. On J440 only 1:4V8 is printed on the top; 2:GND, 3:TXO and 4:RXI are
on the bottom beside their pins. The unkeyed harness is wired
from each printed pin legend, never by connector body alone.

SPI3 uses PC10 SCK / PC11 MISO / PC12 MOSI (AF6) and PD0 GPIO CS, with CS held high
through reset. The hinged Molex 472192001 socket has no card-detect switch; firmware
probes it without blocking primary QSPI logging. Socket and unused DAT pull-ups,
local bypasses and electrical / timing obligations are in circuit.json and the
binding note. J122 pins 1–4 carry the protected servo signals and pin 5 is the
reference GND; motor power and return remain in the harness.

checks it against the maker-derived pin functions and native circuit, and the
binding note records the generated board. Removed GPS/radio control pins, PB0 and PC3_C

supports and the charge fires in the adjacent compartment (first-principles reasoning, not measured; the parent `VEHICLE.md` gives no ejection g figure). Boost itself is only
28–37 g axial. The 500 g equivalent therefore sits between the hard-landing case and the GEVS bound. Round 18 questioned it as not rooted in the flights; it is kept as
an open requirement until the owner replaces it with a weighed or measured figure.

- **Round 23 firmware rules (root decisions; the binding note carries the same rules).**
  1. **Launch rule (R23-SAFETY-07).** The fire path needs LIFTOFF from the accelerometer AND a barometric altitude gain
     (LAUNCH_CORROBORATED), as AGENTS §2.1 requires. There is no inertial-only fire path on R10. With the barometer
     invalid, the primary channel does not fire and the independent Blue Jay covers recovery. FMEA F-6's
     accelerometer-only latch is not enough on R10.
  2. **Cold screen after every power-up or reset (R23-SAFETY-09).** Wait at least 1 s, and verify that RAW_ACT has
     discharged and ACT_GATE is off, before classifying. This covers both U9 variants.
  3. **QSPI_IO2 / PE4 bridge (R23-SAFETY-08 (1); verified Minor, `round23_safety_severity_check.md`).** Physics: Q124's
     gate capacitor is C126, 220 nF (C128, 100 nF, is on the drain). The gate time constant is about 47–58 µs over C126's ±10 % (53 µs nominal; Calculation, driver resistances Inference). A steady
     IO2-against-PE4 fight with the MCU driving IO2 high puts 1.19–1.98 V on the gate and fires the match after about 0.55 ms in all but one modelled
     corner. Page-program logging at
     a QSPI clock of 25 MHz or more stays at most 6 mA RMS, at least 50× below the 0.30 A no-fire (Calculation / Simulation; driver resistances, flash drive, the AON7524 sub-amp curve and the match thermal model are Inference); firing needs a
     sustained IO2-high pattern. **Ground refusal:** before PC1 goes high, run a QSPI quad read-verify of a known
     pattern and refuse on any mismatch (the bridge corrupts quad reads). **QSPI use while PC1 is high:** PE2 stays in
     its QSPI alternate function, never a GPIO driven high; no memory-mapped or parked quad reads; no long all-ones
     writes at a low clock; the flash output drive is not at 100 %. **Cold screen:** during part of the passive cold
     screen, run sustained QSPI traffic and toggle PC14 and the servo PWM; refuse if CONT or BUS moves out of band.
