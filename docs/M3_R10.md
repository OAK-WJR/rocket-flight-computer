## Single screw switch for power and arming (owner decision, 2026-10-28)

One external screw switch on J400 (U9 EN) replaces the separate ARM plug. J124, its 5 A fused link and its ARM / fuse legends are removed. The former PYRO1_BUS joins canonical RAW_ACT on board copper behind Q120 / Q121, never directly to the battery or RAW_PROTECTED. R134 2 Ω, Q124's gate network, the cold pull-up and bus / continuity sense remain.

PC1 (U1.16), formerly NC, drives ACT_EN_MCU through R122 1 kΩ to Q122, with R123 100 kΩ pull-down; there is no automatic RAW_PROTECTED divider enable. After safe boot, firmware keeps PC1 and PE4 low for the qualified passive cold-fault screen before deliberately enabling Q120 / Q121 for powered on-rail bus checks; PE4 separately drives Q124. Both controls are defined off through reset / unpowered. Firmware never fires before launch detected by accelerometer + barometer; on the ground bus / continuity readings are hard refusals and in flight they are logged / annunciated without vetoing recovery.

Switch on only on the pad / rail, on last and off first. The pyro channel is live whenever the board is on; **no physical break while powered** is owner-accepted. GPS / telemetry / IMU pad checks are on-rail. USB or a self-powered camera / GPS / radio can keep logic up with the switch open: connect the e-match only with no back-feed source attached. Bench work uses USB with the pack disconnected or the e-match disconnected; output tests use dummy loads and no e-match. No prelaunch firing self-test with a connected e-match.

The revised topology and required-fitted contracts pass the CAD gate. Revised sense thresholds, physical reset behavior and switch / harness qualification remain bench obligations. AUD-4 removes the advisory pull-pin J123; PC13 is NC / analog and PC14 retains the buzzer. Earlier named-board measurements below are historical until re-qualified.

## Firmware

No R10 firmware exists; v0.8.1 must not run on R2–R10. Build a new reviewed binding through
`m3_firmware/assembly_binding.py`, never by substituting a hash into the R1 chain. The generated evidence note
`sources/r10_design/r10_binding_note.json` binds board, netlist, circuit and pin table, checks U1's pin map and
U4's bottom/180° orientation, and carries the detailed firmware obligations. Its regeneration is evidence only.

- **Arming and recovery:** PC1 / ACT_EN_MCU and PE4 start low before GPIO setup, loader entry and fault shutdown.
  Keep both low for the qualified **PASSIVE cold-fault screen before PC1 is enabled**. After any prior enable,
  wait ≥1 s and verify RAW_ACT discharge / ACT_GATE off before classifying cold readings; the delay and thresholds
  require bench qualification. Any ground fault is a hard refusal before PC1 can go high. Only after that screen
  passes, enable PC1, keep PE4 low and wait ≥100 ms for gate settling before **POWERED bus checks**. Never fire before
  accelerometer + barometer launch detection. Ground bus/continuity readings are hard refusals; in flight they
  are logged and never veto recovery. Use live evidence before re-enabling recovery after a reset. AUD-4 removes
  the pull-pin witness; PC13 is NC / analog.
- **Timing (D-GAP-05):** normal physical current ≤20 ms including tail, timer/DMA termination and independent abort;
  IWDG prescaler ÷4 / reload239 =960 LSI cycles, nominal30 ms and28.571–32.653 ms over the specified RevV range.
  Refresh immediately before PE4 rises, never during firing, then complete a valid SAFETY pass / refresh within 5 ms
  after current is off. Guarded fault current ≤36 ms with intact gate/watchdog hardware. Qualify silicon revision,
  LSI range, reset-to-high-impedance and physical tail. The 100 Hz SAFETY task is independent of IMU progress;
  peripheral operations are bounded and a dead IMU degrades control without a reset loop. First servo output is
  neutral 1520 µs; fault-to-neutral-command target ≤50 ms. Physical servo response remains a bench item.
- **Ports and shedding:** assert MCU RX pull-ups (PC7/PB12/PE7) and idle-high TX before OE. OE stays low whenever
  its port is off; remove OE before EN. AUD-8 puts GPS/radio VCCB on main 3V3, so an erroneous OE can feed an off
  module through1 kΩ. Camera U13 remains. Use PA6 ADC1/2 VLOGIC_SENSE watchdog for shedding, with measured threshold,
  end-to-end latency, actual switch-current decay and retained load; do not credit the ports-off hold-up interval
  before load removal. A low PC8 is not proof that USB is absent (divider threshold requires about4.62 V VBUS at
  3.3 V VDD). The switch has no direct state sensor; sag / source observations do not prove that it opened when
  back-feed holds logic up. Receive UARTs by DMA/IDLE and bound framing-error storms; choose and verify the error
  threshold before firmware release.
- **Pins and debug:** never enable synchronous trace (PE2/PC1/PE4). Lock PC1 with GPIOC_LCKR and PE4 with GPIOE_LCKR
  after low-output setup, read back LCKK and each pin bit, refuse ground readiness on failure. Output data remains
  writable; loader preparation leaves both locked low. Never attach a trace-capable debugger with an e-match.
  Test the potential DBGMCU override with dummy loads. No-net pins, including sentinels, stay analog / never driven;
  explicitly put PA15 into analog without pull. Keep LSEON=0 because PC14 is the buzzer and VBAT is tied to3V3.
  ROM-loader entry is permitted only before IWDG starts, with every port EN/OE low. The pin table records each
  AN2606 loader-pin exception and its circuit containment.
- **Sensors, logging and radio:** PE1 takes EXTI1 with the recorded internal pull-down; follow the per-pin ADC
  instance/channel table (PA6 ADC12_INP3, PC3_C ADC3-only). IMU roll=−gyro_Y, lever arm x−6.0 mm / z−2.06 mm
  (z Inference), SPI≤10 MHz and ignore gyro for 40 ms after power-up. Set W25Q128JV QE before quad mode; pre-erase or
  use bounded erase-suspend slices (sector erase 400 ms maximum, suspend 20 µs), never block the safety timing in
  flight. LR900-F channels stay inside 902–915 MHz or a maker-confirmed legal hop set; uplink never arms/fires.
