**Summary.** M3-ASSY-R10 is a CAD candidate on branch `m3-assy-r10`: a 46 × 90 mm board with one external screw
switch, seven DB125 screw terminals, an external GPS and radio, a hinged microSD socket and no servo-power copper.
It is not accepted and nothing is ordered. R9 (owner-accepted 2026-10-25) stays the accepted board and R8 its
fallback. No firmware exists, and no physical test has been done on a built R10 board. **The PCB changed in the round 18
fix pass.** Rounds 16 to 18 reviewed the previous PCB `632a9ad3…`; the current PCB has had no review round, and rounds 19 and 20 will
review it. The gate pass that followed the board change altered checks and the fab package only, not the PCB.

## Single screw switch for power and arming (owner decision, 2026-10-28)

One external screw switch on J400 (U9 EN) replaces the separate ARM plug. J124, its 5 A fused link and its ARM / fuse legends are removed. The former PYRO1_BUS joins canonical RAW_ACT on board copper behind Q120 / Q121, never directly to the battery or RAW_PROTECTED. R134 2 Ω, Q124's gate network, the cold pull-up and bus / continuity sense remain.

PC1 (U1.16), formerly NC, drives ACT_EN_MCU through R122 1 kΩ to Q122, with an R123 2 kΩ pull-down (100 kΩ before the round 18 fix pass); there is no automatic RAW_PROTECTED divider enable. After safe boot, firmware keeps PC1 and PE4 low for the qualified passive cold-fault screen before deliberately enabling Q120 / Q121 for powered on-rail bus checks; PE4 separately drives Q124. Both controls are defined off through reset / unpowered. With PC1 high the Q122 gate is 2.19 V (1.92 V at the VOH floor); that Q122 saturates there, with at most 0.143 mA of drain load, is Inference, because AO3400A RDS(on) is specified only at 2.5 V. Firmware never fires before launch detected by accelerometer + barometer; on the ground bus / continuity readings are hard refusals and in flight they are logged / annunciated without vetoing recovery.

Switch on only on the pad / rail, on last and off first. The pyro channel is live whenever the board is on; **no physical break while powered** is owner-accepted. GPS / telemetry / IMU pad checks are on-rail. USB or a self-powered camera / GPS / radio can keep logic up with the switch open: connect the e-match only with no back-feed source attached. The GPS and radio ports are fed from the battery path, so they are off on USB-only power; a module that has its own USB or battery supply is a back-feed source. Bench work uses USB with the pack disconnected or the e-match disconnected; output tests use dummy loads and no e-match. No prelaunch firing self-test with a connected e-match.

The revised topology and required-fitted contracts pass the CAD gate. Revised sense thresholds, physical reset behavior and switch / harness qualification remain bench obligations. AUD-4 removes the advisory pull-pin J123; PC13 is NC / analog and PC14 retains the buzzer. Earlier named-board measurements below are historical until re-qualified.

- **Arming and recovery:** PC1 / ACT_EN_MCU and PE4 start low before GPIO setup, loader entry and fault shutdown.
