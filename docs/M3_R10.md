# M3-ASSY-R10: the leaner board (AVA-informed), 46 × 90 mm

## Current state

This section is the one place that states where R10 stands. [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md),
[ORDERING.md section 8](ORDERING.md#8-m3-assy-r10-branch-m3-assy-r10-cad-candidate-not-accepted),

link here. When something changes, change it here first.

**Summary.** M3-ASSY-R10 is a CAD candidate on branch `m3-assy-r10`: a 46 × 90 mm board with one external screw
switch, eight DB125 screw terminals, an external GPS and radio, a spare AUX port, a hinged microSD socket and no
servo-power copper. It is not accepted and nothing is ordered. R9 (owner-accepted 2026-10-25) stays the accepted board
and R8 its fallback. No firmware exists, and no physical test has been done on a built R10 board. **The PCB changed in
the round 19 fix pass.** Round 19 reviewed the previous PCB `a3144b15…` and found no Critical or Major issue. The
current PCB has had no review round; rounds 20 and 21 will review it.

## Single screw switch for power and arming (owner decision, 2026-10-28)

One external screw switch on J400 (U9 EN) replaces the separate ARM plug. J124, its 5 A fused link and its ARM / fuse legends are removed. The former PYRO1_BUS joins canonical RAW_ACT on board copper behind Q120 / Q121, never directly to the battery or RAW_PROTECTED. R134 2 Ω, Q124's gate network, the cold pull-up and bus / continuity sense remain.

PC1 (U1.16), formerly NC, drives ACT_EN_MCU through R122 1 kΩ to Q122, with an R123 2 kΩ pull-down (100 kΩ before the round 18 fix pass) and C123 10 nF from gate to source (round 19 fix pass, D-R19-04); there is no automatic RAW_PROTECTED divider enable. After safe boot, firmware keeps PC1 and PE4 low for the qualified passive cold-fault screen before deliberately enabling Q120 / Q121 for powered on-rail bus checks; PE4 separately drives Q124. Both controls are defined off through reset / unpowered. With PC1 high the Q122 gate is 2.19 V (1.92 V at the VOH floor); that Q122 saturates there, with at most 0.143 mA of drain load, is Inference, because AO3400A RDS(on) is specified only at 2.5 V. Firmware never fires before launch detected by accelerometer + barometer; on the ground bus / continuity readings are hard refusals and in flight they are logged / annunciated without vetoing recovery.

Switch on only on the pad / rail, on last and off first. The pyro channel is live whenever the board is on; **no physical break while powered** is owner-accepted. GPS / telemetry / IMU pad checks are on-rail. USB or a self-powered camera / GPS / radio can keep logic up with the switch open: connect the e-match only with no back-feed source attached. The GPS, radio and AUX ports are fed from the battery path, so they are off on USB-only power; a module that has its own USB or battery supply is a back-feed source. Bench work uses USB with the pack disconnected or the e-match disconnected; output tests use dummy loads and no e-match. No prelaunch firing self-test with a connected e-match.

The revised topology and required-fitted contracts pass the CAD gate. Revised sense thresholds, physical reset behavior and switch / harness qualification remain bench obligations. AUD-4 removes the advisory pull-pin J123; PC13 is NC / analog and PC14 retains the buzzer. Earlier named-board measurements below are historical until re-qualified.

## Ports and signals (`m3_design/r10_route_signals.py`, `r10_route_usb.py`, D-R2-06 … D-R2-09)

by default) on UART5 PB13 TX / PB12 RX, and the spare AUX port J440 on USART6 PC6 TX / PC7 RX (D-R19-14, owner-approved

firmware keeps a port map (default GPS J410, radio J420, AUX spare) and the silkscreen words show that default. The

board TXO (pin 3) is driven by the board: wire it to the module's RX, and wire the module's TX to board RXI (pin 4). J9
is the reverse: pin 3 is where the board receives (the camera's TX goes there) and pin 4 is where the board transmits.
All four terminals use supply pin 1 and GND pin 2. On J440 only 1:4V8 is printed on the top; 2:GND, 3:TXO and 4:RXI are
on the bottom beside their pins. The unkeyed harness is wired

through reset. The hinged Molex 472192001 socket has no card-detect switch; firmware

## Firmware

No R10 firmware exists; v0.8.1 must not run on R2–R10. Build a new reviewed binding through
`m3_firmware/assembly_binding.py`, never by substituting a hash into the R1 chain. The generated evidence note
`sources/r10_design/r10_binding_note.json` binds board, netlist, circuit and pin table, checks U1's pin map and
U4's bottom/180° orientation, and carries the detailed firmware obligations. Its regeneration is evidence only.

- **Arming and recovery:** PC1 / ACT_EN_MCU and PE4 start low before GPIO setup, loader entry and fault shutdown.
  Keep both low for the qualified **PASSIVE cold-fault screen before PC1 is enabled**. After any prior enable,
  wait ≥ 1 s and verify RAW_ACT discharge / ACT_GATE off before classifying cold readings; the delay and thresholds
  require bench qualification. Any ground fault is a hard refusal before PC1 can go high. Only after that screen
  passes, enable PC1, keep PE4 low and wait ≥ 100 ms for gate settling before **POWERED bus checks** (worst-case settled |VGS| is 4.74 V and the time to −4.5 V 31.9 ms, Calculation). Never fire before
  accelerometer + barometer launch detection. Ground bus/continuity readings are hard refusals; in flight they
  are logged and never veto recovery. Use live evidence before re-enabling recovery after a reset. AUD-4 removes
  the pull-pin witness; PC13 is NC / analog. **PE4 is an active-high fire command** (PE4 high turns Q124 on): write ODR low before switching the pin to output mode and
  lock it with GPIOE_LCKR (R18-MCU-03). **Never enable a pull-up, RTC_TAMP3 or the PWR wakeup function on PC1** (R18-MCU-01): with the former R123 = 100 kΩ an enabled
  30–50 kΩ internal pull-up would have held Q122's gate at 2.19–2.52 V, above its 1.45 V maximum threshold, and switched the pyro bus on (PE4's 1 kΩ at the pin does hold
  off). R123 is now 2 kΩ, which keeps the gate at or below 0.218 V against a 30 kΩ pull-up at 3.6 V (3.6 V × 2 / 33); C123 10 nF now also sits from gate to source (D-R19-04); the rule stays. Whether the RTC_TAMP3 and wakeup functions apply a pull is an Inference (RM0433 not re-read). Firing would still need PE4.
  *Option for the owner (R18-SAF-06, not adopted):* after the on-rail powered bus check, firmware could return PC1 low and re-enable it only on confirmed launch
  detection, optionally after a walk-away delay, so that both series FETs stay off while people are near the vehicle. Q120 and Q121 turn on within a few ms (R121 × C120 = 10 ms
  time constant), well before apogee. The accepted single-switch design leaves the bus enabled from the on-rail check until the switch is opened.
- **Timing (D-GAP-05):** normal physical current ≤ 20 ms including tail, timer/DMA termination and independent abort;
  IWDG prescaler ÷4 / reload 239 = 960 LSI cycles, nominal 30 ms and 28.571–32.653 ms over the specified RevV range.
  Refresh immediately before PE4 rises, never during firing, then complete a valid SAFETY pass / refresh within 5 ms
  after current is off. Guarded fault current ≤ 36 ms with intact gate/watchdog hardware. Qualify silicon revision,
  LSI range, reset-to-high-impedance and physical tail. The 100 Hz SAFETY task is independent of IMU progress;
  peripheral operations are bounded and a dead IMU degrades control without a reset loop. First servo output is
  neutral 1520 µs; fault-to-neutral-command target ≤ 50 ms. Physical servo response remains a bench item.
- **Port map (firmware obligation, D-R19-14):** J410, J420 and J440 are electrically identical, so the GPS or the radio
  may be wired to any of them. Firmware keeps a port map from device to port UART: J410 = USART3 (PD8 TX / PD9 RX),
  J420 = UART5 (PB13 TX / PB12 RX), J440 = USART6 (PC6 TX / PC7 RX). The default is GPS on J410, radio on J420 and AUX
  spare, matching the silkscreen words. The camera port J9 is never in the map (5.456 V supply, reversed TX / RX
  order, isolated UART7). A module on AUX may draw at most 100 mA at 4.8 V beyond the GPS 150 mA and radio 300 mA.
- **Ports and shedding:** the three port UARTs have no EN/OE/FAULT GPIOs and are powered whenever the battery path is on (not on USB-only power, and not during hold-up after the
  switch opens). **While they are unpowered, keep PD8, PB13 and PC6 (the three TX pins) analog with the UARTs disabled** (R18-MCU-02): an idle-high TX would push about 2.7 mA
  through 1 kΩ into an unpowered module's RX pin, against u-blox's guidance (MAX-M10S integration manual UBX-20053088 R05, section 3.6.3.1, p. 46: do not drive the PIOs while V_IO is unsupplied;

Wait a settling delay after PA4 goes low. Park PD8, PB13 and PC6 in analog mode with no pull when PA4 rises or the PA6 shedding trigger fires (the same parking mode everywhere). The ports stay powered whenever the battery path is on; firmware cannot switch them. Once the ports are powered, set TX idle-high and configure FT digital RX with pulls disabled for any 5 V endpoint;
  no self-powered modules with the board off. Camera only: EN high, verify settling,
  RX pull-up/TX idle-high, then OE; disable OE before EN. PA6 ADC1/2 watchdog stops
  SD/QSPI writes and UART activity without blocking flush, lowers camera/clock/LEDs/
  buzzer while retaining sensors, state and safety timing. At 300.8 µF all-port/SD-write
  ride-through to 3.8 V is 0.464–0.539 ms; camera-off/logic-shed/card-idle with the GPS, radio and AUX allowance
  still on 1.304–1.412 ms (Calc/Inference). Qualify threshold, latency and actual load
  decay. A low PC8 does not prove USB absent; J400 has no direct sensor and back-feed
