Target `f2b8251`, board `1407e8506efbac0f664b3aeb65568c32ae9670c9b45b502a465cba1130de522e` (same SHA-256 before and
after). Seeds were made only on copies in `.supervisor/r24_gate/` (scripts, seeds, outputs). Full data:

**Totals:** 0 Critical, 0 Major, 3 Minor.

## Findings

### R24-GATE-01 (gate, Minor): right_angle misses a square-cornered bend on non-critical nets

- **The miss:** a bend whose 45° chamfer is cut shorter than (2 − √2) × half the track width per axis still has a sharp
  90° inner corner in its copper. That is 0.0586 mm on a 0.2 mm track. route_check fails such a bend only on critical
  nets, and there only as `short_segment`.
- **Seed D2b:** 0.05 mm cuts (0.071 mm diagonals) on three 0.2 mm bends:
  - SERVO2_MCU, B.Cu, at (30.55, 71.85);
  - BAT_FAULT_N, F.Cu, at (16.55, 83.35);
  - NRST, B.Cu, at (22.54, 36.239).
- **Corner probe:** at each bend, a point 2 µm inside the inner corner is copper and a point 2 µm into the notch is not.
- **Checker output on the seed:**
  - route_check: 0 failing, `right_angle` 0, exit 0. The three cuts appear only as ungated leftovers (10 unrecorded,
    against 5 on the board).
  - DRC: 0 violations.
  - check_r10: only the hash bindings fail (K-binding and F9-1).
- **Control:** the same cut on SD_SCK, and a 0.4 mm cut on the 1.0 mm BAT_IN, fail as `short_segment`.
- **Rule broken:** REQUIREMENTS M2 says the gate fails any 90° bend "on every net". The generator's own floor is a
  chamfer of at least max(width, 0.2 mm) (D-R24-17).
- **Fix:** add the short-chamfer case to `right_angle` on every net, and add a seeded test for it.

### R24-GATE-02 (gate, Minor): nothing checks the 2.5 mm short-edge clearance

- **Seed F1:** R92 goes back to F(31, 87.5) and TP88 to F(35.5, 87.0), with their tracks and GND vias, and the zones are
  refilled. Their courtyards are then about 1.72 mm and 1.95 mm from the forward edge.
- **Checker output on the seed:**
  - DRC: 0 / 0 / 0.
  - route_check: 0 failing.
  - fit_check: OK.
  - check_r10: only K-binding and F9-1 fail.
- **Why F9-1 does not catch it:** both K-binding and F9-1 are regenerated by the build. That F9-1 would then pass is an
  Inference: board `5516eeec…` had R92 at this same spot and passed the 2304-case model.
- **Rule broken:** JLC assembly terms §2.3, as cited in ORDERING 8.1. The records accept only C95, R166, R400, R401, R404
  and C403 as short-edge DFM queries. On the board those six are exactly the parts under 2.5 mm.
- **Fix:** add a courtyard-to-short-edge rule, with an exact SHORT_EDGE_QUERIES list in which stale or grown entries
  fail.

### R24-GATE-03 (doc, Minor): BOOT0's second via cites the wrong decision

- **Where:** the BOOT0 second via is labelled D-R24-05 in five places:
  - the `route_check.py:342` PARALLEL_VIAS reason;
  - the `r10_route_core.py:108` docstring;
  - the `check_r10.py:3038` comment;
  - `test_r10_check.py` lines 4466 and 4934.
- **The error:** D-R24-05 is U9's EN pull-down kept on one via. BOOT0's second via is D-R24-03, as `docs/M3_R10.md` and
  R10_VALIDATION say.
- **Fix:** change the label to D-R24-03 in all five places.
