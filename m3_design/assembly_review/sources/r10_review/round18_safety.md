# Round 18 safety review (Claude)

Target commit `dcbb6c8`, PCB `632a9ad36c19426ede219b6ff6b0d638955665cac1b4704d3392a6afdbe03982` (unchanged before and after). **0 Critical / 0 Major / 6 Minor.** No inadvertent-fire path was found that needs fewer than two independent faults. Every finding below concerns the robustness of the first series barrier (the Q120/Q121 bus switch), which the R10 single-switch decision depends on, or how its faults are recorded.

## Method

I traced the native board and the kicad-cli 10.0.7 netlist export (0 pad-net differences over 660 pads): J1 BAT_IN -> Q120 (D) -> ACT_FET_S -> Q121 -> RAW_ACT -> R134 2 ohm -> J121.2. J121.1 -> Q124 -> GND. PC1 -> R122 1 k / R123 100 k -> Q122 -> R121 10 k -> ACT_GATE (R120 100 k, C120 1 uF to ACT_FET_S). PE4 -> R126 220 ohm with R127 1 k and R128 10 k / C126 220 nF. The J400 switch only drives U9 EN/UVLO (R80/R165 470 k, R81 300 k). Evidence comes from native DRC on a copy (0 errors), exact copper gaps from the repo's own `copper_nodes`/`min_gap` helpers, an inner-barrel scan, the repo `s8()` run as shipped and with extended barrels, two seeded via-open copies through KiCad connectivity, a BOM/CPL cross-check and maker datasheets (AOS AONR21357/AON7524/AO3400A, TI TPS25947/TPS2553/TPS7A91/TPS2121/TXU0202, ST DS12110).

## Findings

### R18-SAF-01 (Minor, new): Load-switch gate (ACT_GATE) barrels are 0.2505 mm from In1 GND and In2 RAW_PROTECTED and sit outside S-8; one inner-layer defect or leak makes the pyro bus live while the switch is open

- **Where:** ACT_GATE vias (0.5 mm pad / 0.3 mm drill, no inner pads): (16.200, 73.300) and (11.800, 70.725), F.Cu-B.Cu
- **Measured:** Drill wall to fill = 0.2505 mm at both vias on both inner layers (pcbnew SquaredDistance minus drill radius; also native DRC hole_clearance 0.2505 mm and repo s8 helpers 0.251 mm). The repo's own S-8 algorithm (tools/check_r10.py s8, imported read-only) passes as shipped (4 barrels: RAW_ACT, PYRO1_IGN, PYRO1_OUT, PYRO1_GATE). With ACT_GATE added to BARREL_NETS it reports 2 violations: 'RAW_PROTECTED copper on In2.Cu is 0.251 mm from the via (11.800, 70.725) / (16.200, 73.300) barrel of ACT_GATE (minimum 1.0 mm)'. In1 GND is also 0.251 mm away, and S-8 exempts GND. R10_GND_In2_CH1 now ends at y = 66.1 (outline (0.3,51.8)...(16.5,66.1)); the old round-6 bay that kept about 1.1 mm beside the ACT_GATE via is gone.
- **Circuit:** Schematic netlist (kicad-cli 10.0.7 export; 0 differences against the 660 native board pads): Q120 D = BAT_IN (J1.1), S = ACT_FET_S; Q121 S = ACT_FET_S, D = RAW_ACT -> R134 2 ohm -> J121.2 IGN. The only off-bias is R120 100 k (ACT_GATE to ACT_FET_S) with C120 1 uF. With J400 open, U9 is off and R92 3.3 k (RAW_PROTECTED to GND) holds RAW_PROTECTED at about 0 V. ACT_FET_S rises through Q120's body diode to about BAT_IN - Vf. A short from ACT_GATE to RAW_PROTECTED therefore gives ACT_GATE about 0.25 V (about 75 uA x 3.3 k) and VGS about -7.4 V. Both AONR21357 are fully on (VGS(th) -1.3 to -2.3 V) and RAW_ACT is about BAT_IN. When J400 is closed, RAW_PROTECTED is about BAT_IN, VGS is about +0.2 V, the pair is held off and PC1 can no longer enable it. A short to In1 GND turns the pair on in every switch state. A resistive leak of about 0.20-0.52 Mohm to any node near 0 V already reaches |VGS(th)| against R120 100 k (Calc: R_L <= 100k x (V/|Vth| - 1), V = 7.0-8.0 V).
- **Same class on the switch EN chain:** BAT_ENABLE vias (19.675, 66.400), (21.300, 68.900), (17.100, 82.800) and the J400.2 (BAT_SW_RETURN) In2 annular ring are each 0.2505-0.251 mm from In2 RAW_PROTECTED. A leak of <= about 1.8-2.0 Mohm into EN (<= about 1.6 Mohm into BAT_SW_RETURN) holds U9 EN/UVLO above its falling threshold against R81 300 k, so the arming switch cannot turn the board off (Calc with TPS25947 VUVLO 1.183-1.223 V and the sheet's 4.45-4.61 V off band).
- **Records:** Current R10_VALIDATION 'R10 deviations for owner acceptance' section 1 names only the four S-8 barrels and says 'GND is permitted'; it does not mention the ACT_GATE barrels. The historical round-10 table (R10_VALIDATION line ~1912) lists 'ACT_GATE vias (16.2,72.3) / (11.8,69) ... 0.2505 / 0.8055 mm ... positive bridge biases the P-FET pair toward off'. Its coordinates and second gap are stale, and its rationale is wrong for the switch-open state. M3_R10 line ~185 keeps the 1.1 mm bay as history.
- **Impact:** In the normal integration and handling state (pack connected, J400 open, e-match in J121), one latent inner-layer defect at these 0.25 mm gaps puts pack voltage on the e-match's IGN leg. Q124's gate pull-downs are then the only barrier, which is the commercial-altimeter level the single-switch decision said R10 exceeds with 'two hardware FETs in series'. The RAW_PROTECTED variant inverts the switch: the bus is live with the switch off and dead with it on. Firmware sees this only as a failure to enable at the PC1 step, never as 'bus live'. Inner copper cannot be inspected, coated or reworked, and a fabrication open/short test (if done) does not screen later leakage or CAF growth (Inference). Firing still needs a second independent fault on the Q124 side, so this is not a single-fault fire.
- **Fix:** Clear In1.Cu and In2.Cu copper to >= 1.0 mm from both ACT_GATE barrels: no-copper rule-area circles of radius 1.15 mm centred on (16.200, 73.300) and (11.800, 70.725) on both inner layers, then refill. Add 'ACT_GATE' to tools/check_r10.py BARREL_NETS with GND treated as foreign for that net (GND is not benign for a P-FET gate). Alternatively, move the layer change onto ACT_EN by placing Q122 and R121 on B.Cu, so that an inner short to GND or to 0 V only holds Q122 off. Optionally give the three BAT_ENABLE vias an In2 RAW_PROTECTED void too (In1 GND is fail-safe for EN). Either way, list the residual in deviation section 1.

### R18-SAF-02 (Minor, new): Q121's gate (the forward-blocking FET) reaches its off-bias R120 and gate-source capacitor C120 only through two single vias and F.Cu at R121.1; one via or track open floats it

- **Where:** ACT_GATE copper: B.Cu island A = Q121.4 (12.525,70.000), via (11.800,70.725), TP121 (10.500,71.500). B.Cu island B = Q120.4 (14.475,72.200), via (16.200,73.300), C120.2 (17.100,73.300), R120.2 (18.500,73.030). The islands join only through F.Cu (11.800,70.725)-(13.375,72.300)-(14.500,72.300)[R121.1]-(15.200,72.300)-(16.200,73.300), all 0.3 mm wide. The ACT_FET_S source bridge at y = 71.1 between the two packages prevents a B.Cu link.
- **Measured:** KiCad connectivity (BuildConnectivity / GetConnectedItems) on the native board gives one island [C120.2, Q120.4, Q121.4, R120.2, R121.1, TP121.1]. With via (16.200,73.300) removed: [Q121.4, R121.1, TP121.1] | [C120.2, Q120.4, R120.2]. With via (11.800,70.725) removed: [C120.2, Q120.4, R120.2, R121.1] | [Q121.4, TP121.1].
- **Circuit:** Q120's body diode conducts BAT_IN to ACT_FET_S, so Q121 alone blocks the forward path to RAW_ACT and J121.2. If via (16.2,73.3) opens, Q121's gate keeps only R121 10 k to Q122's drain, whose IDSS (<= 1 uA at 25 C, 5 uA at 55 C) drains it toward 0 V while VGS heads to about -7.7 V, so Q121 turns on. If via (11.8,70.725) opens, the gate floats on Cgs/Cgd division (Ciss 2830 pF, Crss 365 pF) and IGSS (+/-100 nA). RAW_ACT then reaches about BAT_IN - Vf with the switch open (Inference for the floating case). S-3 is net-level ('a resistor from the gate (ACT_GATE) to the source (ACT_FET_S)'), so it cannot see this.
- **Impact:** A single open in a 0.3 mm via barrel or a 0.3 mm F.Cu segment removes Q121's off-bias and gate capacitor. RAW_ACT can then go live while the board is off, leaving Q124 as the only barrier, as in R18-SAF-01. Q124's network has redundant pull-downs (R127 1 k at the pin, R128 10 k at the gate) and a via-free gate cell. The P-FET pair's off-state rests on non-redundant copper.
- **Fix:** Give Q121 its own copper-local off-bias: a second 100 k 0402 (0402WGF1003TCE / C25741, as R120) on B.Cu from the Q121.4/TP121 island to Q121's ACT_FET_S source pins. Then any single via or track open leaves both gates biased off. Extend S-3 to require a same-layer, via-free gate-source resistor path for each P-FET. A weaker alternative is to double each ACT_GATE layer change with a second via (combine with the R18-SAF-01 voids).

### R18-SAF-03 (Minor, new): Bus-enable chain PC1 -> R122/R123 -> Q122 is high-impedance and non-redundant and runs beside reset-high nets; AGENTS 2.4 does not say whether Q122 is a 'pyro FET'

- **Where:** ACT_EN_MCU F.Cu (26.500,53.600)-(26.500,66.000) runs parallel to BAT_FAULT_N F.Cu (26.900,52.450)-(26.900,66.125) for 12.4 mm. BAT_FAULT_N is pulled up by R89 10 k to 3V3 and driven by U9.4 FLT_N (open drain) and PA5. The ACT_EN_MCU diagonal (24.5,51.6)-(26.5,53.6) passes the V3V3_SENSE via (25.500,51.750); V3V3_SENSE is R62/R63 10 k/10 k, 1.65 V.
- **Measured:** Exact gaps with the repo's min_gap helper: ACT_EN_MCU to BAT_FAULT_N 0.171 mm minimum (diagonals near (25.3,67.2)), 0.200 mm along the 12.4 mm parallel run; to the V3V3_SENSE via 0.251 mm; U1.16 (PC1) 0.200 mm from U1.15 (PC0, PYRO1_CONT) and U1.17 (NC). Q122's gate has only R123 100 k to GND and R122 1 k series, with no gate-source capacitor. Compare PE4 (U1.3): neighbours U1.2 and U1.4 left NC as sentinels, R127 1 k at the pin, R128 10 k and C126 220 nF at the gate.
- **Circuit:** Calc: a BAT_FAULT_N bridge puts ACT_EN at 3.3 x 100/(10+1+100) = 2.97 V whenever PC1 is not driven, i.e. in analog mode during boot, after any reset, during debugger reset or halt and in the ROM loader. That turns on Q122 (VGS(th) 0.65-1.45 V) and with it Q120/Q121. A leak of <= about 397 kohm reaches the 0.65 V minimum threshold. A V3V3_SENSE bridge gives 1.65 x 100/106 = 1.56 V. With PC1 driven low the bus is off, so the PC1-low passive cold screen cannot see these faults; at most PA5 reads FLT low or V3V3_SENSE reads 0. An open R123 leaves Q122's gate floating whenever PC1 is not driven; the hot-plug Miller kick through Crss is about 0.6 V at datasheet 15 V capacitances (more at low VDS), against a 0.65 V minimum threshold (Inference).
- **Impact:** One copper/contamination bridge, or one open 0402, defeats the 'defined off in reset' property of the bus barrier. The fire FET still holds, so this is not a fire path. It is still weaker than the PE4 side the decisions rely on, and invisible to the specified ground screen. AGENTS 2.4's capacitor requirement is met at the P-FET gate (C120 with R121, 10 ms), which also absorbs short Q122 glitches. The rule does not say whether Q122 itself must comply.
- **Fix:** Route ACT_EN_MCU >= 0.5 mm from BAT_FAULT_N and V3V3_SENSE, or put a GND guard between them (a short to GND is fail-safe for this net). Consider a stiffer, redundant Q122 bias (R123 about 10 k plus a second pull-down, or a small gate-source capacitor at Q122). State in AGENTS/REQUIREMENTS whether Q122 counts as a pyro FET under 2.4.

### R18-SAF-04 (Minor, new): Single surface bridges silently bypass R134 and/or the e-match; the R134 bypass turns a fire command into a near pack short

- **Where:** B.Cu PYRO1_OUT sense branch (0.2 mm) segment (17.000,61.800)-(20.830,61.800) runs in a 0.625 mm corridor between R134.2 (RAW_ACT, pad y 62.150-65.850, x 17.850-20.300) and Q123.1 (BUZZ_G, pad y 60.875-61.525, x 18.0-19.0). The IGN 0.5 mm track (7.400,59.400)-(12.000,64.000) passes R131.1 (PYRO1_OUT).
- **Measured:** Exact gaps (repo min_gap): OUT to R134.2 (RAW_ACT) 0.250 mm over 2.45 mm; OUT to Q123.1 (BUZZ_G) 0.175 mm; IGN to R131.1 (OUT) 0.222 mm. Other current OUT neighbours: GND via (15.85,57.70) 0.177 mm (B.Cu), GND via (12.78,59.02) to the Q124.5 drain 0.180 mm (F.Cu). The native DRC on a copy of the board with its own rules gives 0 errors; all of these pass the 0.15 mm floor.
- **Circuit:** Calc/Inference: with RAW_ACT bridged to OUT, cold and powered CONT/BUS readings equal a healthy match because the 2-4 ohm difference is invisible to the 10.2 k / 1 k banks. At the fire command the pack drives BAT_IN -> Q120/Q121 -> bridge -> about 14.8 mm of 0.2 mm OUT sense track (about 36 mohm at 1 oz) -> via (11.63,55.1) -> Q124. That is about 28 A with Rpack 0.15 ohm, so either BAT_IN collapses (U9 off at 4.45-4.61 V; C84 hold-up about 0.5-2.6 ms) or the track fuses; CH1 does not deploy. With IGN bridged to OUT, R134 limits the current to about 3.7 A, the match is bypassed and fails to fire silently. With OUT bridged to BUZZ_G and the bus enabled, about 8.4 V reaches PC14 through R162 1 k, above the FT limit of VDD + 4 V (no fire).
- **Impact:** These are undetectable single surface defects that defeat R134's stated purpose ('a shorted igniter must not brown out the logic') or silently lose CH1. They belong to the owner-accepted default-clearance class. The current deviation section 1 records only OUT-to-GND or failed-short Q124 and 'a short match resembles healthy', and cites an inventory 'scoped to its original copper', not this board's.
- **Fix:** Reroute the OUT sense branch to R173.1 outside the R134.2/Q123.1 corridor so it clears R134.2 by >= 0.5 mm; there is no room for 0.5 mm to both pads inside the 0.625 mm corridor. Move the IGN run or R131 to >= 0.5 mm. Alternatively add both consequences, with these current-board gaps, to deviation section 1.

### R18-SAF-05 (Minor, new): Stale arming-switch notes on the battery-entry schematic sheet (the single source of truth): 'J1.3--4' switch contact and R166 0.33 ohm

- **Where:** 'J1.3--4: external dry contact. Closed = battery enabled; open/disconnected = OFF.'; 'R80 (BAT_IN side) and R165 (EN side) bracket the contact: a chafed SW lead or a battery lead in J1.3/J1.4 puts <= 3.3 V ...'; 'C89 + C95 ... + R166 (0.33 ohm 2512)'
- **Measured:** Netlist: J1 has only J1.1 BAT_IN and J1.2 GND. The switch is J400.1 BAT_SW_SOURCE (R80 470 k to BAT_IN) and J400.2 BAT_SW_RETURN (R165 470 k to EN). R166 is '0.36R 1% 2512', 25121WF360LT4E / C168925, in the schematic and the BOM.
- **Impact:** The sheet that defines the pad-arming device names a contact that does not exist. Someone wiring or reviewing from the schematic could look for the switch on the battery terminal. The documented values still hold for J400 (8.4 x 300/770 = 3.27 V), so this is documentation only.
- **Fix:** Text-only schematic edits: 'J1.3--4' -> 'J400.1 / J400.2 (BAT_SW_SOURCE / BAT_SW_RETURN)'; 'J1.3/J1.4' -> 'J400.1/J400.2'; 'R166 (0.33 ohm 2512)' -> 'R166 (0.36 ohm 2512)'. Regenerate netlist/sheet exports; no copper change.

### R18-SAF-06 (Minor, new): Arming procedure keeps the bus enabled from on-rail arming onward and has no per-flight 'IGN dead with switch open' check, so latent bus faults go unseen and only Q124 protects during the pad wait

- **Where:** PC1: 'firmware keeps low until armed on the pad'. FMEA: enable PC1 for powered on-rail bus checks; nothing says to return it low. ORDERING 8.3: 20 screw-switch cycles verifying RAW_ACT < 1 V is a bench/first-article step. The handoff has no per-integration measurement before the e-match is inserted.
- **Measured:** n/a (records review)
- **Impact:** After the pad checks only Q124 stands between the pack and the match until apogee, including during walk-away. That is the 'one FET' state the single-switch decision said R10 improves on. The latent off-state bus faults (R18-SAF-01/02/03, a shorted Q121 or Q122) are only caught at first article or on the pad after power-up, not during integration with the charge installed.
- **Fix:** (a) Add a per-flight integration step: pack connected, J400 open, measure J121.2 (IGN) to GND < 0.1 V before inserting the e-match (the terminal is reachable; TP120 faces the sled). (b) Firmware policy: after the on-rail powered bus check, return PC1 low and re-enable it only on confirmed launch detection, optionally after a walk-away delay, keeping both series FETs off while people are near the vehicle. Q120/Q121 turn on within a few ms (R121 x C120 = 10 ms time constant), well before apogee.

## Rejected or closed candidates

- Pyro bus fed from BAT_IN rather than RAW_PROTECTED violates AGENTS 2.2 ('after the load switch (RAW), never straight from the battery'): Rejected: RAW_ACT is fed only through Q120/Q121 (netlist: RAW_ACT = Q121 D, R132.1, R134.2, TP120), matching D-SS-02 and F7. The historical parenthetical 'RAW' (frozen design: the main load-switch output) is ambiguous, but R10's reading is explicit and owner-recorded.
- Switch-open board can still be powered and fire via USB / self-powered port back-feed: Known and owner-accepted (R10_DECISIONS single-switch 'Accepted'; deviation section 1). Verified the paths: PORT_4V8 back-feed conducts through U401 (OUT abs max VIN + 0.3 V, TPS7A91 Rev A p.4) and U400 into RAW_PROTECTED -> U11 -> VLOGIC. The camera is isolated (TPS2553 IREV <= 1 uA, Rev G p.7; TXU0202 Ioff). UART lines enter FT_h/FT_u MCU pins with +0 mA positive injection (DS12110 p.107), so they cannot power the MCU. Not new.
- Q124 gate network single faults (R126/R127/R128/C126 open or short) cause fire: Rejected: every case leaves the gate pulled down (R127 + R126 = 1.22 k and R128 10 k are redundant) or makes CH1 unable to fire. With C126 open, drain coupling is about 1 mV at the 10 ms bus ramp.
- Hot-plug or dV/dt turn-on of Q120/Q121 or Q124: Rejected: C120 1 uF against Q121 Crss 365 pF (about 1 nF at low VDS) gives a delta VGS of about -8 mV on a 7.7 V step. The Q122 Miller kick of <= about 0.6-1.1 V lasts about 63 us (R123 x Ciss) and is absorbed by R121 x C120 = 10 ms. Q124's C126 220 nF against Crss 65 pF gives about 2.5 mV for 8.4 V. With the bus on, the match carries C128 charging current <= about 8 mA for about 0.1 ms.
- Gate states in reset, unpowered, brown-out or power-up are undefined: Rejected for nominal hardware: GPIOs are in analog mode after reset (DS12110 Rev 8 p.32); the LQFP100 has PDR active with VBOR0 falling 1.58-1.68 V (p.110); the external pull-downs need no supply. A BOR-level option byte is a recorded firmware item (FMEA F-3).
- Sense-divider single-resistor fault over-stresses PC0 (FT_a) or PC5 (TT_a): Rejected: one top short or one bottom open gives 19.9 V x 2.029/12.081 = 3.342 V < 4.0 V (TT/FT_a analog limit, p.106). Healthy cold CONT/BUS is about 96-99 mV; the cold test current is <= 0.37 mA. Two-resistor faults are outside the declared envelope (disclosed).
- ESD into J121 turns on a pyro FET: Rejected: OUT has C128 100 nF (Murata GCJ soft-termination) at the drain; IGN reaches RAW_ACT through 2 ohm, and Q121/Q120 body diode or avalanche dump into the BAT_IN capacitors while C120 keeps VGS near 0. Gate coupling is mV-level. Pin-to-pin ESD through the match itself is a match property.
- Shorted igniter browns out the logic: Rejected with R134 intact: 4.2 A is drawn from BAT_IN ahead of U9; a 0.15 ohm pack sags about 0.63 V, far above U9's 4.45-4.61 V off band. The only R134-bypass path found is the single surface bridge in R18-SAF-04.
- USB-only bench with e-match connected and pack disconnected can energize the match: Rejected: U11 (TPS2121 always-on RCB, Rev F p.15) and U9 (true reverse blocking) keep RAW_PROTECTED and BAT_IN at 0 V. The only source is the 3V3 -> D120 -> 10 k cold pull-up, at 0.3 mA.
- Miswiring battery, switch and e-match among the identical J1/J400/J121 terminals fires the match: Rejected: e-match on J400 carries <= 7 uA (it closes the switch loop); pack on J121 drives only the 11.2 k dividers through Q124's body diode; e-match on J1 sees no source because Q120's body diode blocks. These cause function loss or an unintended power-on, not ignition.
- Q122 lacks a gate-source capacitor, a literal AGENTS 2.4 Major: Narrowed to a Minor scope question (R18-SAF-03). S-3 and the decisions treat the P-FET pair as the pyro gate (C120, R120, R121 present); C120 with R121 filters Q122 transients.
- S-8 fails on the protected barrels: Rejected nominally: 1.1025 mm J121.1 hole to In2 3V3 (also 1.900, 2.698, 2.100 mm) reproduced. Fabricated drill/registration allocation remains disclosed as pending.
- Netlist/board mismatch or safety parts missing from BOM/CPL: Rejected: 0 pad-net differences over 660 pads; 37 safety/arming parts present in m3_bom_jlc.csv and m3_cpl_jlc.csv with matching LCSC numbers.
- Hot drain leakage of Q122 into the 100 k R120 turns the pair on: Not demonstrated: at the datasheet's 55 C limit, VGS <= 0.5 V against 1.3 V. Higher-temperature leakage is unspecified (Inference); ORDERING 8.3 keeps a 70 C high-side turn-on bench gate.

## Limits

- No firmware exists. Every ground-screen and arming-policy statement is about the specified procedure, not executed code.
- No bench, physical, thermal, transient or ESD testing. Leakage and CAF behaviour and fabrication drill/registration tolerances are inferences.
- No SPICE/transient simulation. Miller and floating-gate behaviour is first-order capacitive division with datasheet 15 V capacitances; low-VDS capacitances are larger (Inference).
- The ST datasheet used is the cached Rev 8, not the Rev 11 earlier rounds cited. The facts used (GPIO reset state, FT/TT limits, BOR) were cross-checked only within Rev 8.
- Whether the fabricator performs a bare-board open/short test, and its isolation threshold, was not verified.
- Single faults were considered for the fire path, gate networks, sense dividers and copper adjacency within 0.5 mm. Double faults were only spot-checked.
- Earlier rounds' reports and the R8/R9 validation records were read as context only; no other round18 report or probe directory was opened.
