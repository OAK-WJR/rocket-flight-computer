# R9 clean-sheet plan

## 1. Decision

Build **B-spine at 46 × 98 mm**:
- **Aft:** RF strip.
- **Middle:** U1 (rot 0, ~5 mm west of axis) and signals; J122 turned on the axis.
- **West edge:** the CH1 pyro column as a dead end. J124 (ARM) sits forward of J121 (IGN), both wires enter from −x, and R134 bridges their facing pins with two 8 mm bars. Q124 and the gate network sit aft, toward U1.3 and U1.15.
- **East column:** all power. J1.1 → face-to-face FETs → a 43 mm RAW_ACT spine on F.Cu, B.Cu and In2 → C121, J124.2 and J122's RAW row.

**Why:** safety holds by geometry, not routing discipline; all three judges ranked it first. Prototype vs R8: foreign nets near/through armed copper 0 (R8 6/7/6); battery-live to armed 7.6 mm (1.089); rule H margin 0.225 (0.025); bare gate to armed pad 2.47 mm (1.03); RAW_ACT 0.58 mΩ / 13.6 °C (1.45 / 17.6); f1 250–264 Hz (209); no MCU pin moves.

**Grafts:**
- **LC-1:** no local clearance override on pyro, RAW, gate, sense, BAT_IN or ACT_* pads (R8's 0.3 mm overrides silently disable rules A/E/A2; they also corrupted flowline's prototype). Pyro margins are measured geometrically, not via DRC.
- **From flowline:**
  - RF chains and modules congruent with R7, regenerated by template in each jack's and module's frame;
  - the bridge table, the seeded rule-H test and the reset-voltage table as gates;
  - a GND guard track beside GATE_N;
  - a GND partner via for TP122 (removes A8);
  - MCU-end interface resistors;
  - its divider arrangement, to lift rule C from 0.077 mm;
  - six track widths and no pour trims.
- **From compact:**
  - the RF zone inside y_f = 22.0;
  - the limiter on B.Cu under U30 with the shunts east of the GNSS line (removes A2/A3 with no F10 edit);
  - strict pad rules in the legaliser and the gate: H + 0.2 mm; gate–sense ≥ 1.2 mm; ACT gate–GND ≥ 1.2 mm; bare gate ≥ 1.0 mm from always-live;
  - the F9 input-protection distance as a rule;
  - a tab-window check, and kill criteria.

**Fallback:** flowline A2 at 96 mm. Compact (86 mm) rejected: near-zero margins.

## 4. Steps

Work in scratch until the owner approves; keep at most 3 boards.

1. **Gate first.**
   - Build: `check_r9.py` (P-1…P-11, R-1…R-13, M-2…M-7, LC-1), `test_r9_check.py`, `pyro_probe.py`, `bridge_table.py`, `rule_h_seed.py`, `gcpw_solve.py`, `tab_windows.py`.
   - Verify: every rule fails on a seeded defect, including an armed-pad override. R8 fails exactly P-3, P-7 and LC-1. The solver hits pass 2's 50.3 Ω within 1 Ω.
2. **Schematic and footprints.**
   - Build: `r9_sch.py`, the variants, overrides removed, test-pad clearance moved into the DRU.
   - Verify: ERC and parity 0; the netlist diff is footprint fields only; still 52/52 extended codes.
3. **Placement freeze.**
   - Build: `r9_legalise.py`, `r9_place.tsv`, with the aft cluster (U1/U3/U4/U5/R10) first.
   - Verify: 0 violations across 249 parts; fit; plate model; F9; F10 5 mm; strict pad rules; J1 ≤ 25 mm; tabs; MST ≤ 1639.
   - Kill: if 98 fails, go to 100.
4. **RF.**
   - Build: `r9_rf.py` templates.
   - Verify: R-1…R-13; launch congruent with R7 in each jack's frame; GCPW within tolerance at every sample; no A2/A3.
5. **Pyro.**
   - Build: `r9_pyro.py`, pad-referenced.
   - Verify: probe margins; no fires-class bridge under 1.5 mm; rule H live; every GATE_N neighbour GND or ≤ 0.2 V in reset; coating; wire room.
6. **Power.**
   - Build: `r9_power.py`: four In2 regions, the spine, the stitching.
   - Verify: run_dc at 0.1 mm; D18; D85; barrel current ≤ 1 A; hot-plug feed.
7. **Routing.**
   - Build: family modules rf_if, qspi_hse, pyro, servo, usb_swd, imu_baro, camera, status, power_ctl.
   - Verify: per-family budgets; empty allowlist; P-7; D19.
8. **Ground.**
   - Build: `r9_ground.py`.
   - Verify: stitching ≤ 2.5 mm; P7/P9/P10; R6b.
9. **Post and fab.**
   - Verify: two builds give the same SHA; full gate; survey with the 0.05 mm DC.
10. **Records.**
    - Build: requalification evidence bound to the board SHA; M3_R9, R9_VALIDATION, REQUIREMENTS, ORDERING, VEHICLE.
    - Verify: every claim cites a file.

## 5. Review loop

Parallel reviewers each pass: pyro safety; RF; power/thermal; mechanical; routing beauty (dedicated); gate integrity (no weakened rules, live seeds, determinism); requirements traceability; manufacturability.

Fixes edit tables and scripts only, then rebuild from scratch.

**Stop when** the gate passes, every done row is met (or the miss owner-accepted), and two consecutive passes find no Critical or Major.

Record Minors (target ≤ 5) without rework, then offer one more check. R8 stays orderable until then.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Aft cluster does not legalise | Spike first; step to 100 or 102 |
| Via count | Owner budget; per-family budgets; via-free servo and pyro lanes |
| DRC blinded by overrides | LC-1; geometric probe; seeded test |
| ARM1_SENSE crossing the spine | Single recorded crossing over solid In1 GND; bridge-table rows |
| RF requalification | R7-congruent templates; validated solver |
| F9 hot-plug (U9 at 14 mm) | Legaliser rule, or a hotplug_feed re-proof |
| ACT gate pair at 0.487 mm | Z5b ≥ 1.0 rule; co-orient R120 and C120 |
| Renumbered footprints | Pad-role check; legends; harness and FMEA text |
| Mid-support hole cuts the spine | DC solve with the hole, else the west spot |

**Effort:** about 20–25 sessions, including 3–4 review passes.
