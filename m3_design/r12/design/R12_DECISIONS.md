# R12 decisions (D-R12-xx)

Stage A, 2026-11-04. Each deletion or replacement names the requirement it still meets, the AVA practice behind it
(every AVA statement is an Inference: no AVA schematic or BOM is public, `cost_study.md` §4) and its live price.
Labels (AGENTS §2.5): **Datasheet** (maker document, cited), **Live** (JLC component API via `tools/jlc_stock.lookup`,
`jlc_r12_stock.json`, 2026-11-04T02:42:47Z unless stated), **Calculation** (arithmetic on Datasheet / Live values,
`r12_windows.json` from `m3_design/r12_budget.py`), **Inference** (any unmeasured or unverified input). Nothing here is
measured. No AGENTS §2 rule changes (§2 check at the end of §1).

## 1. Decisions

**D-R12-01 Architecture.** R11's circuit at `0b26da5` (D-R11-85 … -88 included), pruned and re-sourced; every part on
the top face (JLC Economic PCBA: one side, 255 ± 5 °C reflow, single PCB ≥ 10 × 10 mm, BGA ≥ 0.5 mm pitch, JLC
capabilities page, Live per `cost_study.md` §1.1); four layers, 1.6 mm, 0.5 oz inner, ENIG; 46 × ≤ 100 mm. The board is
laid out new: R11's escape, placement and routing are not reused. Basis: `cost_study.md` §0 (R11 $376.55 / $531.81 for
2 / 5 boards; one-sided Economic about half). AVA puts parts on both faces because it is hand-built; at JLC that forces
Standard two-sided assembly (+$33.77 per order and no Economic tier), so one face is the deliberate departure from AVA.
REQUIREMENTS M1 (Standard PCBA, 1 oz inner) becomes an owner acceptance item (§3); R11 already deviated on inner copper.

**D-R12-02 MCU package: TFBGA-100 kept (STM32H743VIH6, C964146, $8.8446, 4221 / 3913, Live).** The LQFP-100 fallback
(STM32H743VIT6 C114409, $9.42–10.70, cost_study §2.2) is not needed. Escape count per ball ring on R11's ball map with
the D-R12-12 changes (ring 1 = outer; Calculation from `pin_table.tsv`):

| Ring | Balls | Signal | Supply (GND / 3V3 / 3V3A / VCAP) | NC | Escape |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 36 | 23 | 4 | 9 | straight out on F.Cu |
| 2 | 28 | 16 | 3 | 9 | F.Cu between ring-1 balls: 9 gaps per side, 36 in all; at most 8 ring-2 balls per side |
| 3 | 20 | 8 | 1 (VCAP1) | 11 | dog-bone via, then B.Cu |
| 4 | 12 | 2 (QSPI_CLK G5, BOOT0 D5) | 4 | 6 | dog-bone via, then B.Cu |
| 5 | 4 | 0 | 4 | 0 | vias to In1 GND / In2 3V3 |

Geometry (Calculation, ST DS12110 Rev 8 Table 219 land: 0.40 mm pad at 0.8 mm pitch): gap between ring-1 pads
0.80 − 0.40 = 0.40 mm ≥ 0.10 mm track + 2 × 0.10 mm clearance = 0.30 mm (JLC multilayer 0.09 mm); a 0.45 / 0.30 mm via
at an interstitial site is 0.566 mm from four ball centres: 0.566 − 0.200 − 0.225 = 0.141 mm ≥ 0.10 mm; two
interstitial vias leave 0.80 − 0.45 = 0.35 mm, one 0.10 mm track. The ten inner signal balls need ten dog-bone vias and
leave the array on B.Cu, which carries no parts on a one-sided board: 9 channels per side between via columns, 36 ≥ 10.
In2 stays a plane. LQFP-100 would cost +$0.58 (5 b) / +$1.86 (2 b) per board and ≈ 190 mm² (cost_study §2.2). Stage C
implements the escape; if it fails there, the LQFP decision is reopened with this table.

**D-R12-03 Board rules.** Through vias 0.3 mm drill / ≥ 0.45 mm pad only (no small-hole surcharge; 0.25 mm adds $16.88,
cost_study §2.1, Live), no via in a soldered pad (no VIPPO: U401 and its two GAP-3 vias are deleted, D-R12-07), track
and clearance 0.10 mm (JLC 0.09 mm). `m3.kicad_dru` (R10 / R11 rules kept by name plus the R12 floors) and the project
net classes (Default 0.10 / 0.10 mm, 0.45 / 0.30 mm via; Power 0.30 mm) are generated by `r12_sch.py`.

**D-R12-04 USB path → diode-OR.** Deleted U10 (TPS2553 USB limit), U11 (TPS2121 mux), D4 (USBLC6), R85–R88, R90, R91,
C85, C86, C98, TP82 (nets USB_LIMITED, USB_ILIM, LOGIC_ILIM, LOGIC_SS, USB_OVLO, USB_FAULT_N, LOGIC_SOURCE_ST). Added D5
(RAW_PROTECTED → VLOGIC) and D6 (VBUS → VLOGIC), both SS34 (C8678 Basic $0.0351, 40 V 3 A, VF 0.55 V at 3 A, Live
listing). Function kept: bench power and log download over USB-C (F11), battery priority (the higher source wins:
VLOGIC ≥ 5.2 V on the pack against ≤ 5.0 V on USB, Calculation / Inference), no back-feed into the host (D6) and C84
hold-up isolated from the port and camera bucks (D5; better than R11, where C84 back-fed them through U11). USB-only
VLOGIC is 4.30–5.00 V from a USB 2.0 host port (4.75–5.25 V, VF 0.25–0.45 V Inference), above the LMR50410's 4.0 V floor
(D-R12-06); a bus-powered hub's 4.40 V low-power port gives 3.95 V, below it (bench rule O-R12-03). Lost margins: USB
inrush / fault current limit toward the host, USB over-voltage lockout, the USB ESD clamp (STM32's own pin ESD only,
Inference), the USB_FAULT_N / LOGIC_SOURCE_ST flags. AVA: three bare micro-USB ports (Inference). Saves about $1.7 of
parts per board and the TPS2121, USBLC6 and 0.1 % codes (cost_study §3 #17, #49–51, #69–70, Live).

**D-R12-05 PA4 port sense.** LOGIC_SOURCE_ST (U11 ST) is replaced by PORT_SENSE = PORT_4V8 × R112 10k / (R111 4.7k +
R112 10k) on PA4 (both Basic): 3.08–3.40 V with PORT_4V8 on (initial), 3.04–3.45 V (drift), above VIH 2.40 V and below
VDD + 0.3 V = 3.54 V (Calculation, `r12_windows.json` sense). R10's firmware rule "enable the port UARTs only while PA4
reads low" becomes "only while PA4 reads high" (the ports are powered exactly when PORT_4V8 is up). F10 / F12 functions
kept; the sense is the rail itself instead of the mux's selection.

**D-R12-06 3V3 buck: LMR50410XDBVR (C2841056, Extended $0.3335, 26557 / 26280, Live).** R11's AP63203 (C780769) has
−2896 orderable (Live). TPS563201 / TPS563208 cannot run the 3V3 on USB through D6 (VIN 4.5 V minimum, wake-up up to
4.3 V, TI SLVSD90B §5.3 / §5.5, Datasheet). LMR50410: VIN 4–36 V, UVLO rising 3.55–4.0 V, VFB 0.99–1.01 V, 1 A, EN tied
to VIN allowed (TI SLUSDW3A §6, §7.3, §7.5, Datasheet); SOT-23-6 on R11's land (JLCEDA land of C2841056 0.532 × 1.072 mm
at ±1.149 mm, checked in `r12_parts.geometry_checks`). Feedback R14 47k / R15 20k (Basic): 3V3 = 3.270–3.431 V initial,
3.240–3.464 V drift, inside 3.0–3.6 V (VDD33USB, flash, card, sensors; Calculation). Inductor: SWPA4030 4.7 µH, the code
the two TPS563208 bucks use, instead of TI Table 9-1's 10 µH: ripple ratio K = 0.61 at 8.4 V in, at the top of TI's
20–60 % range (SLUSDW3A eq. 9, Calculation); 0.77 only in the wrong-3S band; loop behaviour with 4.7 µH is an Inference
(bench load step, O-R12-06). Output C8 22 µF (Table 9-1: 22 µF) plus C23, C461 and the decoupling. Input C6 22 µF + C99.

**D-R12-07 Port rail: U400 TPS563208DDCR (C468250, Extended $0.1457, 17432 / 17048, Live) makes PORT_4V8 directly.**
Deleted U401 (TPS7A91 LDO) with its two GAP-3 VIPPO vias, R402, R403, R405, C408, the PORT_5V3 bulk C403–C405, C430, the
second input cap C401 and the per-port 22 µF C411 / C421 / C445 (100 nF per port kept). L400 SRP5030 6.8 µH (C1329837
$1.44) → SWPA4030 4.7 µH (TI Table 7-2: 3.3–4.7 µH at 5 V); output C406 + C407 22 µF (Table 7-2: 20–68 µF; the per-port
22 µF would have taken PORT_4V8 above 68 µF nominal). Feedback R400 51k + R404 1k / R401 10k (Basic; the 0.1 % parts
deleted). TPS563208 (FCCM), not the study's TPS563201 (Eco-mode): SLVSD90B gives VFB 0.749–0.787 V over TJ −40…125 °C
for continuous mode only, and Eco-mode reads 774 mV typical at light load, which could take a lightly loaded port rail
past 5.0 V (Datasheet / Inference); the FCCM part costs $0.08 more. Window (Calculation): **4.561–4.967 V** initial
(module limits 4.5–5.0 V: MicoAir LR900 4.5–5 V, HGLRC M100 Mini 3.3–5 V, project records), **4.511–5.024 V** with an
independent 100 ppm/K drift bound: 24 mV over 5.0 V at the corner (Inference: two thick-film parts of one series track
far closer than independent drift; O-R12-05). AUX 100 mA allowance: no longer limited by U401's thermal floor (GAP-3);
the buck is rated 3 A. Fault containment: cycle-by-cycle valley limit 3.3–5.1 A and UVP hiccup (65 %, 10 ms restart,
Datasheet) instead of the LDO's 1.5–1.9 A limit. AVA: a linear port regulator (Inference); R12 keeps one switcher to
avoid the LDO's code, VIPPO and $0.81.
