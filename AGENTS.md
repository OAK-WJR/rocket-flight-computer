## 2. Safety constraints that must never be violated

1. The external power switch is the pad arming device: the board is switched on only with the vehicle on the pad / rail, and the pyro channel is live whenever the board is on. Firmware never fires before it has detected launch (accelerometer + barometer), and on the ground continuity / bus readings are hard refusals (§2.3).
2. The power switch is turned on last and off first; the pyro bus is taken after the load switch (RAW), never straight from the battery.
3. In flight, continuity / ARM_SENSE readings are only logged and annunciated and **never veto a fire**; on the ground they are hard refusals (`docs/FMEA_REDUNDANCY.md` F-10).
4. Pyro FET gates must have a pull-down, a series resistor and a gate-source capacitor, and must be defined off while the MCU is in reset or unpowered.
5. An inference that has not been measured or checked against the original datasheet must be labelled as an inference, never written as fact.

## 3. Development rules

- **The KiCad project is the single source of truth**: edit the schematic and PCB directly in `m3_design/assembly_review/`, keeping them in sync ("Update PCB from Schematic").
- **Verify, don't guess**: package dimensions, lands, pinouts and electrical parameters always come from the maker's datasheet or the LCSC / JLCPCB page, with the source URL written into the review notes.
- New footprints go in `kilib/rocket.pretty`, 3D models in `kilib/rocket.3dshapes`, referenced as `${KIPRJMOD}/kilib/...`; **absolute paths are forbidden**.
- Every part's `LCSC` / `Manufacturer` / `Manufacturer Part` fields must agree, with no second, contradictory part-number field (`quality_audit` rejects it).
- Routing: hand-route critical nets (power, pyro, crystal, USB, SPI/QSPI); use only 0° / 45° / 90° straight segments with no staircases, acute angles or stubs; put a GND via within 1.5 mm (centre to centre) of every layer change of a critical signal net (pyro and arming, crystal, USB data and SPI/QSPI/IMU chip select; on R7–R9 also RF and the RADIO_* / GNSS_* nets, which R10 does not have); size high-current copper per IPC-2152. **R10 AUD-2:** the named filtered-sense / ACT / port-control list in `tools/route_check.py` is excluded only from the 1.5 mm partner-via and one-In2-region/reference rules; fast interfaces, S-8 and critical hygiene remain blocking. **R10 AUD-10:** exact collinear nodes, duplicate segments, inside-pad segments and the 6 mm legend-distance rule are counted for review; partial overlaps, crossings, connectivity, DRC, correct/required legends and mask/text clearances still block. **GAP-3:** only the two explicitly gated U401.11 GND thermal vias may enter a soldered SMD pad, and only with resin-filled, copper-capped planar VIPPO manufacture; ordinary via rules elsewhere remain. The owner's full clean-routing list (no needless detours, extra vias or layer hops, tiny jogs, duplicate or overlap-only copper, untidy pad entries or neck-downs, over the whole board, inherited copper included) is REQUIREMENTS M2. `tools/route_check.py` (run by `tools/check.sh`) gates the part of it that M2 names (on nets with a pour this includes chain detours and layer hops in series; on every critical net, removable local bumps and vias joined to a track only by overlap); every exception is an allowlist entry in that tool with a measured reason, noncritical leftovers are counted and located once in the final review report without per-instance measured metadata or stale-coordinate failures (requirements audit 2); actual routing rules and critical-exception validation remain blocking. The rest is checked in review. All of them stay deviations until the owner accepts that list (M2).
- Silkscreen: reference designators legible (≥ 1.0 mm text, REQUIREMENTS M2), never over pads or off the board edge; on silkscreen only designators, the terminal, arming and connector legends (REQUIREMENTS F8; also UART, SERVO and battery), the revision legend and JLC's order-number placeholder; a legend about a part sits within 6 mm of that part's courtyard (`tools/check_r7.py` `legend_subjects`, which `tools/check_r8.py` and `tools/check_r9.py` (K-legends) run); part numbers hidden on F.Fab. Crowded designators go on the bottom side under their part, never next to another part; one with no spot nearer its own part than any other (clear of pads and vias) on either side is hidden, kept on F.Fab and listed in the current revision's record (`docs/M3_R9.md` §"Silkscreen"; R8: `docs/M3_R8.md`).
- **`tools/check.sh` must pass at zero before every commit**; put a concise summary and evidence link in the pull-request description (requirements audit 2; the supervisor still runs the full gate before every commit).
- Once per reviewed change, update the matching row of `STATUS.md`, stating clearly what was verified and what was not (software tests, DRC, simulation and physical tests must be reported separately).
- A PCB change invalidates the board hash binding in `m3_firmware/build.py`; refresh its reviewed provenance through `m3_firmware/assembly_binding.py`; review electrical pin facts and axes when they change. Cosmetic-only edits still need refreshed hash provenance, not a bypass or a redundant electrical review.

## 4. Workflow and communication

- One branch per piece of work → pull request → reviewed by the other side before merging. Do not push directly to `main`.
- Discussions, questions and decisions go in **GitHub Issues / PR comments** so both sides always get notified; once merged, decisions are also written into the relevant document.
- Large files (datasheet PDFs, build intermediates, toolchains, render history) stay out of the repository; see `.gitignore`. Review notes link to datasheets instead.
- Never commit personal information, accounts, tokens or local absolute paths.
